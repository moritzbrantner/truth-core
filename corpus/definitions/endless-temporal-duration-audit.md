witness plus `Interminable`. The latter negates a pair of admitted comparable
times with differing life-presence. In the existing `successive` frame, Time is

## Alternatives considered before the endpoint decision

Let T be supplied times, `<` the supplied earlier-than, and D(t) the subject's
temporal presence. Require strict linear order laws explicitly: irreflexivity,
transitivity, and comparability for distinct times. These are framework scope,
not a theorem that physical time has those properties. No discreteness,
immediate successor, metric, completeness, or choice of Nat/Int is imposed.
Dense timelines must remain admissible.

Future-unending candidate:

- `exists t, D(t)`;
- `forall t, D(t) -> exists u, t < u and D(u)`;
- convex support: `forall a b c, D(a) -> D(c) -> a < b -> b < c -> D(b)`.

This permits a first moment, also permits a beginningless duration, and forbids
a final present moment. It does not say that every ambient time is present.
Convexity excludes arbitrarily punctured support; its inclusion as part of
"duration" needs explicit confirmation rather than silently strengthening a
legacy reading. A weaker endpoint-only alternative can omit convexity.

Two-sided candidate: retain those clauses and add
`forall t, D(t) -> exists u, u < t and D(u)`.
It implies the future candidate by projection; a future-only Nat support would
distinguish the converse. If both readings are consequentially adopted, register
sibling statements and a certified sole-premise strengthens result, using the
batch generator; do not silently rewrite the legacy sense.

Empty support fails the existential clause, even on an empty timeline. Finite
strictly ordered support cannot satisfy the future clause. A constant state on
an unbounded ordered support must remain possible; this is the control against
smuggling life-state change. None of these proposed controls is claimed checked
here, and neither candidate is yet a Lean/corpus declaration.

existing sense and definition directly. Its type is
`{Time : Type u} -> [Preorder Time] -> (Time -> Prop) -> Prop`, with body
`(exists t, present t) and forall t, present t -> exists u, t < u and present u`.
The supplied preorder makes strict comparison irreflexive and transitive;

parameter or state inequality occurs. Beginninglessness is not needed by any

present moment fails, Nat with presence everywhere succeeds despite having a
first moment, bounded Nat support fails, and constant states coexist with
