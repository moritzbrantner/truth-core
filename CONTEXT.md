# Truth Core Corpus

The corpus records defensible, source-derived philosophical claims and the
formal arguments that may be constructed from them. It keeps scholarly
interpretation distinct from formal verification.

## Language

**Corpus**:
The source records, claims, results, and research candidates maintained by
Truth Core. It is the canonical scholarly record.
_Avoid_: dataset, knowledge base

**Claim**:
One atomic, source-derived reading with a precise locator and a Lean
declaration. Alternative defensible readings are separate claims.
_Avoid_: fact, theorem

**Result**:
A source-grounded argument whose advertised Lean theorem has passed the
project's independent comparator checks.
_Avoid_: claim, proof of historical truth

**Candidate**:
A research item identifying a philosophical proposition or missing premise
that should be investigated before it becomes a claim.
_Avoid_: unverified claim, backlog task

**Corpus Explorer**:
A read-only scholarly interface for inspecting the corpus and its links to
sources and formal verification.
_Avoid_: dashboard, editor
