# Cogito: from occurring thought to a thinking subject

This is a **conditional** Cartesian bridge, not an unconditional proof of
metaphysical ego or of any actual occurrence.

- `ThoughtOccurs Occurs`: some thought episode occurs.
- `EveryThoughtHasSubject Occurs SubjectOf`: every occurring episode has
  a subject. This is a separate open philosophical premise.
- `SomeThinkingSubject Occurs SubjectOf`: some subject bears an occurring
  thought.

The constructive theorem composes the first two propositions to obtain the
third. An explicit countermodel has one occurring episode and an empty
subject relation; it refutes the shortcut from occurrence alone. Another
model satisfies the universal bearer principle vacuously while no thought
occurs, refuting the shortcut from the bearer principle alone.

The subject may be momentary. Nothing here entails an enduring person,
substance, immaterial soul, temporal change, reliable external perception,
or physical reality. These are distinct obligations. The existence of a
thought in the actual world must be independently witnessed, rather than
introduced through a Lean `Unit` constructor.

The schema is source-free and stipulative; no proposition is attributed to
Descartes without reading a pinned primary witness. Related work: #509,
#515 and PR #516. No Catholicism root premise is discharged.
