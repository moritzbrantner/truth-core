# The Creed proposal premise

`STMT-CATHOLIC-CHURCH-DEFINITIVELY-PROPOSES-CREED` is the existing Catholicism skeleton’s Creed-proposal input. Its meaning remains institutional proposal of the twelve existing Greek article contents. The new records connect two documentary readings that jointly support this institutional-status interpretation. They do not establish the articles’ truth, divine teaching authority or the historical identity of Christ’s Church.

The materialized [Holy See collection](https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_con_cfaith_doc_1998_professio-fidei_en.html) supplies two needed distinctions. Its CDF commentary identifies the Nicene-Constantinopolitan Creed in §4, classifies Creed articles in §11, and explains their revealed/irreformable proposal category in §§5 and 9. Sections 6 and 10 distinguish other definitive teachings and non-definitive teachings. The solemn modes include the Pontiff speaking ex cathedra and the College of Bishops gathered in council; the ordinary and universal mode involves the bishops dispersed throughout the world in communion with the Pope. Thus an individual's profession or an arbitrary ecclesiastical assertion is insufficient evidence for this premise.

The consulted edition is the hosted English collection, with translation basis recorded; its wording is not represented as the original 1989 formula. The collection was pinned twice with matching byte/text hashes and read in full.

## Greek content and Catholic formula

The root retains `CREED-C381` and its twelve-clause modeling division. The hosted Catholic recitation includes additional wording. The comparison below locates the existing Greek contents. Official recognition of the Greek Symbol supplies its scope; no Latin/Greek theological-equivalence premise is required. The table is not a machine-checked translation or an identification of different formulas.

The procession clause is not justified by projecting Latin *processio* into Greek *ekporeusis*. A separate [official 2025 joint resource](https://www.christianunity.va/content/dam/unitacristiani/Settimana%20di%20preghiera%20per%20unit%C3%A0/2025/2025%20EN%20%20WPCU.pdf#page=11), pp. 9–11, quotes Catholic recognition of the Greek 381 Symbol and attributes it to the 1995 clarification. The consulted witness is this later quotation. The complete original scan is image-only, and the attempted text republications were not byte-stable; neither is silently substituted for a materialized original. The selected PDF text agrees under Poppler 24.02 and 26.01.

`DISAMB-CREED-CONTENT-AND-FORMULA` makes this intertextual reading explicit. It does not settle Filioque or claim that the procession terms are interchangeable. The CDF classification supplies proposal-status evidence; the Greek reception quotation identifies the Greek Symbol. This is a documented intertextual synthesis, not an added philosophical correspondence premise. Neither passage is made to state the synthesis alone.

## Verification boundary

The two source readings have independent statement identities: Catholic-profession proposal category and Greek-Symbol reception. Two `supports` relations connect them to the existing root premise, with precise limitations. No result is invented merely to transcribe the claims.

The proposal premise changes from bare to sourced through these support relations, but is still open. The formal open statement IDs are unchanged. The First-Way route selector, Creed propositions, authority truth bridge and stage 6 identity remain unchanged.
