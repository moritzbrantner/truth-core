# Regularity, intervention and cosmological scope (#270)

The verified results refute five **specified sufficiency inferences**, under
explicit binary intervention semantics. They do not refute Hume's epistemology,
Lewis's full-mosaic supervenience or the claim that spacetime is brute. A scoped
brute account is consistent in the finite model but leaves a substantive
explanatory challenge. No Catholic or Thomist premise enters these tests.

## Evidence and readings

  true system, with simplicity/strength and, in the probabilistic version, fit.

## Competing commitments and shared answers

These are selected accounts over the **same six questions**, not complete
worldviews. Denying an account's answer does not discharge the need for a
replacement. `U` means unexplained in the recorded fragment. `S` means a sourced
proposal whose adequacy remains open; `V` means a verified model or conditional
consequence, not truth about our world; `R` means a particular stated inference
is refuted under the advertised semantics.

The formal bundles in `TruthCore/Comparison/HumeRegularity.lean` are:

1. **Bare observational fragment:** nontrivial X–Y equality in both backgrounds.
   The additional **naive sufficiency package** says that these observations
   determine efficacy, direction, unobserved equality and future continuations.
   These claims are deliberately stronger than Hume's first definition (which
   includes succession), and are not attributed to Hume or Lewis.
2. **Intervention-enriched fragment:** the same observations plus a response
   table under surgical settings, with X affecting Y and no reverse effect.
   This is a substantive rival causal account, not a reduction of all causal
   notions to observations. Woodward's intervention conditions already employ
   other causal facts; the binary model stipulates the valid setting operation.
3. **Scoped brute fragment:** ordinary processes have temporal causes, while
   spacetime as a totality has no explanatory ground. This is a hypothetical
   separate interlocutor, not an attribution to any of the sourced authors.

Lewis's best-system account is a fourth, stronger **regularity** proposal:
`BestSystemReading` identifies lawhood with selected system theorems. It is not
identified with the intervention-enriched bundle. The selection standard and
a causal/counterfactual interpretation still require an account. Hume's
historical account is a fifth, epistemological/definitional comparison, kept
outside all three analytical bundles.

## Stress tests and strongest replies

`HUME-REGULARITY-TESTS`, theorem
`TruthCore.Results.Modern.Hume.Regularity.checks`, checks all these components:

- **ConfoundingTest.** U→X→Y and U→X, U→Y both give X=Y=U, with both binary
  values witnessed. Setting X changes Y only in the chain. This refutes the
  conjunction-to-efficacy rule when efficacy means change under valid settings.
  **Reply:** a Humean need not identify cause with this two-variable projection.
  The complete mosaic contains other patterns and experimental histories.
  This reply survives: the theorem never supplies two identical whole mosaics.
- **DirectionTest and TemporalTest.** U→X→Y and U→Y→X have identical observations
  and opposed efficacy. Equality itself survives reversal; the ordered fact
  1<2 does not survive swapping its arguments. Hume's actual succession clause
  therefore avoids the crude symmetry objection. A common cause can still
  precede X and Y, with X earlier than Y and no X efficacy. **Reply:** add
  temporal/boundary structure or global counterfactual structure. This is a
  coherent enrichment; which structure obtains and explains the arrow remains
  **underdetermined**, not inconsistent. No theorem here derives an arrow from
  time-reversal-invariant fundamental laws alone.
- **CoincidenceTest and BestSystemTest.** Independent variables restricted to
  the equal-valued sample appear perfectly paired, but the pair (false,true)
  breaks the pattern outside it. This is separate from the fork's shared U.
  **Reply:** Lewis does not elevate every coincidence to lawhood: a selected
  global system filters truths. The finite candidate-language test explicitly
  admits both a true selected pattern and a true unselected incidental fact.
  It demonstrates consistency of this distinction, not an optimization of
  Lewis's actual simplicity/strength/fit tradeoff. Robust winner selection,
  natural vocabulary and explanatory force remain **challenged**.
- **InterventionTest.** Chain and fork share observations but differ under
  setting X with U fixed. Conversely, two models with identical entire X-setting
  tables agree on X efficacy, by substitution. **Reply:** enriched regularity
  or structural approaches can include those facts; interventions are not
  metaphysical magic outside a Humean world. The test accepts that reply.
  Observational identification is refuted, not full-mosaic supervenience.
- **PredictionTest.** For any cutoff, one sequence is always false and another
  first differs exactly at that cutoff. A separately granted universal law
  entails its own instance. **Reply:** laws and successful science support
  defeasible prediction; Lewis's proposal concerns what makes a law, rather
  than a deductive finite-data guarantee. The guarantee is **refuted**; defeasible
  warrant is **underdetermined** by this test, and no chance theory is rejected.
- **BoundaryTest.** Two models satisfy the same local temporal causal demand.
  One has an explanatory ground for spacetime and one does not. The grounded
  one also lacks an earlier temporal cause for spacetime. Universal grounding
  entails a ground for the totality, so it conflicts with the brute model
  **conditionally**. Neither local demand nor the absence of an earlier event
  chooses between the models. No PSR is assumed.

`AnswerMapTest` verifies all five naive answers fail and all five enriched
ordinary diagnostic answers hold; the enriched cosmic slot stays unexplained.
`HUME-REGULARITY-MODELS`, theorem `...Regularity.models`, independently supplies
models for the bare, enriched and scoped-brute bundles. Consistency is limited
to the encoded finite fragments. Neither result is a transcription: the
response functions, sample pairs, sequence construction and ground relations
are reduced and checked, with no opaque bridge carrying a conclusion.

## Brute exception: principled distinction and remaining attack

The strongest scope reply is that interventions and earlier causes apply to
systems embedded in a larger temporal environment. Spacetime as a whole is not
another such system. Woodward §4.6 specifically declines to infer that all
possible global causal claims are meaningless; it requires further work to
make the counterfactuals determinate. Hartle–Hawking give an actual physical
proposal with a different boundary structure, not a license for arbitrary
uncaused appearances in ordinary laboratories. The model preserves ordinary
causal regularities while allowing that distinction. It therefore defeats the
caricature that admitting a brute totality entails anything appearing anywhere.

The remaining exception-selection challenge is precise: **why does exclusion
from this temporal/intervention criterion warrant exclusion from all grounding
or explanation?** A brute interlocutor may reject universal grounding as an
additional metaphysical norm, while retaining ordinary causal explanations.
That is consistent in `brute`. To refute it one must independently defend an
explanatory requirement covering the totality, or find a contradiction in a
stronger stated bundle. Neither the mere word *brute* nor ordinary success of
science supplies that premise. Conversely, an explanatory requirement cannot
be rejected merely by showing that a previous time is unavailable. The paired
model `grounded` makes that illicit inference visible. Both commitments remain
live alternatives; no beginning of actual time or quantum cosmology is proved.

## Verification and fidelity sweep

The source definitions preserve Hume's succession and separate mental clause;
the counterfactual wording is not modernized silently. Lewis selection is
explicitly incomplete, Woodward's intervention validity is stipulated in models,
and Hartle–Hawking are not assigned a brute-fact thesis. The typed targets keep
an earlier temporal cause distinct from an explanatory ground. Load-bearing
terms link the nine `DEF-REGULARITY-*` records and the existing answer-map
record, with their causal-sense disambiguation. Each result is premise-free
only because it proves conditional laws and finite models; it does not
establish any historical or empirical premise.
