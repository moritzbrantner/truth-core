## Distinct Kantian readings

**Cognitive scope.** Categories furnish objective, determining synthetic cognition
only in relation to possible sensible experience. This includes a priori
conditions of experience; it is stronger than Humean observation of repeated
sequences. An inference to something outside experience needs a justification
that the deduction of categories has not supplied. This reading does not assert
that things in themselves are produced by experience or by our minds.

**Objective significance.** The Analytic’s apparently stronger claim is that
categories detached from sensible conditions cannot determine an object at all.
A248/B305 expressly retains a transcendental meaning while denying transcendental
use. Its negative/limiting or problematic role must therefore be retained. This
is not a blanket claim that talk of the intelligible is meaningless. The two
scope claims remain distinct proposition IDs; neither is silently equated with
an ontic denial. Both are constraints in a Kantian account of cognition rather
than universally accepted premises of the comparison.

**Conditionality and necessity.** The fourth antinomy’s thesis and antithesis are
staged conflicts, not two final commitments simultaneously endorsed by Kant.
Their resolution permits empirical conditioning without treating an intelligible
ground as another member of the empirical chain. It also denies that this
compatibility proves the ground exists, or even demonstrates its real possibility.
A state’s being replaced at another time does not establish that the contrary
was possible at the same time. Treating this as simple modal contingency would
change the argument’s subject.

**Ontological dependence.** Kant’s target is a completion of the cosmological
argument: existence of a necessary being, then identification of that being as
*ens realissimum*. The experience of an existent does not determine that
identification. Kant argues that the proposed conceptual identification makes
the most-real concept sufficient for necessary existence, importing the
ontological proof. This is distinct from his scope objection and his objection
to terminating the regress. It is not automatically a criticism of every
possible demonstration from effects.

## Strongest counterreadings and replies

These are reciprocal philosophical challenges, not kernel-certified verdicts.
Aquinas and Leibniz predate Kant: the primary sources state their own positions;
their deployment as replies to Kant is the present inquiry’s reconstruction.

| Question | Independently sourced counterreading | Strong Kantian reply | Remaining obligation |

| Can an effect warrant a transcendent cause? | Aquinas I.12 [6]–[9]: a *quia* demonstration uses sensible effects without first knowing the divine essence. Origin in sense need not restrict every demonstrated object to sense. | The question is precisely whether a causal category has objective validity beyond possible experience. Calling the step demonstration *quia* supplies no independent license for its transcendental use. | Defend either the restriction on determining cognition or an extra-empirical causal warrant. Neither is assumed universally here. |
| Must contingency reach an unconditioned ground? | Leibniz §§32,36–38: the reason for the contingent order cannot be supplied merely by more contingent members, even infinitely many. This concerns explanation of the series, not its first temporal instant. | An explanatory demand may regulate inquiry without constituting a known object. Mere logical noncontradiction of a proposed complete ground does not establish its real possibility; A609–610/B637–638 contests both the causal extension and reason’s satisfaction at terminating conditions. | Justify a whole-order sufficient-reason principle and its application, or show why the Kantian restriction blocks this particular inferential form. Local causality alone settles neither. |
| Does necessary being mean most real? | Leibniz §§39–41 adds uniqueness, universality and dependence of everything else before inferring unlimited reality. This has more content than bare necessary existence. Aquinas’s *quia* method does not start with an understood divine essence. | Those additions need independently warranted proofs; if their ultimate force is only the concept of the most-real being, the dependence objection returns. “We do not know the essence” does not itself demonstrate the alternative derivation. | Trace each attribute/identity bridge and its sources separately; do not erase this burden by defining “God” as whatever a proof has found. |
| Does the epistemic limit deny an intelligible being? | A realist may affirm such a being while accepting limited human cognition. | Kant himself disallows excluding an intelligible condition merely from empirical dependence, A562–564/B590–592. Practical faith and the complete Kantian moral system are outside this cosmological test’s commitments. |

The non-Thomist comparison is not made artificially independent of ontological
reasoning: Leibniz §45 explicitly offers an a priori proof too. His distinction
between that route and the earlier a posteriori route is his own position; Kant
contests their independence. The research leaves that dispute visible.

### Strongest positive account and its actual work

The useful positive strategy is not the claim that a predicate contains no
visible negation. It proposes that **complete real natures determine what can
exclude what**, and that genuinely simple, unlimited positive realities have
no constituent through which one excludes another. If all necessary exclusions
must be either immediately contradictory identities or analysable into such
identities, a putative hidden incompatibility cannot simply be asserted as a
primitive exception. This is an affirmative account of compatibility with an
explanation of why exclusions would be absent. It deserves more credit than an
unsuccessful contradiction search.

Its strongest epistemic defense starts with successful essential reasoning:
when a proposed kind has a fully understood constitution and its constituents
are independently realizable together, we often know why it can exist without
first observing an instance. A construction of a geometrical object can supply
such understanding within mathematics; an adequately supported productive
process can warrant an ordinary physical kind within its domain. This supports
the method of real definition. It does not show that our account of an unlimited
concrete nature is equally complete, that mathematical construction realizes
concreteness, or that physical recombination applies beyond physical requisites.
These are different applications, each needing its own warrant.

Descend through the positive strategy's three load-bearing steps:

1. **From genuine positive primitives to joint compatibility.** The argument
   needs completeness of its account of necessary exclusions. Making two names
   primitive removes definitions in our language; it does not establish that
   their real meanings lack essential relations. The strongest reply says the
   primitives are genuinely understood simple realities, not merely undefined
   words. Then the open question becomes whether we possess those adequate
   concepts and know that every exclusion between them would be analytically
   accessible. Ordinary successful analysis supports some cases; it does not
   establish that universal metaphysical completeness principle. A critic may
   accept knowable essences while allowing primitive essential exclusions.
   Neither side has been refuted by the corpus's stipulated finite models.
   Pairwise compatibility alone is insufficient for a whole nature; the positive
   argument intends arbitrary collections, so its warrant must cover that
   stronger scope, not merely an inspected pair.
2. **From compatibility to this concrete bearer-kind.** A jointly admissible
   description must include the relevant existence, individuation and concrete
   role. Simple positive qualities cannot merely be selected because a model
   makes them jointly realizable. Defining positivity as genuine joint
   realizability supplies the missing warrant in the classifier. Conversely,
   defining it without existential import leaves the realization step open.
   Known ordinary powers provide reason to take some positive activity seriously;
   deleting their material/organisational conditions does not yet show those
   powers subsist without those conditions. The reply that those are limits of
   recipients rather than of power is a substantive essence/participation
   account. It needs independent support, not a change of label.
3. **From real bearer-kind to necessary existence.** A noncausal intrinsic
   reason for existence is intelligible as a proposed explanatory relation and
   avoids the efficient-self-causation objection. It still needs existential
   content. A conditional saying that any instance has existence essentially
   neither yields an instance nor establishes an accessible one. A generic real
   essence may be uninstantiated; a particular existentially loaded essential
   fact needs warrant that this very bearer obtains. An actualist account on
   which such an essential fact requires its existing bearer can license an
   inference once that fact is known, but knowledge of a nominal description
   does not supply it. A production-based real definition is not the only
   admissible possibility argument; demanding production of an unproduced being
   would beg the question. What is missing is an adequately supported alternative
   essential route, not a demand that necessary being cause itself.

The selected intrinsic-existence strategy omits the full divine-attribute bundle
but is stronger than the bare necessary-concrete target: it seeks a concrete
bearer whose existence is intrinsic rather than received. That reduces the
number of potential attribute conflicts, which is a legitimate research gain.
It also removes the all-perfections argument's proposed reason for classifying
necessary existence as a perfection of this bearer. Thinness alone supplies
neither a complete real definition nor an essential existence fact.

### Defeasible positive warrant and the critic's burden

The preceding completeness test evaluates a proposed **demonstration**, not a
universal prerequisite for rational modal belief. The strongest advocate can
instead propose a defeasible presumption: independently intelligible positive
features are provisionally recombinable when no identified essential condition
excludes their union. Successful ordinary essential/constructive reasoning gives
some reason for this policy; it is not merely a demand that the critic disprove
an arbitrary string of predicates. This presumption can supply limited positive
credibility before complete analysis, with concrete defeaters kept visible.
It should not be discarded solely because a hidden exclusion is imaginable.

In this application the critic has **not** supplied a contradiction of the
selected intrinsic-existence nature or of bare necessary concreteness. The
strongest specific challenge to this strategy is a domain change: our successful
cases preserve independently understood realizers or productive conditions,
whereas the strategy's proposed bearer dispenses with received realization
altogether. This is not an extra condition on other necessary-concrete routes. The unbounded/intrinsic-existence step is precisely the feature
for which the analogy supplies no independently identified example. Whether
that is a recipient restriction or part of the power's nature is unresolved.
This is an undercutting challenge to the extrapolation, not a proof that the
new nature is impossible. The advocate can reply that unreceived being has
fewer dependency conditions and that demanding a producer prejudges the case;
that reply correctly defeats a universal producer requirement. It still needs
some content fixing what unreceived concrete subsistence is, and why that
content, rather than just its label, merits the recombination presumption.

Accordingly the positive conception is a serious, provisionally intelligible
support option. This defeasible warrant does not demonstrate the sufficient
possibility premise; provisional downstream inference may use it only with its
domain-extrapolation dependency explicit. A demonstrated
essential exclusion would defeat that version; an independently justified
intrinsic-existence nature would strengthen it; mere continued failure to find
a contradiction would neither turn it into a demonstration nor render the
presumption worthless. No criterion here requires exhaustive exclusion testing
before any defeasible modal confidence is allowed.
