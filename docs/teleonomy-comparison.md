# Darwinian teleonomy and the Fifth Way

This develops the existing alternative from #179 and #251. Its identity remains
`STMT-NATURAL-ENDS-WITHOUT-DIRECTING-INTELLIGENCE-TELEONOMY`. No primary mechanism
paper below is attributed that whole philosophical thesis. In particular,
explaining adaptation without an agent in the mechanism is weaker than denying
any intelligent director of nature. The latter remains an explicit open
commitment of the naturalistic rival.

The strongest rival considered here combines inherited variation and differential
reproduction, historical contingency and environmental constraint, local feedback
and self-organization, and lawful physical processes. It does not claim pure
random sampling produces adaptation, every trait is selected, selection creates
its own variations, evolution ensures perfection, or biological selection explains
falling bodies. The worldview accepts laws as its current explanatory stopping
point; it has no derived answer to why these laws exist. Mind and normative reason
are not explained by merely renaming them evolved capacities.

## Primary evidence and scope

Each source was fetched twice, pinned, materialized with `source_evidence.py`, and
read before adding its claims. Only hashes, selectors and paraphrases are stored.

* `DARWIN-ORIGIN-IV`: the complete first-edition chapter IV. Its opening and
  summary describe a better chance of survival and reproduction, with inheritance;
  these are tendencies, not guaranteed survival. The opening excludes neutral
  variation from selection; the third substantive paragraph requires profitable
  variation before selection operates. The chapter distinguishes artificial and
  natural selection but uses personifying language about nature. That language
  supplies neither a conscious selector nor a denial of God. The religious-belief
  passage requested by the old candidate has not been silently replaced with a
  claim that Origin IV says the same thing.
* `BLOUNT-LENSKI-INNOVATION-ABSTRACT`: Blount and colleagues, *Genomic and
  phenotypic evolution of Escherichia coli in a novel citrate-only resource
  environment* (2020), eLife 9:e55414. The reported 2500-generation experiments
  show refinement and mutations, alongside apparent fitness declines and cell
  death. This is a primary experimental report of constrained local adaptation;
  its abstract is not evidence for global optimality or the nonexistence of a
  director. The numerical findings are historical/experimental claims, not
  probabilities assigned to worldviews.
* `DYE-MECHANICAL-FEEDBACK-ABSTRACT`: Dye and colleagues (2021), eLife 10:e57964.
  Cell polarity and active rearrangements form a mechanical feedback mechanism;
  a continuum theory and MyoVI perturbation support an explanation of wing-disc
  shape and stress patterns. This does not identify all morphogenesis with
  selection. Nor is a living tissue example evidence that all nonliving order
  has this same mechanism.

The eLife author/publisher pages identify these as original English work, under
Creative Commons Attribution. The independently extracted source readings retain
the authors' experimental restrictions. Neither abstract establishes the entire
worldview. Nonliving pattern experiments were located (Tompkins and colleagues,
PNAS 2014, chemical droplets), but PMC and APS live response bytes drifted between
requests. No claim from those pages is represented as pinned evidence; this
remaining witness is recorded as a candidate. The argument proceeds with its
physical-law account open.

There is no `refutes` relation. Countermodels invalidate specified entailments;
they do not refute a sourced premise's real-world truth. In particular, the

no proved conclusion is introduced as a fresh premise.

## Fidelity critique and acceptance of the proofs

The Boolean filter's rule is explicitly stipulated. Replacing Darwin's
“better chance” by guaranteed retention would strengthen the source; therefore
no Darwin claim names that Lean declaration. Likewise, the two-cell copying
rule is only an independent possibility test; attributing it to Dye would erase
mechanics, stress and experimental context. The source claims have their own
independent identities and no invented Lean equations. Biological benefit is
relative survival/reproduction, not moral good or a globally best world.

The loaded senses are recorded in `DEF-TELEONOMY-SELECTION-FILTER`,
`DEF-TELEONOMY-LOCAL-COUPLING`, `DEF-TELEONOMY-MODEL-SCOPE`, and the reused
`DEF-INTENTIO-AS-TENDING` and `DEF-GOVERNMENT-DIRECTION-TO-END`.
`DISAMB-TELEONOMY-FINALITY-AND-AGENCY` distinguishes retrospective selected
function, local interaction, broad finality and intelligent direction. Body,
benefit, cognition, chance and director in the finite models have explicit
stipulative interpretations, rather than being equated with empirical objects.

I accept the stated model consequences: the filter computes a differential
output; copying computes alignment and stability; the preexisting teleonomy
and necessity theorems supply the two distinct countermodels; the one-director
assignment gives compatibility; and two distinct archers defeat the quantifier
exchange. None contains a bridge premise whose consequent is the advertised
global philosophical conclusion. Accepting these finite consequences does not
accept the truth of either worldview, the fidelity of a stronger source reading,
or closure of the Fifth Way's open philosophical obligations.
