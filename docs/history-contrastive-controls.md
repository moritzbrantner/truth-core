# Complete-history contrastive controls (#467)

The question is whether explaining every member of a contingent complete history
also explains why that history obtains rather than a genuinely possible rival.
`TruthCore.Modal.HistoryContrastive` isolates one missing bridge without assuming
that an explanation must be external. This is a bounded mathematical slice of
#467, not completion of its original automorphism/nondefinability acceptance or
a metaphysical answer to the owner's selection question.

## Same memberwise structure, differing contrastive assignments

The two Bool worlds are universally accessible. Each contains exactly the
members `(w,n)` for its tag `w` and every natural `n`. Every member has an
existing predecessor `(w,n+1)` via the existing world-local `Causes` relation.
Both histories occupy all supplied Nat times; time and predecessor indices are
separate parameters. This infinite chain is an analyst stipulation, not a
philosophical proof that an infinite regress is possible or adequate.

`SameHistory` compares presence of every member at every supplied time. These
histories differ, as witnessed by `(false,0)`. This is completeness of the
supplied presence extension, not a claim to encode every qualitative fact.
No aggregate being is formed.

The two assignments `reasons false` and `reasons true` have definitionally
identical ontology, causes and local `explains` relation: reason `r=w` is
assigned to each member of world `w`. Both satisfy local coverage and soundness.
They differ only in reason truth:

| Assignment | Reason truth | Contrastive consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constant | Every reason is true everywhere | The selected reason is necessary but fails discrimination at the accessible rival. |
| Internal | Reason `r` is true iff its bearer `(r,0)` exists | The selected reason is contingent and passes discrimination. Its bearer is absent at the rival. |

`Discriminates` explicitly demands that the reason is true at the designated
world and that every accessible world where it remains true has the same
supplied presence history. It is an entailing standard which some adequate
explanation theories may reject, not a synonym for metaphysical explanation.
The internal rival is tested before any externality restriction: even this
entailing standard permits an internal candidate. But this candidate's marker
can be considered circular or merely redescriptive; its local explanation label
does not settle that objection. **Neither assignment is certified as an adequate
sufficient explanation.** Why the internal marker itself obtains remains open.

The substantive omission is that the same infinite memberwise causal and
explanatory structure is compatible with different reason-truth assignments and
contrastive outcomes. It does not determine the designated world or whether a
reason for selection exists. Replacing the constant reason by an internal
contingent marker changes a supplied premise; it is not a derivation from local
coverage. No trichotomy by definitions is offered as a discovery.

## Necessity and epistemic controls

`necessary_discriminating_excludes_rival` proves, for arbitrary supplied
histories and reason truth, that the following bundle is inconsistent:

1. The same reason is true at every world accessible from the designated world.
2. That reason discriminates the designated presence history.
3. An accessible world has a different presence history.

Necessity supplies reason truth at the rival; discrimination supplies equality;
the rival supplies its negation. Necessity alone fails in the constant control.
This is the precise conditional exclusion; it does not establish necessity,
necessitation, causal production, or absence of rivals. No ground is silently
identified with a reason token.

Finally `discovered` is empty while an internal discriminator still exists.
This models no discovered reason without asserting that no reason exists; no
claim about actual human discoverability is made.
