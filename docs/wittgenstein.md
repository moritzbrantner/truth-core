## Modest use/practice positions: analyst classification (#1090)

The following four independently recorded propositions are **OPEN analyst
formulations; attribution is DEFERRED**. They supply the missing logic-first
comparison, not verified claims about Wittgenstein's wording or intentions.
No source/claim/passage record, self-evidence marking, inference or Lean result
is added. Their existential scope is deliberate: none says that every expression,
concept or practice must have the described character. No historical coverage
item is silently marked complete.

### Use-sensitive meaning

`STMT-USE-SENSITIVE-MEANING` says that at least some expressions cannot be
adequately understood merely by identifying a named object: their rule-governed
role in a specified practice is also needed to distinguish competent uses.
This is a positive proposal about what understanding some expressions requires,
not the universal claim that meaning is nothing but observed behavior or a
finite list of inferential transitions. Rules concern competent use, not merely
whatever most people happen to endorse. A familiar orientation is the different
role of “check” in chess and banking; this illustration is not source evidence
or a proof of the general philosophical proposal.

**Case classification:** neutral to the truth of the three nominal-identification
leaves (`STMT-GOD-NAMES-THE-FULLY-DESCRIBED-PRINCIPLE`,
`STMT-GOD-NAMES-THE-UNIQUE-MAXIME-ENS`,
`STMT-UNIQUE-PURE-ACT-FIRST-MOVER-IS-GOD`). It poses a **methodological challenge**
to transferring a formal predicate or word into religious use without checking
its role and intended reference. That challenge is not a truth rival asserting
that the referent does not exist.

The existing `REFERENCE-ANCHOR-BOUNDARY` (`#1074`) shows that agreement in all
specified first-order sentence truth and finite-premise inference behavior
need not fix a name's literal value on a fixed carrier. It neither quantifies
all pragmatic facts of use nor proves this modest meaning thesis. Its common
unique-anchor recovery remains conditional, and the structuralist who identifies
permuted interpretations survives. The stronger nonreference rival is already
`STMT-GOD-NAMES-NO-BEING-WF-P1`; it does not follow from the new position.

### Language games without insulation

`STMT-ACTIVITY-SENSITIVE-LINGUISTIC-PRACTICES` says that at least some linguistic
activities have activity-specific rules for competent moves, whose purposes and
rules are part of understanding their expressions. This permits a practice to
answer empirical questions with evidence shared outside the activity; it does
not claim that all actual practices do so. The independent issue is which moves
are correct by a practice's rules, not whether every assertion made there is
true or categorically binding.

**Case classification:** neutral to
`STMT-REVELATION-POSSIBLE-AND-RECOGNIZABLE`. Activity-specific rules alone neither
exclude shared evidence for revelation nor supply its recognizability. The sharp
cut-off statement `STMT-WF-RELIGION-CUT-OFF-FROM-SHARED-REASON` remains the distinct
**truth rival** already mapped to that leaf. No implication from the modest
position to that rival is registered.

`Fideism.Practice` provides reusable move/correctness/own-standard distinctions,
not a formal proof of the new existential position or a model of every language
game. Its Catholic-practice consequence uses the conciliar-definition and
shared-reason bridges explicitly; their applicability is still open. Existing
`STMT-MINIMALISM-ALLOWS-EXTERNAL-EVIDENCE` records a reported contrasting account.
It is not reattributed to Wittgenstein or merged with this analyst proposition.

### Family resemblance under a specified feature vocabulary

`STMT-FAMILY-RESEMBLANCE-CLASSIFICATION` proposes that at least one competent
ordinary classification can be sustained by overlapping patterns of similarity
among its instances without any single feature in a specified, independently
chosen classification-feature vocabulary belonging to every instance. The
feature vocabulary must be fixed separately from the category: “belongs to the
category” is not an informative independent feature for this comparison.

A stipulated illustration has three profiles with features {a,b}, {b,c}, {a,c}:
pairs overlap, but none of the listed features occurs in all three. This only
explains the proposal's shape. It does not establish that any actual concept is
adequately analyzed that way. A rival may posit a further common feature or a
disjunctive essence, dispute the feature vocabulary or deny the proposed
classification's adequacy. No connected-set theorem would settle those issues.

**Case classification:** neutral; **no root or floor target identified** for
this modest existential thesis. It does not say that all concepts lack a common
nature, that every definition fails, or that the case's uses of “being”, “cause”
or “God” are incoherent. Nor does the absence of a strict genus by itself prove
family resemblance or the analogy-of-being account. Those are different
positions requiring their own arguments. No universal anti-essentialist rival
is manufactured from this restricted proposal.

### Forms of life as practical background

`STMT-PRACTICAL-BACKGROUND-OF-LINGUISTIC-COMPETENCE` proposes that competent
participation in at least some linguistic practices depends on learned practical
capacities and shared regularities of action, for which possession of further
verbal interpretations alone is insufficient. The proposal is about competence,
not a definition of truth as agreement. “Shared” does not mean identical beliefs,
unanimous endorsement or one homogeneous culture; it does not make every
practice isolated or exclude a learner's acquiring the relevant capacities.

**Case classification:** neutral to the occurrence premise
`STMT-COGITO-THOUGHT-OCCURS` (candidate F5). It poses an **epistemic-basis
challenge** only to the additional proposal that verbal interpretation by itself
secures competent rule application. It neither denies thought nor establishes
that private introspection, public agreement or every shared practice is
infallible. No new direct rival to F5 is registered.

The existing interpretation-regress theorem shows the conditional incompatibility
of determination-only-through-interpretation, well-founded interpretation and
some determined application; it does not establish that practical background
exists or uniquely fixes meaning. `CorrectionCriterion.characterization` concerns
exact data-relative classification under a supplied norm; it provides neither
justification for that norm nor an account of forms of life. The already recorded
`STMT-OC-JUSTIFICATION-ENDS-IN-ACTING` is a source reading about justification's
practical endpoint, not an identification with this new existential competence
position. Nielsen's global insulation charge likewise remains a separate report,
not the consequence of practical dependence.

## Wittgensteinian fideism

### The worldview at full strength

The label is Kai Nielsen's (*Philosophy* 42, 1967). The SEP entry *Fideism*
(§2.2.4) reports that the view is "variously characterized" as entailing that
(1) religion is logically cut off from other aspects of life, (2) religious
discourse is self-referential and does not let us talk about reality, (3)
religious beliefs can be understood only by believers, and (4) religion cannot
be criticized. It also reports that Nielsen aimed at Wittgensteinians (Malcolm,
Winch), not Wittgenstein; that the entry's author doubts Wittgenstein would
have endorsed any of the four;
and that Phillips called the charge a caricature (1981, 89-90): he denied only
his critics' *conception* of the connection between religion and life and of
criticism (`STMT-PHILLIPS-DENIES-ONLY-CRITICS-CONCEPTION`).

Three variants are kept apart (`DEF-WITTGENSTEINIAN-FIDEISM`):

- **WF-N**, the sharp form: internal grammar (`STMT-WF-RELIGION-INTERNAL-GRAMMAR`)
  and cut-off (`STMT-WF-RELIGION-CUT-OFF-FROM-SHARED-REASON`).
- **WF-P1**, early Phillips: religious belief expresses a moral vision, and praise
  of God "does not refer to some object called God"
  (`STMT-PHILLIPS-GOD-TALK-NOT-REFERENCE-TO-AN-OBJECT`). This is a remark about
  the activity of praise. The stronger thesis that the name "God" applies to no
  being (`STMT-GOD-NAMES-NO-BEING-WF-P1`) is an unattributed reconstruction that
  the remark only supports.
- **WF-P2**, late Phillips and minimalism: "God" may be said to refer, but what
  reference, object and existence amount to must be shown in the religious
  context, and "true" has several senses
  (`STMT-PHILLIPS-REALISM-CONCEPTS-RELIGION-INTERNAL`). Religious beliefs depend
  on non-religious features of life but are "not generally" conclusions from
  them (`STMT-PHILLIPS-RELIGION-NOT-GENERALLY-CONCLUSION`). Minimalists allow
  outside evidence into religious justification
  (`STMT-MINIMALISM-ALLOWS-EXTERNAL-EVIDENCE`).

Its answers to the shared question set are in the answer map of
`TruthCore/Comparison/Fideism.lean`. Each answer there is labelled *reported*
(the SEP entries report it) or *reconstruction* (the comparison's own,
unattributed). Reported: what "God exists" says is fixed inside the practice
(WF-P2). Religious belief expresses a moral vision (WF-P1). Criticism from
outside is denied (WF-N) or reconceived (Phillips). No witness reports a
fideist answer to the cosmological or first-mover questions, so they are
**unexplained**, and the reconstructions there are labelled as such.

Sources. Phillips, Winch and Nielsen are in copyright, and no legitimately
hosted copy was found. They have bibliographic source identities only
(`PHILLIPS-RELIGION-WITHOUT-EXPLANATION`, `NIELSEN-WITTGENSTEINIAN-FIDEISM-1967`,
`WINCH-UNDERSTANDING-A-PRIMITIVE-SOCIETY`,
`NIELSEN-PHILLIPS-WITTGENSTEINIAN-FIDEISM-2005`). Every claim is read from the
pinned SEP entries, and each says it reports the position.

### Defeat by consequence: the practice's own rule

Fideism's thesis is descriptive: it says how the religious game is played.
The Catholic practice describes its own game in the opposite way. *Dei Filius*
ch. 2 teaches that God "can be known with certainty from created things by
the natural light of human reason", and canon II.1 condemns the denial
(`STMT-DEI-FILIUS-DEFINES-NATURAL-KNOWABILITY-OF-GOD`).

`TruthCore.Results.Alternatives.FideismCatholicPractice.fideismFailsAsDescription`:

```
CouncilDefinesNaturalKnowabilityReading P → DefinitionIsOwnStandardReading P →
  NaturalReasonIsSharedReading P → ¬ (InternalGrammarReading P ∧ CutOffReading P)
```

By the fideist's own internality, the defined move is correct. By the cut-off
thesis it is not. Each premise is needed (countermodels in the same Comparator
config), and the fideist bundle alone is consistent (`WF-CONSISTENT`). So the
conditional defeat comes from the community's rule together with the two open
bridges, not from incoherence.

What it shows, and what it does not:

- Given its open premises, it refutes WF-N **as a description of Catholic
  practice**. It says nothing
  about other practices, or about every possible fideistic proposal.
- It does not touch Phillips's "not generally" thesis: that thesis allows
  God's knowability by natural reason. Nor does it touch minimalism, which admits
  outside evidence.
- It is conditional. The canon is stated. The bridge that conciliar definitions
  belong to the practice's grammar (`STMT-CONCILIAR-DEFINITION-IS-PRACTICE-OWN-STANDARD`,
  supported by ch. 3) and the reading that natural reason is shared reason
  (`STMT-NATURAL-REASON-IS-SHARED-REASON`, supported by ch. 2's contrast with the
  "supernatural way", `DISAMB-NATURAL-REASON`) are open. Under #999 no refutes
  relation is recorded.
- The fideist's escapes are exactly the denials of the two bridges.
  - Treat the council as theology *about* the practice rather than its grammar
    (`needsDefinitionIsOwnStandard`). It may connect with Phillips's remark
    that his approach rescues belief and atheism "from philosophical
    distortion" (2005, 75, as quoted), but no witness says he read conciliar
    teaching so. The reconstruction is unattributed.
  - Re-read "natural reason" as a practice-internal standard
    (`needsNaturalReasonIsShared`).

  A further objection is recorded as unresolved and is not a consequence of
  either escape: that a fideist who takes an escape imports a standard from
  outside the game, against the internal-grammar thesis. The countermodels show
  only that each bridge is needed. The objection would need two bridges, neither
  recorded:

  1. that rejecting conciliar definitions as part of the grammar appeals to a
     standard not internal to the practice. It could instead appeal to
     distinctions within the practice, and the second escape is itself an
     internal re-reading of "natural reason";
  2. that the internal-grammar thesis forbids external standards.
     `InternalGrammarReading` formalizes only that internal endorsement implies
     correctness.

  It is not counted. Blocker: a typed account of who fixes a practice's
  grammar.

Self-application: an unresolved objection, not a result. One might argue
that the fideist thesis is a claim about the grammar of religion made in
philosophy, outside the religious game, and so cannot be correct by its own
lights. That does not follow from the reported theses.

- Thesis (3) restricts who can *understand* religious beliefs, not who can
  correctly make philosophical claims about religion. A believer doing
  philosophy is compatible with it.
- The generic schema `STMT-SELF-APPLICATION-DILEMMA` (`DEF-SELF-APPLICATION-NORM`)
  applies only if the fideist claim falls within the norm's scope and fails its
  standard. Placing the claim outside the game establishes neither.

The objection would need three explicit bridges, none recorded:

1. a norm whose scope includes claims *about* a practice's grammar;
2. a bridge from understanding to correctness;
3. the premise that the fideist claim fails that norm's standard.

Until then it stays an objection for the whole-worldview comparison, with no
Lean and no counted defeat.

Nielsen's critique (`STMT-NIELSEN-WF-INSULATES-FORMS-OF-LIFE`) is recorded as an
`attacks` relation on the cut-off thesis. It judges a consequence (no form of
life can be criticized or defended) unacceptable. Under #1007 that is
whole-worldview comparison, not falsity.

### The two bridges, sourced and defended (owner request 2026-11-05)

Both bridges remain open: each is supported, not stated, and neither is
established. So `WF-FAILS-AS-DESCRIPTION-OF-CATHOLIC-PRACTICE` still does not
count (#999). What changed is the evidence on each side and the fideist reply
at full strength, recorded as an `alternative_to` the bridge.

**(a) Conciliar definitions are the practice's own standard**
(`STMT-CONCILIAR-DEFINITION-IS-PRACTICE-OWN-STANDARD`). Supporting texts, each
a `supports` relation with its gap:

- *The practice's law of teaching.* CIC can. 749 §2: an ecumenical council
  teaches as "fidei et morum doctores et iudices" for the universal Church
  when it declares a doctrine definitively. §3: nothing counts as infallibly
  defined unless that is manifest.
- *Its standards of membership.* Cann. 750-751 make obstinate denial of what is
  proposed as revealed heresy. Can. 1364 attaches excommunication to heresy.
  Can. 1365 punishes teaching "doctrinam ... a Concilio Oecumenico damnatam".
- *Its self-description.* *Dei Filius* ch. 4 and can. IV.3: the sense of dogma
  is the sense "quem semel declaravit Sancta Mater Ecclesia".
- *Wittgenstein.* PU §373: "Welche Art von Gegenstand etwas ist, sagt die
  Grammatik. (Theologie als Grammatik.)"

The gaps that keep the bridge open:

- §3 and the dogma clauses do not show that can. II.1 is a dogmatic
  definition.
- Sanctioning the denial excludes a move; it does not yet endorse the defined
  move.
- §373 is an unexplained parenthesis.

Fideist reply, an unattributed reconstruction that Phillips's "rescued from
philosophical distortion" (2005, 75) only supports:
`STMT-COUNCIL-TEACHING-THEOLOGY-NOT-GRAMMAR`. Conciliar definitions are
second-order theology about the practice. The grammar is shown in worship and
life, and doctrinal formulae may carry philosophical distortion. Recorded
against it, as `attacks`, not refutations: §373, and the practice's own law
(can. 1365), which does not treat conciliar condemnations as optional theory.
The deciding question is who fixes a practice's grammar. It has no vocabulary
yet, so it stays a judgment.

**(b) "The natural light of human reason" is reason shared with outsiders**
(`STMT-NATURAL-REASON-IS-SHARED-REASON`). Supporting texts:

- *Dei Filius* ch. 4 distinguishes the two orders of knowledge "principio"
  ("in altero naturali ratione, in altero fide divina"). It cites the
  Apostle's testimony that God was known "a gentibus ... per ea, quae facta
  sunt".
- Scholastic usage: *Summa contra Gentiles* I.2 n.4, natural reason "cui omnes
  assentire coguntur", invoked against Muslims and pagans, who share no
  scriptural authority with Christians. I.3 n.2: the
  philosophers proved God's existence "ducti naturalis lumine rationis".

The gaps that keep the bridge open: the council does not say "shared with
outsiders", and Aquinas is a theologian of the practice, not its council.

Fideist reply, unattributed, supported by Clayton and Taylor as SEP *Fideism* §3
reports them: `STMT-NATURAL-REASON-WITHIN-THEOLOGICAL-HORIZON`. The tradition's
natural reason works within a theological horizon, towards fellow theists.
Recorded against it, as `attacks`: SCG I.2 invokes natural reason against
outsiders to Christianity, and *Dei Filius* ch. 4 appeals to the gentiles.
Whether those outsiders stand outside every theological horizon (the reply
allows fellow theists) is a further bridge the texts do not state. Clayton's own report
places Vatican I after the modern shift to a neutral starting point, which
cuts against the reply.

**Scriptural, patristic and scholastic usage** (owner decision 2026-11-06).
Each is a stating claim on its own narrower statement and a `supports` relation
to bridge (b), with its gap:

- Rom 1:19-21: those who suppress the truth knew God through the things that
  are made, "ut sint inexcusabiles". Rom 2:14-15: gentiles know the law by
  nature. (Nova Vulgata on vatican.va.)
- Justin, *Second Apology*: the seed of the logos is implanted "in every race
  of humans"; all writers could see the things that are, dimly, through it.
  (First1KGreek.)
- Augustine, *De civitate Dei* VIII.6: the pagan Platonists, reasoning from
  changeable things, reached the unchangeable first principle. He applies
  Rom 1:19-20 to them.
- Aquinas, ST I-II q.91 a.2: the light of natural reason discerns good and
  evil, and the gentiles have the natural law. q.94 a.4: the common principles
  of reason are equally known to all.

Rom 1 and Augustine are also recorded as attacks on the theological-horizon
reply: those Rom 1 speaks of are idolaters, and the Platonists are pagans.

**Bridge (b) is still not closed.** No text states it in the bridge's form.
Several speak of practical reason (Rom 2, ST q.91, q.94), or of God's showing
(Rom 1, Augustine). q.94 a.4 itself implies that God's existence is not a
common principle equally known to all. Under #999, closing the bridge would
need it established, which means refuting the horizon reply from established
premises.

**What would make the defeat count.** Each bridge would have to be established:
self-evident with a met basis (not plausible for either), or defended, with
every recorded rival refuted from established premises. That means refuting the
two replies above, which today are met only by stated texts. Both bridges stay
open, and that is the honest result.

### Who fixes the grammar: by cases (owner decision 2026-11-06)

The deciding question for both escapes is who fixes a practice's grammar.
`FIDEISM-GRAMMAR-CASES-PARTITION` splits it into three cells from excluded
middle alone, in the same form as the F7 partition:

1. the grammar is fixed by the practice's recognized authorities;
2. or not, and it is fixed by its ordinary practitioners' use;
3. or it is fixed by neither.

The partition is an instantiation, so it fixes the form only, not the cell.

`FIDEISM-FAILS-IN-AUTHORITY-AND-USE-CASES`:

- **Authority cell.** An open case bridge
  (`STMT-AUTHORITY-GRAMMAR-MAKES-DEFINITIONS-OWN-STANDARD`, supported by CIC
  can. 749 and *Dei Filius* ch. 4) yields the definition bridge. The two earlier
  defeats then apply. They are composed by importing their theorems.
- **Ordinary-use cell.** An open case bridge
  (`STMT-USE-GRAMMAR-MAKES-ORDINARY-USE-OWN-STANDARD`) makes ordinary-use moves
  the practice's own standard. The evidence that ordinary use affirms natural
  knowability and confesses the one true God is **supported, not stated**:
  - CCC 31-36: the ways to God are "argumenta convergentia et persuadentia",
    and CCC 36 repeats *Dei Filius*.
  - CCC 12: the Catechism goes chiefly to bishops and, through them, to
    catechists. So it is the teaching the faithful receive, not evidence of
    what they say.
  - CCC 185-197 and 199-200: the creed is a norm for all and the faith
    Christians profess. "Credo in Deum" is the first affirmation, and confessing
    one God is inseparable from confessing his existence.

## On Certainty

The witness is the German text, public domain in the EU, pinned as the raw
wikitext of one revision of the Ludwig Wittgenstein Project page. The Paul and
Anscombe translation is in copyright and is not used.

### Doubt rests on what stands fast (support, narrowly)

§341: "unsre *Zweifel* beruhen darauf, daß gewisse Sätze vom Zweifel ausgenommen
sind". §115: "Wer an allem zweifeln wollte, der würde auch nicht bis zum Zweifel
kommen."

`TruthCore.Results.Wittgenstein.DoubtPresupposesCertainty.universalDoubtFails`
derives the failure of universal doubt from §341. The premise is needed.

`anyGivenSentenceDoubtable` bounds what the result gives: §341 is consistent
with doubting any one chosen sentence. So the result could serve, once §341 is
established, against a *global* sceptical rival of an observation floor item. It supports no
particular item. It does not establish 'something changes' (F3) or 'something
is moved' (F4). It does not touch Pyrrhonian suspension, which neither
asserts nor doubts in this sense.

### What the hinges are: the reading that decides "established"

Four senses are recorded in `DISAMB-HINGE-STATUS`:

1. **Known truth** (Moore's own claim). Only on this reading would a hinge
   count as an established floor item with an observation basis.
2. **A-rational commitment** (Pritchard's quasi-fideism).
3. **Non-propositional animal certainty** (Moyal-Sharrock).
4. **Nonsense** (Read, Crary).

Passages that bear on sense 1 (how they bear is part of the dispute; which
passages each reading relies on is not recorded):

- §151: "Moore *weiß* nicht, was er zu wissen behauptet, aber es steht für ihn
  fest".
- §204: justification ends not in seeing that something is true but in our
  acting.
- §205, recorded as a conditional only: *if* the true is what is grounded, the
  ground is neither true nor false.

Which sense the text supports is disputed, and none is attributed here.

The bearing on F3 and F4 is conditional. It needs a bridge that no source
gives: that these particular items ('something changes', 'something is
moved') function as hinges. §151 discusses Moore's own propositions, and the
reported readings concern hinges in general. None identifies F3/F4 as hinges
or denies that those items are known.

*If* that bridge holds, then on senses 2-4 F3 and F4 would stand fast without
being established as known truths in the corpus's sense. They would not be
denied, but they would not be known truths that refute their rivals either.
Only the nonsense and non-propositional senses (3-4), and §205's conditional,
bear on whether they are true at all. Sense 2 (Pritchard) says nothing against
their truth.

The bearing is on the floor **basis**, not on the items. No reading of *On
Certainty* says that nothing changes or that nothing is moved. A hinge
reading is therefore not an `alternative_to` F3/F4, and none is recorded.
Recording one would reopen the approved markings.

### "There are physical objects" (F2)

§§35-36: "»Es gibt physikalische Gegenstände« Unsinn", since "physical object"
is a logical concept. This bears on how a candidate F2 ('something exists') is
phrased, not on its truth. Applied to an F2 phrased as an existential over a
formal concept, the remark would need a further, unattributed step: that
"something" is a logical concept the way "physical object" is. Phrased with a
material predicate ("here is a hand", §1), F2 would be a Moore-type
proposition. Whether it is then a hinge is the same open bridge as for F3/F4.

## Philosophical Investigations

The witness is the German *Philosophische Untersuchungen*, Part I, public
domain in the EU, pinned like *Über Gewißheit*.

### Rule-following (§§198, 201-202): the shape of the regress

`TruthCore.Results.Wittgenstein.InterpretationRegress.interpretationRegress` is
the logical point. Suppose an item is determined only through a determined
interpretation of it, and there is no endless chain of interpretations
("Deutung hinter Deutung"). Then nothing is determined. The proof is
well-founded induction.

`regressFork` adds that these two premises and "some application is
determined" cannot all hold. The readings choose differently:

- The diagnostic reading, following §201's own next sentences, keeps
  determination and gives up the interpretation premise: "eine Auffassung
  einer Regel ..., die *nicht* eine *Deutung* ist".
- Kripke's sceptic, as reported, takes the paradox as a genuine sceptical
  problem and denies that any fact determines meaning. In this vocabulary that
  is mapped, unattributed, to giving up factual determination; whether the
  figure keeps the interpretation premise is not recorded. Kripke does not
  identify the figure with Wittgenstein.
- An endless regress gives up finiteness.

The result settles no reading (`DISAMB-RULE-FOLLOWING-PARADOX`). Neutral to the
routes.

sections (reductio or nonsense, `DISAMB-PLA-FALSE-OR-NONSENSE`): the argument

**neutral** to candidate F5 (`STMT-COGITO-THOUGHT-OCCURS`) as an occurrence

### The rival and the two readings

On the **traditional** reading (`DEF-TLP-NONSENSE-ILLUMINATING`), the book bans
metaphysics to nonsense yet points at the unsayable. Reconstructed from 4.003,
4.11, 6.42 and 6.432, it yields a rival to the *arguability* of natural
theology: sentences about God are not false but nonsensical
(`STMT-TLP-GOD-SENTENCES-NONSENSICAL`). No proposition states it in this form,
so it is unattributed, and three claims (4.003, 4.11, 6.432) only `support`
it, each with a gap note.

Its object is `STMT-NATURAL-THEOLOGY-ARGUABLE`. Every route presupposes that
statement, but no route premise states it, so no route leaf gains a rival
entry. This is an honest gap: the routes leave their meaningfulness
presupposition implicit.

On the **resolute** reading (`DEF-TLP-NONSENSE-PLAIN`), the book's own
propositions are plain nonsense, and the book does not point at ineffable
truths but leads its reader away from that temptation. This is a claim about
the book, not a denial that such truths exist. On this reading the
reconstruction is not the book's thesis, so the book supplies no rival. That is the book's
self-application, which #1008 owns (`DISAMB-TLP-NONSENSE`).

Lean: not built. The Tractatus's tautology remarks (4.46-4.462) concern
sentences true under every truth-assignment. Applying them to necessary-being
claims is an unattributed reconstruction. It needs an open bridge: that the
necessity predicated of a being is the logical necessity of the proposition
asserting it (`DISAMB-NECESSARY` distinguishes the two), and that the
assertion is true under every assignment. The bridge is not recorded. Given
it, the remaining step would be a two-line unfolding with no new logical
content.

## Lecture on Ethics (1929)

The text is written in English by Wittgenstein. It is CC BY-NC by release of
the copyright holders, and public domain wherever the term is life plus 70
years.

- **Absolute value is no fact.** Relative value judgments are statements of
  fact, but no statement of fact is or implies a judgment of absolute value
  (`STMT-LE-ABSOLUTE-VALUE-NO-FACT`). Classified neutral to F6 on a relative
  reading of F6 ("one ought to believe what the evidence supports" as
  instrumental to truth). On an absolute reading of F6 the Lecture would class
  it as nonsense rather than false. Which reading F6 takes is not settled here.
- **Wonder at the existence of the world is nonsense,** because one cannot
  imagine the world not existing (`STMT-LE-WONDER-AT-EXISTENCE-NONSENSE`). The
  Lecture's condition concerns when *wondering* that something is the case makes
  sense. An objection to the statementhood of "the world exists" (a candidate
  F2) is an unattributed reconstruction. It needs a missing bridge: that the
  imaginability condition for wonder is also a condition of sense for existence
  assertions, as the Tractatus's bipolarity would suggest. The bridge is not
  recorded, and the objection is not attached to F2. Wittgenstein says that
  people referred to this experience when they said that God created the world.
- **Religious terms seem to be similes,** and where the facts cannot be stated
  without the simile the expressions are nonsense
  (`STMT-LE-RELIGIOUS-TERMS-SIMILES`). This is a rival in spirit to realist
  God-talk, close to WF-P1. It is not attached to a leaf: the Lecture says the
  terms "seem" to be used so, and it ends in respect, not denial
  (`STMT-LE-ETHICS-RELIGION-RUN-AGAINST-LANGUAGE`).

*Lectures and Conversations ... on Religious Belief* (1938) survive as students'
notes, edited by Barrett in 1966, and are in copyright. No hosted copy was

## Sentence use and reference: bounded analyst test (#1073)

`REFERENCE-ANCHOR-BOUNDARY` tests a missing bridge in #1010, with attribution
deferred. It does not formalize a claim of Phillips or Wittgenstein. The profile
has one name, one unary predicate, and Mathlib's full first-order syntax,
including equality, connectives and quantifiers. Literal reference is the name's
value on a fixed two-individual carrier. The original name denotes `false` and
the predicate picks `false`; transport both interpretations by Boolean negation
and the name denotes `true` while the predicate picks `true`.

Reuse inventory: pinned Mathlib `FirstOrder.Language.StrongHomClass.realize_sentence`
already establishes sentence truth under structure isomorphism, including the
quantifiers; `Equiv.inducedStructureEquiv` supplies the transported structure.
There is no reason to recreate either syntax or induction. Existing
`CorrectionCriterion` concerns classifiers on data fibers; `InterpretationRegress`
concerns well-founded interpretation dependencies; `Fideism.Practice` concerns
correct moves. None identifies the value of a name. Existing Verificationism
syntax lacks interpreted names. Russell's `DescriptionExists`, `ExistsPred`,
`AtMostOne`, and `Results.Modern.Russell.Description.checks` already supply the
exact existence/uniqueness analysis needed for the anchor.

The certificate composes those theorems: all sentences agree and every finite
premise-list truth-preservation inference at either interpretation agrees, yet
the literal referents differ. This is stronger data than any selected finite
sentence table, but it remains extensional truth/inference behavior, not all
pragmatic, causal or historical facts about use. The two labels designate
individuals of the model; they do not assert two actual metaphysical objects.

A fixed external predicate `anchor x := x = false` has a definite description
and distinguishes the referents. More generally, if both referents satisfy one
fixed `DescriptionExists` predicate, Russell's previously proved uniqueness
analysis forces identity. Both satisfaction requirements and the common fixed
anchor are additional conditions; sentence agreement does not provide them.
Transporting the anchor along with the other predicate abandons this extra
constraint. No premise establishes a real language's possession or justified
recognition of such an anchor; that applicability remains **OPEN**.

The strongest rival is structuralism: it identifies interpretations related by
such a permutation and therefore accepts their agreement. Literal carrier
inequality does not refute it. Rejected inferences: no name denotes, God does not
exist, religious discourse is meaningless, or the Catholic nominal-definition
leaves are discharged. The experiment does not connect their shared reference
relation yet. Root premises and #1010's existing conditional defeat remain
unchanged; #1073 supplies one boundary, not completion of #1010.
