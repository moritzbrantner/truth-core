**Argument-first, demand-driven decomposition** for self-directed frontier investigations: start from a substantial thesis, its end-to-end case and its strongest rivals; seek the decisive fork; promote a distinction to its own statement or task only when it passes the decomposition gate (inference depends on it, it discriminates positions, it admits independent progress, it is reused, or an invariant requires it). Source ingestion, the proof contract and frontier closure are unchanged. See `docs/systematic-corpus-loop.md`.
