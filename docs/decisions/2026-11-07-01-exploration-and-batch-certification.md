**Exploration counts; the no-bare-premise rule is discarded.** A change that adds bare premises is no longer "not progress": newly found propositions, intermediate steps and analyst bridges are kept, labelled open, and reported (`frontier-diff` still lists bare premises added, for visibility, not as a regression). Exploration progress is measured with `corpus.py thickness` (derived root-path statements and derivations). Work proceeds in two tiers: an **exploration map** proposes a whole argument DAG for a target quickly (existing statement IDs reused, new nodes marked), and **batch certification** checks a cluster of 10–15 derivations in one module and one PR, records generated from one spec with `scripts/argument_batch.py`. **Controls are lighter by default**: one nonvacuity witness per batch, plus omission countermodels only for the premises that carry the argument; full per-premise omission controls remain welcome but are no longer required for every derivation. Transcription rules, frozen Challenges, zero-axiom/Comparator/NanoDa gates and exact premise reporting are unchanged.
