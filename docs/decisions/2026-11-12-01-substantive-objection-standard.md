**Substantive Objection Standard.** After criticism that an agent dismissed the
Grim Reaper argument as one special case without formulating any difference
that would stop it generalizing, the owner required every objection to name the
premise, inference or assumption it challenges, the concrete failure mechanism,
independent justification for that mechanism and its relevance to the actual
argument, and to distinguish established counterexamples from hypothetical
possibilities. A claim that an argument does not generalize must identify the
specific relevant difference and why it matters. Contrived alternatives kept for
artificial balance, and unspecified possibilities of error, carry no evidential
weight; when no substantive objection is available, say so plainly. The standard
applies equally to arguments for and against theism, naturalism and every other
worldview. A finite countermodel is an established fact about one formal
interpretation, not by itself evidence that a premise is false. Proof, binding,
source and closure rules are unchanged. The current contract is in `AGENTS.md`,
"Substantive Objection Standard".
