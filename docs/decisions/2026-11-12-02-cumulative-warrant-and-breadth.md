**Cumulative warrant and meaningful breadth.** Expand the corpus across relevant
propositions, argument routes, interpretations and rival views; meaningful
coverage and additional conditional support are progress even when counts of
open leaves rise or formal closure is unchanged. Keep the direct prima facie
option for a proposition alongside its derivations. Do not demand independent
proofs for every premise or disjoint routes in an interconnected evidential
web. Assess substantive contributions, shared dependencies, non-question-begging
support, necessary inquiry preconditions and serious defeaters holistically.
Distinguish self-refutation, self-undermining, lack of internal adoption reasons
and incompatibility with necessary preconditions as separate comparative tests.
Surviving a substantive test may improve relative credence; passing no known
objection is not by itself proof. Frontier closure and Lean verification remain
unchanged. Supersedes the older research-priority reading of "depth over volume"
and the premise program as a demand for recursively independent justifications;
the current contract is in `AGENTS.md` and `docs/systematic-corpus-loop.md`.
