Copyrighted modern works may be cited by edition and page without pinned bytes (option 1c), but only when no pinnable witness (git text, public-domain edition, `web_snapshot` of a legitimately hosted copy) exists. This is the passage kind `bibliographic_span` (author, title, publisher or journal, year, ISBN or DOI, exact pages or section, at most a short quotation) with a required `why_bibliographic` note; it amends AGENTS.md invariants 1, 2 and 18. Every claim on it carries `witness: "bibliographic"`, shown as not byte-verified by `corpus.py frontier`, the dossier and the explorer; `verify` lists it unverified and `materialize` refuses it. It is upgraded in place to a pinned witness without changing claim IDs; owner-held hashed local copies (option 1b) are the intended future upgrade. See `docs/source-evidence.md`.
