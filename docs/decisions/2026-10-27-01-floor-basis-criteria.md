Floor by criteria, not owner approval, and only as a last resort (#378 point 3: people disagree about what is self-evident). Close a premise by derivation or by refuting its alternatives where possible; mark `self_evident` only when no other closure is available. A marking names its `basis`, and `corpus.py frontier` recomputes whether the criterion holds from the current records. **retorsion**: a verified non-transcription result refutes the recorded denial (`denial_statement_id`, an `alternative_to` the premise) from closed premises only. **common_ground**: the premise has no recorded rival, and every position on the disputed question (`question_statement_id` and each `alternative_to` it, at least one) has a proponent claim stating the premise; a newly recorded rival reopens it. **observation**: a claim states the premise, and every recorded rival is listed in `sceptical_rival_ids` and refuted. Every marking except common_ground (where the rivals' agreement is the record) carries `disagreement`: the recorded rivals and/or claims that deny it is self-evident, or `"none recorded"`; a recorded rival missing from it reopens the marking. `logical` is no basis: prove the premise instead. An unmet basis closes nothing and `check` warns; closure is reported relative to the listed bases. `STMT-EXCLUDED-MIDDLE` keeps its marking as the documented `legacy` default.
