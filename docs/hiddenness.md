# Divine hiddenness and nonresistant nonbelief

This is the independent objection of [#252](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/truth-core/issues/252), within [#143](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/truth-core/issues/143). It concerns missing access to conscious relationship, even where no suffering is alleged. The logical problem of evil [#150](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/truth-core/issues/150) and evidential/natural evil [#168](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/truth-core/issues/168) therefore remain separate. Neither an answer to evil nor an argument for a first cause automatically answers hiddenness.

## The strongest argument encoded here

Schellenberg's author-hosted 2025 *Précis*, especially pp. 2–4 and 10, makes capacity, time and the causal role of resistance explicit. His conceptual premises claim necessity; the empirical existential concerns actual people. `TruthCore/Hiddenness/Vocabulary.lean` accordingly uses worlds, persons and times, with an actual-world index. The world domain is a parameter, not a proof of metaphysical possibility or a modal completeness theorem.

The premises are `STMT-HIDDENNESS-GOD-LOVES`, `STMT-HIDDENNESS-ALWAYS-OPEN`, `STMT-HIDDENNESS-ACCESS-NEEDS-BELIEF`, and `STMT-HIDDENNESS-NONRESISTANT-NONBELIEF`. Assuming God exists supplies a perfectly loving God. At the time and person supplied by the empirical existential, unconditional openness then supplies conscious reciprocal access. The belief constraint excludes that very access, yielding `STMT-HIDDENNESS-NO-GOD`. `HIDDENNESS-NO-GOD` binds the exact four premises and conclusion; it does not assume a bridge whose consequent is already the final conclusion.

The belief premise is kept in its negative source form: lack of belief prevents access. This avoids silently using double-negation elimination to turn that sentence into an affirmative belief-producing principle. Every conceptual premise is quantified over the supplied worlds; only the empirical existential is asserted at `actual`. The conclusion concerns a personal religious ultimate, not every possible nonpersonal ultimate and not the truth of metaphysical naturalism.

`HIDDENNESS-PREMISES-INDEPENDENT` constructs four separate finite interpretations retaining God and respectively omitting each premise. The omissions allow, in order, a nonloving God, delayed access, conscious access without belief, or universal belief. They show the conclusion does not follow from any three premises. The omitted premise also fails in each witness, and this is forced rather than incidental: `HIDDENNESS-NO-GOD` derives `¬ GodExists` from all four, so a witness with God existing and the other three premises cannot satisfy the fourth. The four premises are therefore mutually independent. They do not establish that those interpretations are philosophically adequate or metaphysically possible.

## Reading choices and replies

**Nonresistance is causal.** It is not global blamelessness, evidential reasonableness, or merely a sincere profession of seeking. A person who never acquired the concept need not actively seek to qualify; conversely, testimony of seeking alone does not establish nonresistance. Doubt counts as nonbelief. `DISAMB-HIDDENNESS-NONRESISTANCE` records these distinctions. Capacity is indexed to time; an inference from all prehistoric humans to capable nonresisters would require further evidence.

**The relationship is conscious and reciprocal.** An unnoticed causal relation, an implicit orientation toward the good, salvation, and felt presence are different questions. Schellenberg explicitly objects to substituting an unconscious relation when disputing his belief premise. Such a reply should instead dispute what relation perfect love must make available at each capable time. Constant vivid sensation is not required by the encoded objection.

**Rea's reply concerns more than coercion.** His author-hosted *Divine Hiddenness, Divine Silence*, pp. 270–274, proposes formation of non-self-interested love and divine personality/transcendence as possible reasons for silence; he also proposes communion mediated by narratives and liturgy. A believer may still refuse relationship (`HIDDENNESS-BELIEF-WITHOUT-ACCEPTANCE`), so a bare implication from revealing existence to compelled acceptance fails. This does not answer the subtler motivational concern, nor prove that any particular mode of revelation preserves freedom.

The reply must meet the strongest objection: why could a loving God not provide modest, nonoverwhelming belief, retaining room for free refusal, development, and deeper relationship? A possible benefit of silence does not establish a sufficient reason to withhold all explicit access from this person now. Divine transcendence also does not license attaching the word “loving” to any possible behavior. `STMT-HIDDENNESS-QUALIFIED-OPEN` records the weaker norm, and `STMT-HIDDENNESS-REASON-FOR-EACH-GAP` makes its stronger application obligation explicit and unattributed.

**The Catholic positive account includes grace and salvation.** The Latin *Lumen gentium* §16 says providence does not withhold necessary salvific helps from those without fault who have not reached explicit acknowledgement of God and strive with grace for a right life. The conditions matter. This neither guarantees every person's salvation nor says every such person already has the conscious access Schellenberg demands. The same paragraph maintains the importance of evangelization. Its neighboring chapter context preserves that ecclesial setting.

`HIDDENNESS-CATHOLIC-REPLY-CONSISTENT` constructs a two-time interpretation of the qualified reply, including nonresistant nonbelief early and explicit access later. `HIDDENNESS-QUALIFIED-NOT-ALWAYS` verifies an interpretation that satisfies the reply's commitments (with God existing and nonresistant nonbelief) and violates unconditional openness. It therefore does not refute the strongest argument while keeping its premises. It shows exactly where the reply must disagree. The interpreted sufficient reason is not a discovered divine reason. No result proves eventual access for all actual persons or after death.

## Shared questions and rival bundles

The Catholic bundle combines personal creation and final destiny, objective goodness, grace, and historical revelation with the qualified hiddenness reply. The non-theistic rival combines denial of a personal ultimate with a developmental account of belief, reflective doubt, and continued inquiry into nonpersonal ultimate possibilities. It is a comparison bundle, not an attribution of every adopted premise to Schellenberg, and it is not identified with a closed naturalist metaphysics. Unanswered questions stay **unexplained**, rather than being treated as victories over the other bundle.

The same twelve questions are asked of both systems. “Answered” means an adopted proposition or proposal is stated, not that its truth, causal adequacy or full empirical coverage is established.

Counts: the Catholic bundle gives **11 adopted answers/proposals and 1 unexplained adjudication**; the non-theistic bundle gives **7 adopted answers/proposals and 5 unexplained questions**. These are coverage counts, not plausibility scores. Several adopted answers are open empirical or interpretive obligations. In particular, adopting a sufficient-reason answer does not explain any individual case until its sufficient reason is defended.

The finite theorem bundles cover only the time-indexed hiddenness commitments, not every cell above. `HIDDENNESS-NONTHEISTIC-CONSISTENT` checks the logical core. `HIDDENNESS-NONTHEISTIC-DEVELOPMENT-CONSISTENT` strengthens it with concept-dependent belief and the developmental replacement account, using the same human time history as the Catholic interpretation. Religious belief can be false under that model. Genuine reciprocal access is unavailable at both times, because the model has no divine party; later belief supplies no actual relationship. Satisfying its interpreted account is not empirical verification of cultural explanation, and the universal divine norms there hold vacuously. No comparative superiority follows from that vacuity.

## Logical incompatibility and explanatory concern

The deductive argument requires the exceptionless access premise and one actual capable nonresister lacking belief. Disputing either premise blocks that derivation; merely preferring another explanation does not refute it. Conversely, consistency of a qualified Catholic model does not answer the explanatory question: why these people, cultures, durations, losses of faith and missed opportunities, rather than modest access compatible with refusal? The developmental rival must account for those same observations and for apparent encounters and conversions, not only for absence of belief. Comparative adequacy is an open investigation of competing accounts, not a deductive verdict, probability calculation or aggregate coverage score.

## Open evidence obligations

The existential remains an open premise in the formal frontier. `HIDDENNESS-GAP-HAS-REASON` and the explicitly described transcription `HIDDENNESS-GAP-HAS-DEVELOPMENTAL-ACCOUNT` also expose the rival application premises as open frontier nodes; neither supplies empirical validation. Its attributed primary source is an argument for the premise, not independently established testimony or a dataset. Concrete cases require evidence of capacity, actual lack of belief, and the absence of resistance causing that nonbelief. Self-reports, third-person histories and psychological evidence may support those different claims differently. Prehistoric concept absence requires historical/anthropological evidence; mere absence of surviving monotheistic records is insufficient.

The Catholic application premise needs an actual sufficient reason for **each** admitted gap, including duration and losses of relationship; suggesting goods without showing their necessity or compatibility does not discharge it. The rival's universal developmental account needs representative histories, competing causal accounts and evidence that its proposed mechanisms cover the observed distribution. These are explicit open obligations, not certainty numbers.

Schellenberg's examples of doubting contemporary inquirers and pre-monotheistic concept absence motivate separate candidate ranges. The Schellenberg and Rea author PDFs were each fetched twice with stable bytes, materialized through the merged PDF adapter, and read in full; the Vatican chapter was likewise materialized and read. Rea's possible explanations and their limits are sourced separately from the stronger universal application premise. The historical findings already materialized for #223/#254 remain open under their own report/event distinctions. This issue adds no duplicated resurrection vocabulary and closes none of those historical nodes.

## Fidelity self-sweep

The four-premise conclusion is accepted as a formal consequence: the proof instantiates the actual person and time and derives incompatible access facts. Its force depends on the four open premises, not the model author's agreement. The two-time constructions are accepted as interpretations of the encoded constraints only. They establish neither real-world premise truth nor modal possibility.

Definitions and disambiguations are linked from the new statements and arguments: God/personal ultimate, perfect love, capacity, causal resistance, belief, conscious access, acceptance, sufficient reason, concept possession, reflective doubt, model scope, and encoded-theory consistency. The contrast records separately cover reasonable nonbelief, seeking, salvation, implicit relationship, and mediated presence. Source claims preserve those contrasts and do not use a translation for the Latin conciliar witness. No source claim attributes the universal sufficient-reason or exhaustive developmental account to an author.
