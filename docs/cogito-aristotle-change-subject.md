# Cogito and the Aristotelian subject of change

## Finding: a scoped bridge, not a universal bearer proof

The corpus already records the source-derived **Physics III.1, 201a10–11**
reading `STMT-CHANGE-ACTUALITY-OF-POTENTIAL`
(`ARIST-PHYS-201A-CHANGE-A`): change is the actuality of something
potentially existing, precisely qua potential. It is **not** a theorem
that every actual act or every thought is a change.

The new conditional composition has three explicit premises:
1. `STMT-THOUGHT-CHANGE-ACTUAL-THOUGHT`: some thought episode occurs.
2. `STMT-THOUGHT-CHANGE-CLASSIFICATION`: every selected thought episode
   is change in the source's sense (an **open**, unattributed bridge).
3. `STMT-CHANGE-ACTUALITY-OF-POTENTIAL`: Aristotle's recorded definition,
   an independently sourced but **not independently established** premise.

The conclusion is that **some actual thinking change has a potential
subject**. This is a **transcription/composition** of the source reading
with the other premises, not a new proof of their truth.

Three independent finite omission controls demonstrate minimality
relative to this vocabulary:
- **No thought:** the classification and Physics reading hold vacuously,
  but no actual thinking change has a subject.
- **Nonchanging thought:** a thought occurs, the Physics reading holds,
  but the thought is not classified as change.
- **Unconstrained change:** a thought occurs and is called change, but
  the Physics reading is false and no potential subject is witnessed.

The controls are logical interpretations, not demonstrations of
metaphysical possibility. In particular, the static thought control
prevents silently deriving physical motion or temporal succession from
the mere performance of an assertion (see #509 and PR #516).

## Independent route already in the corpus

Aquinas, *ST* I q.75 a.2, has a different and stronger
`STMT-PER-SE-INTELLECTUAL-OPERATION-REQUIRES-SUBSISTENCE` premise.
`AQUINAS-ST-I-Q75-A2-INTELLECT-SUBSISTENCE` already formalizes the
qualified inference and records that the operation/subsistence
correspondence remains **open**. Its conclusion is weak non-inherence,
not completeness, immortality or an enduring person. Reuse that result;
do not duplicate its vocabulary here.

## Remaining epistemic frontier

The next genuine philosophical burden is to justify at least one of:
- that the actual thought is a change in the Physics III.1 sense; or
- that a non-changing act of thought also requires an ontological
  bearer, using a separate theory of dependence or intentionality.

Neither follows from logic, the occurrence of an episode, first-person
grammar, or the sourced definition of physical change alone. An
independently justified observational occurrence remains open too.
