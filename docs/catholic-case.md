# The Catholic case: program, decisions, and working method

This document is the durable handoff for the campaign tracked in epic #143. It records what the owner decided and how the work is run, so that a new session on any machine can continue without the previous session's local notes. The current snapshot of open work lives in the latest "Handoff" comment on #143. GitHub issues and PRs remain the work state.

## Goal

Investigate the philosophical, historical and theological case for the fullness of Catholicism through rigorous comparison with its strongest alternatives, without presuming the conclusion. In the present foundational phase, thoroughly explore competing philosophical systems: what they entail, what they explain, where they conflict, and which commitments remain open. Connect findings from multiple angles into the larger case.

Formalize proposed arguments as a graph of interpreted Lean 4 propositions, with sources where attributed and explicit analyst formulations otherwise. Investigate assumptions, verified implications, countermodels, substantive forks and premise warrant. Measure progress by clearer distinctions, alternatives assessed and decisive dependencies exposed or resolved, rather than coverage or result counts. On any proposed root proof, premises must be defended under the existing closure criteria; a provisional assessment does not settle an unresolved rival or premise.

Lean verifies **consequence**, not premise truth. Assess premise warrant through observations, actual performances, testimony, conceptual arguments and explanatory considerations, with explicit dependencies and defeaters. Issue reasoned, dated and revisable **overall provisional judgments** under the [systematic loop](systematic-corpus-loop.md), keeping them distinct from proof verification, fidelity review and computed establishment. Personal approval supplies no independent philosophical warrant.

Formal results retain the [axiom policy](axiom-policy.md): Comparator configs permit no axiom, except that a result whose proof uses Mathlib may permit exactly Lean's standard `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`. Such a classical result never supplies evidence in a dispute that itself withholds excluded middle. Defeasible premise support must not be encoded as an added axiom.

## Root and structure

- **Root (phase 1, #156/#159):** `STMT-CATHOLICISM-TRUE` := (A) the Catholic Church is a teaching authority founded by God (no uniqueness asserted) (Vatican I, *Dei Filius* ch. 3, pinned on vatican.va in #170) ∧ (B) the twelve articles of the Nicene–Constantinopolitan Creed (Greek, `CREED-C381`).
- **Phase 2 (later):** the full list of defined dogmas (Ott, Denzinger), candidate `CATHOLICISM-ROOT-PHASE-2-DOGMAS`. The phase-1 root is a limited formal target within the broader inquiry; establishing it would not by itself establish the fullness of Catholicism.
- **Skeleton:** the root ⇐ six stages plus explicit bridges (`TruthCore/Results/Case/Catholicism/Skeleton`):
  1. God exists
  2. classical attributes
  3. revelation possible and recognizable
  4. Jesus' claims and the Resurrection
  5. Christ founded a visible Church
  6. that Church is the Catholic Church
- Unproved stages are open premises, never axioms.
- **Refutation track:** objections and rival worldviews (atheist and naturalist, other religions, Protestant and Orthodox positions).

## Modeling decisions worth knowing

- **First Way (#151):** strong per se reading ("moved by none in any respect") plus a weaker sibling reading; an interpretive argument that the strong reading is Thomas' meaning. The conclusion is double-negated (`¬¬∃`), so excluded middle is an explicit premise.
- **Excluded middle (#169):** a single general principle `STMT-EXCLUDED-MIDDLE`, marked `self_evident`, argued from Aristotle's elenchus and from constructive Lean results that refute *denial*. Intuitionist *withholding* is recorded as an alternative that is not yet refuted.
- **Stage-1 gap (#166, #173):** pure act, simplicity and uniqueness are derived. The identification with God was relocated to narrower premises such as `STMT-MAXIME-ENS-IS-GOD` (now derived, `AQUINAS-ST-I-Q2-A3-MAXIME-ENS-IS-GOD`) and `STMT-SIMPLY-PRIOR-IS-CAUSE-OF-BEING`. Countermodels show that no listed premise follows from the others in its argument.
- **Graph integrity:** `corpus.py bind-check` (#164) verifies in CI that each argument's JSON premises and conclusion are exactly the Lean theorem's hypotheses and result type. Theory bundles are unfolded one step (#160).
- **Visualization:** `apps/corpus-explorer` `/graph` (#163). Run `cd apps/corpus-explorer && bun run dev`, then open http://localhost:3000/graph/.
- **Connected stages 1–2 (2026-10-25):** `CATHOLICISM-FROM-PRIMA-VIA-WITH-CLASSICAL-ATTRIBUTES` carries one named maximal mover through the nine attribute proofs into the existing skeleton. The root theorem no longer takes the complete attribute bundle as an independent input. `STMT-GOD-IS-MAXIMAL-IN-BEING` remains open for the uniqueness attribute; no naming converse is inferred (the premise is itself that converse) and no frontier closure is claimed. See [the connected proof](skeleton-composition.md).
- **Connected regress and logic inputs (2026-10-25):** `CATHOLICISM-FROM-EFFICIENT-REGRESS-WITH-CLASSICAL-ATTRIBUTES` additionally imports the existing efficient-cause regress transfer and excluded-middle instance before applying that connected root proof. Those proved intermediates are no longer independent root-theorem inputs; the same 37 philosophical premises remain open.
- **Act and potency (#352, 2026-10-26):** the eight act/potency premises shared by the root routes are mutually independent (finite countermodels), and defining potency or being moved as the definition records describe them would make two of them analytic without discharging them. The prima via needs only that each moved thing has *some* mover in act. No root premise is closed; see [the act/potency note](act-potency-theory.md) for the remaining obligations.

## Working method

- **Coordinator and bounded workers.** Follow the [systematic loop](systematic-corpus-loop.md): the master prioritizes live uncertainties, gives fresh workers explicit context and budgets, integrates useful early returns, revises provisional judgments and requeues concrete next steps. Use isolated writer checkouts and the ownership rules in `AGENTS.md`; parallel lanes A/B/C remain available. Keep decisions and the one current handoff in the existing records.
- **CI.** GitHub Actions run on every PR (`.github/workflows/ci.yml`). Use the toolchain declared by `lean-toolchain` and the repository's current setup, rather than assuming a floating stable Lean release.
- **Review and merge.**
  - Request one targeted `@codex review` per PR, plus at most one re-request when a review hangs or after real P1 fixes. Fix real P1s only.
  - Merge with plain `gh pr merge --squash` only after a **completed** review with no open P1 whose reviewed commit is the head (or only trivially rebased).
  - Never use `--admin`. Never merge without a completed review; #158 was the one exception, kept by owner decision.
  - Renovate PRs are the standing exception (owner decision on #378): they merge on green CI without waiting for a Codex review, which skips bot PRs. Branch protection still requires `corpus-and-lean`, `corpus-explorer` and `comparator`.
- **Memory-constrained machines.** Wrap heavy commands (`lake build`, `verify-*`, `bun install`, `next build`, `source_evidence.py verify`) in `flock /tmp/<user>/truth-core-heavy.lock` and keep build parallelism low.
- **Fresh machine setup.**
  - Install `elan` and use the declared `lean-toolchain`; follow [worker startup](worker-startup.md) for the repository's current toolchain/cache setup.
  - Run `scripts/bootstrap-verifier.sh`, which builds the Comparator, lean4export (the tag matching the resolved Lean version), landrun and NanoDa into `~/.cache/truth-core-verifier`.
  - Media spans also need a youtube-corpus export (see `docs/source-evidence.md`).
  - Bun's `globalStore = true` (in `~/.bunfig.toml`) breaks the explorer's lint and `next build` with missing-module errors. For local CI, install with a `HOME` that has no such bunfig.

## Lessons from earlier attempts (2026-10-23 retrospective)

- **Schema and infrastructure before content.** truth-engine went through about 10 schema versions in 15 days, and its solver services had no real workload. They are archived.
- **Decorative formalization.** Opaque `Prop` atoms with the conclusion inside a bridge premise: about 84 of 103 Aristotle results are 1–2-line transcriptions.
- **Volume metrics.** About 100 results in 3 days, then a stall.
- **The rule that follows, refined 2026-11-10:** report what changed for a live question: clarified meanings, useful conditional consequences, exposed dependencies, assessed warrant/defeaters or resolved alternatives. Report computed settlement separately with `python3 scripts/corpus.py frontier --reaching-root`; neither node volume nor closure count substitutes for research value.
