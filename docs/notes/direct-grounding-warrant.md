## What the primitive must mean

The Profile's `grounds w y x` is an input relation. For this application it is
direct existential support by y for x, restricted to candidates existing in the
selected world; `ActualConcreteGround` additionally requires the supplier to be
CONCRETE_6. It is not defined by the inference that a root exists. Existence,
concreteness and support remain interpreted inputs. Time is not encoded here:
when a world denotes a complete history, world-existence does not entail
simultaneous existence. Present sustaining requires an additional abstraction
bridge, not a gloss on `ExistsAt`.

Fuel sustaining a flame or electrical supply maintaining a lamp can orient an
intervention-sensitive dependence relation. They do not identify direct support
of *existence as such*: a generator produces electrical activity, not every
aspect of the lamp's existence, and these open physical systems have external
inputs. A genealogical ancestor, an equation describing motion, an earlier
cause of an event, and a part constituting a whole cannot be entered as the
same edge without argument. A law might describe, govern or ground a fact;
only the last, with the right target, could challenge existential support.
No transitivity, asymmetry, conserved quantity, uniform loss, or supplier
concreteness follows from the primitive's name.

**OPEN application obligations:** identify the supplied explanandum (a thing,
its existence fact, activity or quantity); determine what makes an edge direct;
justify the world/time abstraction and candidate inventory; distinguish partial
from sufficient support; and explain why all relevant actual suppliers are
represented. CCR concerns every predicate collection of actual concreta, not
merely observable, finite or experimentally isolated networks.

## A discriminating audit of the borrowed-reality bridge

Borrowed reality is `45890c84-1d33-4273-b0ea-0f75bfd67a33`,
`NoWhollyBorrowedCollection`; supplier concreteness is
`b28f3a70-fbb4-4c61-b9ac-099d97477f4d`,
`ActualSuppliersOfConcretaConcrete`. `closedRootedOfBorrowed` already certifies
their joint implication to CCR. Two additional inspection findings need to be
kept distinct from that registered theorem:

1. A supply-closed collection is required to consist entirely of concreta.
   If one member has an actual abstract supplier, it cannot belong to any such
   collection: closure would require the abstract supplier to be concrete.
   Thus this borrowed-reality predicate is not an unrestricted prohibition on
   wholly borrowed *mixed* collections. `Control.abstractSupplier` exploits
   this scope restriction. An abstract supplier for each concrete member can
   make the collection test vacuous without showing that supplier unborrowed.
2. Under the concrete-supplier bridge, supply-closed and ground-closed
   collections coincide. For a member x of such a collection, every actual
   supplier is concrete by the bridge; conversely an actual concrete supplier
   is an actual supplier. The two no-root tests then coincide classically.
   Consequently borrowed reality and CCR are classically equivalent under
   this bridge. This converse is a proposed definition-unfolding deduction,
   **not a new Lean-certified equivalence**. It supplies a precise follow-up
   theorem obligation; this slice changes no proof files.

This does not make a conceptual argument for borrowed reality worthless. It
does prevent counting it as independent corroboration merely because it has a
different predicate name. Its substantive rationale must discriminate the
rootless network from a source-grounded one. Debt or transmission language
alone cannot do so: a positive fixed point of mutually constrained values is a
mathematical solution, whereas a rule that admissible reality must be generated
from independent inputs chooses a stronger semantics. Why that generative rule
applies to existence is the open warrant question.

The universal supplier bridge also excludes harmless abstract co-grounds.
A mixed account might include a concrete sustaining source and an abstract
structural constraint; it can satisfy CCR while violating the bridge. The
existing abstract-supplier control proves neither that all mixed accounts fail
CCR nor that abstract grounders are impossible. A potentially weaker bridge
would require every nonempty rootless concrete closed collection to be
supply-closed, or some suitable supply-closed subcollection to be extractable.
Those are proposed bridge shapes, not registered premises or independent
warrant: the former still forbids precisely the external abstract escape and
the latter needs a proof of the extraction. Parent reassessment should select
one only if a substantive account motivates it.
