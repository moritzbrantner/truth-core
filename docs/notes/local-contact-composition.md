# Local composition scope in the contact regress

The definition-use audit of `DEF-CONTACT-OR-CONTINUITY`,
`DEF-ONE-MADE-OF-ALL` and `DEF-LOCAL-BODILY-MOTION` found a consequential
strength distinction in `ARIST-PHYS-VII-1-LOCAL-REGRESS-FROM-CONTACT`.
Its exact original conclusion denies an injective infinite chain of the selected
`LocalMovedMoverLink`. It does not assert well-foundedness or exclude finite
cycles. The old `TouchingChainFormsOneReading` instead requires a whole for
every injective touching chain, including chains unrelated to those links.
The existing typed source comment already identifies that generalization.

`STMT-LOCAL-TOUCHING-MOVED-MOVER-CHAIN-FORMS-ONE` is an analyst weaker sibling.
Its domain includes the same injectivity and touching conditions plus actual
local moved-mover links on each adjacent pair. It uses the **same** original
`IsLocal`, `MovedIn`, `MovesIn`, `MovesPrimarily`, `Touches` and `ComposedOf`.
No new physical contact, unity or causal relation is substituted. In particular,
“one made of all” stays primitive rather than silently becoming a Mathlib fusion.
The sole-premise scope theorem registers the global-to-local strength order.

The new exact-target argument retains contact, aggregate traversal and
impossibility of infinite traversal as three separate premises. Contact makes
the hypothetical link chain a touching chain; restricted composition supplies a
whole of that chain; its traversal contradicts the unchanged impossibility
premise. Only composition scope changes. This adapts the existing proof's typed
inference directly: applying its frozen stronger theorem to a weaker premise
would be invalid. The old source reading, attribution and frozen Challenge are
preserved, and no claim of philosophical necessity is made from the old proof's
particular interface. No new source claim is introduced.

The strictness control uses `Nat × Bool` with two tracks. The false track has
actual local successor moved-mover links and an exported whole. The true track
is another injective touching chain and has no whole. Local composition holds
for every qualifying chain because its links force every member onto the false
track. Consequently global composition fails. Population and the actual
relevant infinite chain are exported in the checked proposition, rather than
satisfying the weaker premise by removing all local chains. This controls only
the stipulated predicates: it is neither a real spatial aggregate nor a claim
that an infinite physically moving chain is possible. It does not assert the
traversal and impossibility premises, which together with contact and local
composition would rule out its exhibited chain.

The prior Physics use review is retained: natural/forced and self/passive
classifications are explicit nested OR premises; the literal per-se/per-accidens
conclusion has an existing countermodel to promotion into a per-se mover. The
per-accidens-to-per-se bridge is separately OPEN. Physical-to-broad reduction
scope is another separate OPEN bridge with its own omission controls. VII.5
alteration/growth scaling stays a labelled opaque transcription and supplies no
numerical dynamics. This batch does not reinterpret those propositions or claim
to complete the whole Greek corpus audit.

Remaining burdens are the intended physical instantiation, actual contact or
continuity, unity/aggregate construction, and the source's quantitative traversal
argument. The local scope theorem establishes no such inputs. The existing
local-regress conclusion has no root path in the inspected context, so this is a
weaker conditional sufficient configuration rather than root closure. Exact
frontier comparison is reported in the PR and CI; no reduction is inferred just
from registering a weaker premise.
