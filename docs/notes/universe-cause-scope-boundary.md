# The universe has no cause versus no existing cause

`UniverseHasExistingCause o F w` requires a candidate causer that exists at the
selected world. `UniverseUncaused o F w` denies every candidate edge to the
selected whole. The latter genuinely contradicts the former, so its original
full-strength brute-universe alternative is preserved. However it is stronger
than the exact no-existing-cause complement on generic `Ontic`: the signature
itself does not require the endpoints of a causal edge to exist.

The audit reuses the EXISTING `CausesHaveActualEndpointsAt` policy rather than
adding actuality to `Ontic` by definition. A sole-premise checked strength
registration restricts it to `WholeCausersExist`: actuality of causers of this
selected whole only. Neither actuality of unrelated effects nor all worlds is
needed by the selected positive argument. A candidate edge plus this weak
policy yields the EXACT old `UniverseHasExistingCause`; the existence conjunct
is no longer silently read into an unconstrained causal predicate.

## The decisive fork

Eight checked results expose these consequences:

- The original no-edge position implies exact no-existing-cause; the converse
  fails on the generic signature in the absent-edge control.
- Under explicit excluded middle, either an existing cause exists, no candidate
  edge exists, or at least one candidate edge exists but no existing cause does.
  These are exact logical cases under ONE fixed profile, not a census of
  naturalist and theist theories.
- The third case implies exact no-existing-cause and contradicts the weak
  selected-whole-causer actuality policy. With that policy supplied, the usual
  existing-cause OR no-cause fork is checked. Coverage therefore exposes the
  actuality assumption instead of presuming the two named accounts exhaustive.

The exact matching-scope negative is an unrefuted formal alternative in addition
to the original stronger brute-universe account. The latter stays unchanged as
a position; denying or refuting it alone cannot remove the generic third case.
No philosophical premise, independent causal explanation, actual temporal
beginning, necessity, concrete cause, distinction from the whole or God is
inferred from these scope lemmas.

## Controls and applicability

The eight-result bundle exports three two-role interpretations. The spacetime
whole is populated in all three:

1. `absent`: the candidate causer is absent, the whole exists, and the candidate
   has an edge to that whole. Exact no-existing-cause holds but no-edge fails.
   The weak actuality policy fails. This is deliberately OUTSIDE the intended
   causal-actuality policy. It is a countermodel to an unguarded inference over
   generic `Ontic`, not a claim that nonactual individuals can really cause.
2. `actual`: the SAME candidate edge and whole are retained and both roles
   exist. Existing-cause and the original full endpoint policy hold.
3. `uncaused`: both candidates exist but there are no causal edges. The full
   endpoint policy holds vacuously; the old no-edge position and absence of an
   existing cause are both exported.

Causation keeps its prior efficient/dependence-or-conservation-of-existence
sense; it is not changed into explanation, temporal succession, mere parthood
or an event causing the universe's beginning. No transitivity, asymmetry or
well-foundedness is assumed. Ordinary productive examples have actual causers;
that interpretation is explicitly expressed by the reused actuality policy.
Applicability to reality and any claim that this whole has a cause remain OPEN.
