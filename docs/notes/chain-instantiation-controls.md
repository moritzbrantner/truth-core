# A chain's instantiation is a further question (#704)

The checked target `ChainInstantiation.Controls` distinguishes three questions:
can a structure satisfy its equations; are the claimed individuals and links
actual; and do the actual grounds adequately explain the target? The target
answers only the first question and proves two precise limits on an offered
answer to the second. It supplies no source attribution or metaphysical verdict.

## Exact comparison and reuse

`Ontic Unit Nat` reuses the existing candidate-domain/existence distinction.
`chain present` has one complete candidate world and world-local edges
`y = x + 1 ∧ present y ∧ present x`. Thus no edge has an absent endpoint.
The index is neither elapsed time nor a distinct real entity just because it
is a natural number. `finite N` exists exactly at indices at most N;
`infinite` exists at every index; `empty` nowhere.

For every N, `SameWindow` observes presence at indices ≤ N and edges only
between pairs of those indices. `finite N` and `infinite` agree exactly on
that record. Both include zero. The finite interpretation has an existing
uncaused-by-this-relation boundary N, no existing N+1, and only bounded existing
indices. The infinite one is populated everywhere and non-well-founded,
using the imported `successorChainNotWellFounded`, with accessibility
restricted from guarded edges to the already certified successor relation.
A query about the boundary's *unrestricted* predecessors distinguishes them;
the theorem makes no claim about that stronger observation map.

Both `empty.ExistsAt ()` and `infinite.ExistsAt ()` satisfy the **same**
`LocalTransmission` equation `∀ n, present n ↔ present (n+1)`. The empty
interpretation has no actual stage even though its candidate type is nonempty;
the populated interpretation has an actual zero in its formal interpretation.
These are not distinct inference rules tailored to their conclusions.

The earlier [escaping-source control](../../TruthCore/Causality/GroundingLimits.lean)
already separates finite inductive grounding from its pointwise limit; this
change does not repeat that theorem or assert that all actual grounding must
be inductive. The new observation includes *population and guarded edges*,
rather than just the activity/source assignments of finite approximations.
`LocalObservationSelectsSolution` selects a global mathematical assignment
from an observed zero **plus** a globally valid local law. It does not warrant
that global law's real applicability or instantiate its domain in reality.

## A proposed existence bridge and strongest counterexample

A serious bridge starts with an independently warranted actual episode E
(e.g. the presently occurring measurement or judgment), a defended realization
map to a concrete bearer, and a reason that this bearer exists in the selected
actual world. Reuse #536's `ThoughtOccurs`, `EveryThoughtHasSubject`,
`EveryBearerIsConcrete`, and `EveryBearerExistsAt`; their meanings and omission
controls remain in `TruthCore/Logic/CogitoConcrete.lean`. This is empirically
or metaphysically substantive: a real episode must be distinguished from a
merely described or simulated occurrence; a bearer/concrete realization and
its actual-world identification require justification. Its conclusion is a
concrete actual inhabitant, **not** the actuality of an infinite series.

A further causal extension must warrant real relata and the particular
world-local relation at each claimed link. A plausible empirical route uses
repeatable interventions, stable mechanisms and independently identified
bearers within a measured range. This supports those links conditionally on
the measurement/causal identification theory. It does not silently establish
all links beyond the range or a universal existential predecessor principle.
The strongest counterexample to extrapolating that finite evidence is the
*populated* `finite N` control: it preserves every stated observation and every
observed edge, yet the next stage is absent and the boundary has no incoming
edge. The counterexample grants observed reality; it does not evade the
question by making all observed individuals fictitious. Conversely, the
infinite interpretation defeats inference from the same finite record to
termination. Neither construction is evidence which history is actual.

If one separately defends the globally applicable equation on actual
existence plus actual stage zero, the earlier observation-selection theorem
propagates presence to every index. This conditional success has a clear
price: its globally applicable existence equation is precisely the added
bridge, not something established by checking finite prefixes. Nor does it
show those links are full grounds or adequate explanations.

## Strongest replies and remaining fork

The positive infinitist uniformity/parity case is preserved in the existing
[§ Strongest objection and consequential fork](unique-feedback-explanation-311.md)
from #693. It has not been reduced to consistency here. If actual full local
grounds are independently warranted and the aim is memberwise explanation,
continuity of explanatory practice supports the rival conditionally; no
initial deposit of an explanatory quantity has been shown necessary. The
remaining burdens are actual instantiation, local sufficiency and uniformity
of the relevant kind of explanation across levels.

An infinitist may instead posit a brute obtaining structure. That is a
conditional memberwise answer if its local grounds really suffice, but it
retains unexplained global actuality. It is not contradictory by definition.
A foundationalist who merely posits a brute necessary entity retains an
analogous actuality/explanatory burden; only an independently defended
intrinsic sufficient reason can break that parity.

Denying that there is one further whole-series entity avoids creating a
chain-object solely to apply PSR. Whether the obtaining facts nevertheless
require a further explanation remains disputed. A whole-series explanation
principle is separate from the already prior instantiation demand. The denial
does not establish actual infinity, and the demand does not by itself refute
it. #677/#698's weak explanation-token coverage likewise cannot supply adequacy
or actual individuals merely by adding explanatory tags.
