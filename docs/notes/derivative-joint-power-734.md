# Derivative joint power and the supplier-cut obstruction (#734)

The question is whether an actually exercising complete group of derivative
powers needs intrinsic supply, rather than merely a graph source or a selected
solution of transfer equations. The new checked result is a **contributor-cut
obstruction**, instantiated on the exact #634/#733 groups. A source in every
complete group and existential graph grounding of every event do not provide
an inductive derivation supplying every contributor. This does not refute the
strongest globally coherent/selected rival. The actual exercise-to-supply and
physical-to-existential bridges remain **OPEN**. Intended and actual root delta:
zero premises added, removed or discharged.

## One context and the meanings being compared

`Supply h I e` is a proposed inductive explanatory standard: an independently
specified intrinsic contribution is supplied; the effect of an admitted
supplier record is supplied once **every productive contributor** is supplied.
For actual use `h` must be the restriction to exercised supplier records, as
in #733's `Exercise.exercisedGroups`. In the rooted and source-free exercise
controls every record is admitted and exercised, so no alternative is silently
promoted to exercise. `I` means underived contribution in this selected exercise
respect; it is not defined as absence of graph predecessors. A power can be
intrinsic in the selected respect while its bearer, history or existence has
further dependencies. Nothing identifies this predicate with being God.

Borrowing itself is **not** defined by this inductive standard. `LocalPower`
allows an event's active contribution precisely when it is intrinsic or one
supplier group has all contributors active. It selects neither an inductive
nor a coinductive solution. It is also distinct from event occurrence,
`Exercise.exercised`, and scenario sufficiency. All are checked separately in
the rooted model. The occurrence predicates in the fixtures are chosen to
match local power; no general theorem identifies occurrence and productive
power.

`AtContext h clock respect t r` explicitly requires each supplier's effect
and contributors to have the same selected exercise instant t and respect r.
The A/B/C controls use one Unit instant and one Unit respect throughout, with
separate event tokens and the existing bearer map. This represents a single
context; it proves no real simultaneity, duration, event individuation or
productive interpretation. Nat indexes dependencies, not successive times.
The #711 alternating-respect circle is retained as a **separate** boundary;
it is not relabelled as a same-respect motion cycle. Its sustaining relation
was independently supplied, and the imported temporal certificate includes
`Reciprocal.consume`, which actually consumes the temporal coverage theorem.

An enabling condition needs to **occur**, not exercise productive power in
the contribution's respect. `Supply`, `LocalPower`, `Trap` and `Escape` therefore
use `contributors`, not `members`. Exercise coherence still requires the whole
contributor/enabler union to occur. `EnablerControl` has source 0, instrument
response 1 and enabling event 2: all occur, 1 has Supply from 0, yet 2 has no
Supply in that respect. Requiring Supply of 2 merely because it is a member
would impose a stronger unexplained premise. Classifying oxygen, a switching
condition or circuit geometry as enabler rather than productive contributor
is itself application-specific. Deleting a contributor in the rooted
indispensability test still retains **all** enablers through #733's definition,
even when roles overlap; the rooted fixture happens to have none.

A hand operating an instrument offers a limited conceptual illustration: the
instrument's present contribution depends on an actualizer, rather than on
its having been manufactured. A series-switch load with its supply and
background fixed illustrates conjunctive requirements. Closed switches with
a dead supply, an unplugged amplifier with an input signal, and an enabled
but unpowered instrument are near misses. Stored energy, independently acting
agents, redundant routes and previous manufacturing are counterexamples to
classifying every causally influenced item as derivative in this respect.
These are ordinary scope illustrations, not empirical laws, source attributions
or evidence that actual productive explanation must be inductive.

## The new consequence

A `Trap h I D` has no intrinsic member, and **every** admitted supplier for a
target in D still needs at least one contributor in D. Contributors outside D,
including an intrinsic source shared by all groups, are allowed. Induction on
Supply proves that no member of D has Supply. The premise tests all supplier
alternatives, not just a chosen graph path or one bad branch. Allowing an
alternative whose complete contributor set escapes D defeats the obstruction.
No global acyclicity, asymmetry, transitivity, WF or finite branching is assumed.
Immediate joint contribution is not asserted transitive. The proof uses
inductive derivation structure, not causal authority conferred by a name.
Infinite contributor sets and unbounded branch depths are permitted; calling
this a uniformly finite-depth or finite-cardinality explanation would overstate
its type. With a nonempty contributor set for every supplier, each derivation
also yields an existing `FiniteCausalPath` to an intrinsic contribution. This
is a necessary one-path consequence, not a converse or graph-source theorem.

`Escape h I A` says every nonempty subset of active contributions contains an
intrinsic member or a supplier record whose entire contributor set escapes
it. Under `PoweredGroups` (contributors of admitted supplier records for active
targets are active), all active contributions have Supply **iff** Escape.
The recorded schema also retains LocalPower as explicit comparison background;
the equivalence proof itself only needs PoweredGroups. Neither a global weakest
metaphysical principle nor inclusion-minimality of these backgrounds is claimed.
This exact equivalence exposes the cost of the no-self-contained-borrowing
principle: within this profile it is as strong as selecting inductive Supply.
It is an OPEN philosophical commitment, not an independent proof of selection.

The initial unqualified draft equivalence was rejected before certification.
An existential local equation selects **some** active alternative; it does not
make all merely admitted alternatives active. `AdmissionOmission` has an active
true-event self-supporting group and a second admitted group needing the
inactive false event. LocalPower and Escape hold, but Supply fails and
PoweredGroups fails. Making false intrinsic instead supplies true through the
external alternative. The revised claim explicitly retains supplier-power
coherence and contributor-only supply; its corrected Challenge precedes the
completed corrected proof. No accepted Challenge or source claim was weakened.
