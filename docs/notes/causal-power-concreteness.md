# Owner-proposed causal-power and activity concreteness commitment

**Historical status.** This note preserves the original intake and the later
2026-11-06 approval recorded below. That approval remains legacy metadata used
by the existing frontier computation; it supplies no independent philosophical
warrant. Current research follows the
[systematic research loop](../systematic-corpus-loop.md), assessing reasons,
applicability and rivals through reasoned provisional judgments. This
qualification changes no approval record or computed closure.

The owner's working proposition is: “only concrete things have power and can do
things, abstract things cannot do or cause things.” This note registers its
causal sense as an OPEN framework input, not a source attribution, self-evidence
marking or proof of metaphysical truth. “Do things” here concerns causal activity,
not every sense of mathematical operation, logical implication or explanation.

## Exact proposition and classification policy

`CausalPowerConcreteness.OwnerCommitmentAt o concrete hasCausalPower w` is:

1. `∀ x, hasCausalPower w x → concrete x`;
2. `∀ x y, o.Causes w x y → concrete x`.

`concrete : B → Prop` and `hasCausalPower : W → B → Prop` are supplied
independently. The second clause reuses the existing outgoing `Ontic.Causes`
relation; neither clause proves the other. In particular, cause-to-power is not
assumed to transfer between them. Defining the commitment does not define
concrete status as possession of power and does not prove the commitment.

Abstractness is also supplied independently. The explicit policy
`AbstractConcreteDisjoint abstract concrete` says `∀ x, abstract x → ¬ concrete x`.
This policy is REQUIRED by the intended profile described below. The capacity
clause excludes abstract powers and the activity clause excludes outgoing
abstract causation. No exhaustiveness or
complement definition is assumed. These are logical consequences of assumed
clauses, not separately certified research results. No theorem/result is added.

The selected-world statement is not automatically boxed or asserted at all
worlds. No existence predicate is inferred from either standalone input. IntendedProfile
requires endpoint actuality explicitly as an interpretation guard below. No conditional
about an absent candidate's merely possible powers is silently inserted.

## Required operative contract for intended P2-B/P3 profiles

The initial intake left disjointness optional and the exercise-to-power dictionary
unspecified. The owner rejected that treatment: free labels do not enforce the
intended abstract/concrete causal distinction. That approach is superseded.

`IntendedProfile W B` now requires:

- `underlying : BruteFoundationBoundary.Profile W B`, preserving its exact
  ontic existence, concrete predicate and world-relative `grounds` relation;
- independently supplied `abstract` and `hasCausalPower` predicates;
- `disjoint`: abstract status excludes concrete status;
- `powerExists`: a bearer actually possessing power at w exists at w;
- `causalEndpoints`: both endpoints of a represented causal exercise exist at w,
  without imposing inequality;
- `exerciseHasPower`: every actual outgoing `underlying.ontic.Causes w x y`
  exhibits thin `hasCausalPower w x`;
- `powerConcrete`: the owner's power-to-concrete input at each represented world.

The exercise-to-power field is an INTERPRETATION WELL-FORMEDNESS requirement.
It cannot be proved for arbitrary predicates. Exercising causation manifests
at least thin causal power; this does not demand an accessible intervention
response difference, full intrinsic control or reasons-responsive agency. The
power-to-concrete field is the substantive owner framework input. Neither
field is a proof of metaphysical applicability. Together with disjointness they
rule out outgoing abstract causation in every well-formed intended profile.
A freely labelled abstract causer cannot be admitted without violating a required
field. No theorem is added merely to apply these fields.

`P2At p w` requires this wrapper and is exactly the existing
`EveryContingentGroundedAt p.underlying w`. `P3At p w` requires the SAME wrapper
and is `WellFounded (p.underlying.grounds w)`. Thus intended investigations cannot
enter this interface with an unconstrained causal/classification interpretation.
Both premises still concern the SAME old grounding relation; neither is included
in the semantic contract. The existing conditional necessary-concrete theorem
can be applied to `p.underlying` once its existing population/P2/P3 premises are
independently provided. No new theorem duplicates that application.

This is an operative requirement for the intended interpretation, not a global
change to generic `Ontic`. Legacy mathematical profiles, frozen Challenges and
results remain unchanged. They count as intended profiles only after the
required dictionary and classification fields are supplied; naming their
predicates does not supply that evidence. The wrapper does not convert generic
causation to existential grounding or eliminate
intrinsic constitutive self-support. In particular, a null-preserving equation
or an underived condition is not automatically a thin causal power or its
concrete bearer.

## Meaning, examples and rivals

Power means ability to cause: a capacity can remain unexercised. The claim does
not require every concrete individual to have powers, and a passive recipient is
not classified as abstract. Concrete does not mean material, extended or located
in spacetime. An immaterial causal agent is permitted by the formulation.

An ordinary magnet's unexercised attraction capacity illustrates the distinction
between capacity and current activity; an operating magnet's causal interaction
illustrates the activity clause. These are orientation examples, not newly
verified empirical evidence or an analysis of causation by response differences.
A number or abstract structural description is an intended candidate for the
inert abstract classification, not an independently proved instance of that
classification. A concrete inscription or someone's thinking about a number is
not identified with the number. Incoming causal dependence of an abstract object
is NOT prohibited by the outgoing constraints.

The strongest opponent can dispute whether abstract structures have genuine
causal efficacy, distinguish nonproductive mathematical explanation from causing,
or reject exhaustive abstract/concrete classification. The formulation leaves
the classifications independent and does not refute such opponents by defining
causal power as concrete. No transitivity, asymmetry, irreflexivity,
well-foundedness, causal sufficiency or causal necessity is assumed. Independent
warrant for the owner premise and its actual applications remains OPEN.

## Bearing on P2-B

The commitment supplies concreteness **conditional on causal power or actual
causation**. It does not supply a cause, a supporter or an adequate explanation
for every contingent concrete individual's existence. An actual cause-in-esse
can use the direct activity clause if its identification with the chosen
`Ontic.Causes` relation is independently justified. Even with the REQUIRED causal interpretation contract, a constitutive supporter
requires its own support-to-cause or support-to-power interface; not every
constitutive/explanatory relation is declared causal. Endpoint actuality is REQUIRED for intended-profile powers/exercises;
its applicability is not proved for arbitrary raw Ontic interpretations.
Existential PSR, grounding coverage and well-foundedness remain OPEN.

Thus even accepting the owner's proposition does not derive
`BruteFoundationBoundary.EveryContingentGroundedAt`. It addresses the concrete
classification of a causally established source, while #545's existence and
existential-support questions and #311/#367's foundation question remain.
No root premise is discharged, no frozen Challenge changes, and no metaphysical
truth is established by this intake. It adds an explicit open framework input and the required intended-interpretation
contract;
existing root-route premises are unchanged.

## Adopted sense, floor approval and derivation (#1103, 2026-11-06)

- **Adopted sense of concrete.** The owner fixed "concrete" as having esse in re
  (`DEF-CONCRETE-ESSE-IN-RE`), with abstract as its contradictory
  (`DEF-ABSTRACT-NO-ESSE-IN-RE`); see `docs/notes/concrete-abstract.md`.
  Concreteness is therefore no longer a free label for intended
  interpretations. It is not defined through causal power, so the principle
  stays substantive.
- **Floor item.** The owner approved the WEAK version as a floor item
  ([decision](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/truth-core/issues/1103#issuecomment-6683616439)):
  efficient causes (`Ontic.Causes`) and bearers of causal power are concrete.
  The strong version, covering causes in every sense (formal, final, exemplar,
  explanatory), is not approved. Four rivals are recorded at full strength,
  each `alternative_to` the item:
  - the generic denial `STMT-SOME-CAUSE-LACKS-ESSE-IN-RE`;
  - productive Platonic Forms;
  - Lewis's events as sets;
  - a causal reading of Baker's cicadas.
  In the existing computed floor status, this approval supersedes the intake's
  "OPEN" label. It does not supply independent warrant or settle applicability.
- **Derivation.** `CAUSAL-POWER-CONCRETENESS-FROM-OPERARI` derives the item
  from operari sequitur esse (ST I q.75 a.2 co.; q.25 a.1 co.) and
  `STMT-ACTUALITY-ONLY-INSOFAR-AS-BEING` (q.4 a.1 ad 3).
  - It is not circular: causation and power are primitives, and concreteness
    is defined from `InAct`/`esse`.
  - It is not a transcription, but it is short: each clause composes one
    reading with the act-to-being reading.
  - All three premises are open. The act-to-being reading is a stated premise
    on the cheapest root route. The derivation therefore does not establish
    the item from established premises. Its recorded owner approval supplies the
    current computed floor status, not independent philosophical warrant.
