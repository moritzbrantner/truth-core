# Ultimate adequacy: joint supply does not select one least foundation (#735)

The new discriminator fails in **both directions**, even after granting the
existing inductive contributor standard. Supplying every actual productive
contribution does not require one least membership-graph foundation: two
intrinsic contributions can each be indispensable for the same target. Having
one least graph foundation does not supply the target: the exact #634 infinite
branch still needs an unsupplied contributor. Neither result establishes
complete metaphysical explanation. The immediate additional burden is now
clearer: an independently defended **unity** principle is needed beyond joint
supply, while independently defended **productive applicability** is needed
before treating Supply itself as actual explanation. Intrinsic existence and
epistemic entitlement are further questions.

It supplies #735's consequential failed-discriminator/countermodel outcome;

## One question, one interpretation policy

`UltimateAdequacy.groups` and `exercise` reuse the existing Groups/Exercise
signature. Every comparison has Nat event candidates, Nat bearer candidates,
bearer map `id`, target **event2**, Bool×Bool scenarios, all scenarios allowed,
actual `(true,true)`, all records exercised, and one Unit time/respect. There
are no enabling events in these particular fixtures; the earlier #733/#734
enabler controls are retained authorities, not silently replaced by a demand
that every enabling condition have productive Supply. All Nat candidates
actually occur. Thus these are **infinite populated domains**, including the
single and plural cases; “finite” below refers only to the displayed target
supply derivations, never finite total population.

The shared question is: **what supplies the actual contribution of event2 in
this context?** For a reality-facing application that event must be identified,
and its bearer/power interpretation defended. This is not silently changed
into “why does every bearer exist?”, “why is there anything?”, “why does this
whole law/arrangement obtain?”, or “why should an agent trust a law?”. Those
are additional questions with explicit bridges. A group indexes antecedent
records; no group substance or extra whole-series object is introduced.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Single | contributor `{0}`, effect `g+1`; intrinsic0 | Every event has Supply, and0 is least. Target2 has a depth-one joint derivation. |
| Plural | contributors `{0,1}`, effect `g+2`; intrinsic0 and1 | Every event has Supply. Removing either contributor from target2's group permits failure while retaining the other. Both are graph sources, so no least exists. |

For each configuration `Background` proves actual occurrence of **every**
event, exercise coherence, sufficiency across the supplied scenarios, actual
LocalPower, PoweredGroups, same-context contributions, and equality of the
raw membership edge with the Exercise ontology's actual causal edge. Because
all candidates actually occur, no absent isolated candidate is counted as an
actual source. The existing actual-event subtype remains available; no second
graph engine is introduced.

Off scenarios support deletion tests. Single/chain occurrence is the first
bit; plural occurrence is bit1 at0, bit2 at1, and their conjunction elsewhere;
branch occurrence is always true at0 and otherwise the first bit. An early
background draft demanded LocalPower at **every** off scenario while fixing
intrinsic contributions; that would force intrinsic exercise even when its
scenario switch was off. It was rejected before solution work. The delivered
background asserts LocalPower at the **selected actual context**. It does not
smuggle a modal law about every off scenario. Scenario sufficiency and deletion
counterexamples still quantify over the entire declared scenario space.

The plural case therefore improves on #707's graph-only plurality. Its two
root contributions really are each indispensable under the specified
sufficiency test, and the inductive Supply proof needs them both. Completeness
of that inductive derivation neither merges their identities nor creates an
upstream edge between them. No claim that these scenario implications are
already productive causation is needed for the countermodel.

The four combinations concern two **formal** tests. They are not four
exhaustive worldviews; adequate explanation is not one of the tests. In
particular a least node is least under finite membership ancestry, not an
established least *explanatory* foundation.

## Fifth rival, necessity and contingent effects

A necessary or brute **impersonal foundation** can use the very same
single-source contribution account: an actual natural power/structure supplies
the target through its law. It can propose a real nature that explains its
own actuality, or accept primitive ultimate actuality while explaining the
local effect. The theistic intrinsic-source proposal can similarly appeal to
an actual independently characterized source rather than efficient
self-production. These are positive rival answers to the same event2 question,
not “unknown” substituted for one side's account. Their real-nature explanation,
actuality and productive application remain OPEN for **both**.

There is no personal/impersonal label theorem: the exercise signature contains
no intelligence or divine-identity predicate. Inability of this signature to
discriminate them does not prove equality of their full explanatory merits.
The fifth account is compared through its shared actual source structure and
the explicit modal extensions, not falsely claimed to be another graph shape.
No single intrinsic power in a productive respect establishes that its bearer
has underived or necessary existence.

`modal k b` supplies a **separate** universally accessible Bool-world ontology
on the same Nat bearer candidates, designated actualfalse. All candidates exist
there and actual causes are exactly the previously checked actual graph. At
the other world, b=true retains every candidate; b=false retains only the
intrinsic-contribution candidates. Thus every candidate is necessary in the
first extension, while target2 is contingent in the second, for **each**
configuration. These are checked actual-data-preserving extensions. They do
not preserve arbitrary cross-world exercise/causation claims, make worlds
into scenarios, establish real metaphysical accessibility, or identify every
bearer's existence with its exercise. The id bearer map is a stipulated
one-event-per-bearer policy in these fixtures, not a universal identity thesis.

A full reason might include a contingent selector/enabling fact, or a defended

## Philosophical bridge, strongest case, and sources

The strongest unity case says that genuinely independent contributions cannot
explain their coordinated obtaining without a further unifying reason.
The strongest plural reply supplies each intrinsic contributor's nature and
a genuine joint law, arguing that their compatibility need not be another
produced entity. The new plural model grants Supply and genuine relative
indispensability, so **even an exercise-to-Supply bridge alone leaves this
rival alive**. To infer one least foundation one must additionally justify
why those sufficient independent contributions have a common upstream
foundation, or independently establish an individuation/unity argument.
Shared downstream effects do not supply that premise. Naming the entire
cooperation a group neither supplies a unifier nor proves it unnecessary.

The source-free/global reply can propose an independently obtaining whole-law
or coinductive organization rather than only unresolved local on/off equations.
#736's unique beginningless signal remains a strong mathematical control for
that reply; it has no proved productive interpretation or law-actuality
explanation. #734's selected-on rooted rival likewise preserves no-Supply.
Restriction to its unique allowed scenario must not inherit the broader
scenario deletion witnesses. A brute account accepts ultimate explanatory
debt; an intrinsic global account claims to pay it and owes a real nature/rule
argument. Neither is contradictory merely through lack of Supply, until
actual productive applicability of that standard is defended.

The **immediate OPEN repair** is therefore two-stage, with no claim of a
mathematically weakest metaphysical norm: independently show that a particular
actual nature/full group makes this target's exercise obtain, with direction,
all relevant contributors/enablers and individuation; then justify any demand
that such an account have **one** least foundation rather than plural adequate
sources. An existence-as-such conclusion further needs the power-to-esse
bridge. None follows from source count, a modal necessity table, copied truth,
local scenario implication or the absence of a proof term under Supply.

An ordinary two-input powered control or two independently exerted forces on
a supported load motivates distinguishing joint sufficiency from either input
alone. A missing input, background restraint, disconnected actuator or
unpowered supply is a near miss. These illustrations do not establish which
inputs are intrinsic in reality or universalize an instrumental-power rule to
the existence of their bearers. No undisputed concrete example of intrinsic
explanation of existence itself is supplied. Both a proposed divine nature
and a proposed necessary natural structure remain disputed applications.
