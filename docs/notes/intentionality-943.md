# Intentionality, error and the physicalist rival (#943)

of **what a judgment says** to a truthful report of the thinker’s own affection.

new propositions are stipulated configurations, under the owner’s logic-first
direction of 2026-11-05. Historical attribution is deferred; no source or claim
records are added. This is conditional configuration progress; no premise of
the Catholic case is closed.

## Fixed semantics and certified constraint

`TruthCore.Alternatives.Intentionality943.Semantics` adds content, truth at a
situation, token occurrence and a distinct report predicate to the existing
`MindPhysicalism.Frame`. There is no accessibility relation, modal necessity,
temporal ordering, countability or finiteness assumption in the general theorem.
Content identity is not inferred from truth-condition equivalence. The frame's
target kind is categorical judgment/signal tokens in this comparison; tokening
all q.75 exercises into this kind remains an additional open bridge.

The inclusion-minimal core is:

1. `STMT-INTENTIONALITY943-ERROR`: a target token occurs with false content at
   its situation (**OPEN**, explicit framework input).
2. `STMT-INTENTIONALITY943-REPORT-CORRECT`: the qualified report of a token's
   affection is true where it occurs.
3. `STMT-INTENTIONALITY943-CONTENT-ONLY-REPORT`: the token's content truth is
   equivalent to the truth of that report.

`TruthCore.Results.Comparison.Intentionality943.errorCore` takes the erroneous
token and situation, derives report truth there, transports it through the
contested equivalence and contradicts that very content's falsity. This is a
typed consequence, not an opaque proposition whose consequent already is the
advertised conclusion. `selfOnlyAccuracy` isolates the universal-accuracy
consequence; `rejectAffectionOnly` composes the core and discharges the third
commitment. The general core requires neither excluded middle nor any axiom.
It excludes every superset in this same semantics, without choosing which
member a dissenter must abandon. It is not minimum cardinality across other
formulations.

All three single-member deletions have registered finite interpretations in
`corpus/models/intentionality943-*.json`, using the same definitions:

| Omitted member | Retained commitments and witness |
| --- | --- |
| Content/report identification | A false threat alarm occurs, its own occurrence-report is true, and its external content is false. |
| Error | A mental token still occurs; its content and qualified report both report its occurrence truly. |
| Qualified report correctness | The report itself is false where the signal occurs, so it can coincide with false external content. |

The carriers are the reused three-point occurrence type, Boolean kinds and
situations, and unit content. Enumeration certificates record these exact
bounds. The first interpretation additionally has a consumer function with one
response and one end. `PhysicalistErrorModelExists` certifies the joint witness,
not finiteness of every model and not a working biological implementation.
The general carriers may be empty, but the error premise forces an occurrence,
a content and a situation. Each deletion model independently retains an
occurring target token. There are no unreachable worlds or nonserial modal
frames here: situations are direct evaluations, and occurrence is not a
concrete-existence predicate. The inconsistent triple itself cannot have a
joint witness; each two-member subset does, so the conflict is not caused by an
inconsistent common causal background.

## Strongest consulted physicalist reply and retained gaps

Papineau can accept the error constraint while rejecting the report-only
restriction. His content is fixed through the consumer's function and its
relations to conditions beyond the vehicle. Occurrence identity and a physical
target kind do not make those conditions about the occurrence itself. The new
finite false-alarm interpretation reuses the existing `reductiveBackground`:
completeness, causal influence and distributive non-overdetermination hold in
the same frame as consumer content and error. Thus the new semantic premises
do not eliminate the reductive branch of `MindPhysicalism.physicalistFork`.
The original fork continues to require excluded middle and all three causal
premises; this model does not independently establish any of them.

The reply owes a noncircular natural account of selectional functions and
specific ends, unique content determination, its application to human abstract
thought, and the relation of content to conscious understanding. `Consumer`
leaves selection and proper-function success conditions primitive. The finite
model witnesses the formal compatibility claimed, not a reduction of those
primitives to physics or an empirical selectional history. Consciousness-first,
causal-input and nonetiological output theories remain serious alternatives;
Papineau discusses them, but the new core does not adjudicate them. Aquinas's
positive species doctrine also retains interpretation and applicability burdens.

`bodySharingBridgeOmission` combines this very causal/semantic interpretation
with the prior `pointTokened` and `pointPerSe` witnesses. It retains an operation
without bodily sharing while the physical-basis/body-sharing bridge fails.
Therefore the new content commitments do not supply that bridge.
`MindPhysicalism.noPerSeOperation` still needs it, besides exercise tokening,
excluded middle and the three causal premises. The exact earlier theorem was
inspected, not replaced by a report/content analogy.

Likewise `HumanIntellectIncorporeal.intellectIsNotBody` still requires universal
bodily cognition, bodily determinacy and intrinsic bodily interference.
Content truth is not `CanUnderstand`, and erroneous physical signals do not
prove that every bodily principle fails unrestricted cognition. No new theorem
connects this constraint to the intellect/will route (#355). Making such a
connection by renaming content, embodiment or representation would smuggle the
missing premise rather than prove it.

Rejected readings: species as invariably the primary object; every introspective
report infallible; every representation veridical; physical vehicle identity as
identity with the represented object; physicalism as report-only semantics;
Papineau's hypothetical alarms as ethological evidence; consumer functions as
actual lucky success; or a finite formal witness as metaphysical possibility.

## Premise comparison

Intended and actual root-path change: **none**. No root premise is added,
removed or independently established. Existing q.75 scope, determinacy,
interference, operation attribution and operation/existence commitments remain
open, as do the modern causal and vocabulary bridges. The new core tests three
explicit semantic commitments, derives the report-only restriction's rejection
from two, and preserves a joint physicalist rival interpretation. The consumer
configuration is explicitly stipulated and OPEN. No source claim is delivered;
consulted author findings do not establish the analyst universals.

The next discriminating research step is whether a specific naturalistic
consumer-function account can determine the relevant human content without
presupposing intentional ends, rather than demanding an unrestricted defense
of every primitive or repeating the existing q.75 omission models.
