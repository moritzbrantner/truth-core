## Positive package and the explanatory fork

Layer 1: posit substantive necessary ethical principles, actual nonmoral
features and their applicability conditions. Necessity concerns the conditional
or principle, not the actual existence of a suffering episode or child.
Mind independence does not mean absence of relations to contingent creatures.

Layer 2: distinguish two candidate interfaces. An instantiation account says
that an actual case satisfies the antecedent of a true normative principle,
so its derivative moral status follows. A robust-making account additionally
asserts metaphysical production/dependence of that status on those features.
Neither entailment nor covariance defines that extra relation. If it is causal,
the cause is the natural feature and the moral property may be an effect with
no further causal powers; there is no contradiction from causal impotence.
No temporal process, energy transfer, asymmetry or well-foundedness is inferred
merely from the word making. These remain OPEN interpretation/property burdens.

Layer 3: distinguish explaining a token moral fact given a norm from defending
the norm's content or explaining why it is fundamental. A true substantive
necessary principle can explain a token by instantiation without explaining
itself. Asking for the latter is a further explanatory standard, not exposing
a missing step in the former. The strongest rival reply is that necessary
ethical truths terminate explanation legitimately, while creatures contingently
instantiate them; there is no random matching event needing a cosmic arranger.
A comparative argument must justify rejecting that stopping point and apply
the same standard to divine goodness, wisdom and legitimate authority.

## Two discriminating cases

Pain/cruelty: fix a sentient patient, an agent deliberately causing intense
unwanted suffering solely for enjoyment, feasible refraining, and absence of
consent, therapeutic benefit or emergency rescue. A candidate basic norm says
that such suffering counts against causing it independently of endorsement.
The actual features plus that substantive norm supply a pro-tanto reason;
a decisive prohibition additionally needs the relevant priority/defeater rule.
Pain's badness for the patient alone does not quantify over agents' duties.
Pleasure, anesthesia and justified treatment variations test applicability,
not a change from necessary norms to contingent norms. This is a positive
normative explanation conditional on the norm, not a derivation from pain's
purely descriptive character.

Parental duty: fix equal moral worth of two children, equal immediate need,
one parent's established care responsibility for one child, ability to help,
and no replacement carer. Need supplies a welfare consideration; a separate
responsibility norm selects who owes care. Biology alone must not silently
supply that responsibility: adoption and abandonment are discriminating cases.
A necessary relational norm can apply to contingent adopted relationships
without treating duties as optional. A parent who lacks ability exposes a
scope/assistance question rather than proving the child has no moral worth.
When both children need rescue and only one can be saved, a pro-tanto special
duty does not yet fix decisive priority over greater urgency or survival benefit.
Standing to demand and all-things-considered priority remain separate questions
for #1508; no numeric or total-order decision procedure is assumed here.

These examples favor testing a structured natural base with patient, agent,
relationship, alternatives and defeaters. A single Boolean base loses exactly
the relations at issue. A second arbitrary mapping over that base would only
repeat the existing control, so no duplicate Lean library was introduced.

## Symmetric replacement comparison and proposed assessment

The robust realist can take impartial concern plus special responsibility as
basic, applying without a person's endorsement. That is a positive replacement
with specific content, not a rulebook whose authority depends on consent.
Its open burden is warrant for those principles and whether primitive making
adds explanation beyond true normative instantiation. This is not a known
inconsistency or a demonstrated explanatory failure.

The #1460 theistic proposal could connect creaturely vulnerability, goods,
intelligible ends and moral order through one wise source. For these cases it
still needs sentience-to-due-good, due-good-to-agent-reason, responsibility and
priority bridges, plus wise intentional authorship and rightful jurisdiction.
Bare creation, actuality goodness, or a common source supplies none by itself.
Using the same desired verdict to stipulate divine wisdom and then deriving
that verdict from wisdom would offer no independent comparative support.

Railton/Boyd offer objective interests/homeostatic human goods as affirmative
natural bases. They also need the independently defended impartial or normative
criterion and a responsibility account. Their lack of universal instrumental
incentive for the indifferent agent is not absence of moral obligation. None
of these three packages wins merely by having fewer named primitives.

Proposed overall provisional assessment: the positive necessary-norm package
answers the token-application objection conditional on substantive norms and
accurate case facts. Existing mapping controls do not defeat it. Comparative
superiority remains underdetermined: no independently warranted bridge here
shows that the theistic source explains the responsibility/priority content
better or that the realist stopping point is illegitimate. This judgment is
for challenge, not approved support. Independent norms, case applicability,
explanatory sufficiency and authority remain distinct open questions.

## Exact next test and reassessment

Hold pain's badness, equal patient worth, feasible help and impartial reasons
fixed. Compare biological parent without undertaken care, adoptive responsible
carer, and equally able stranger, first at equal urgency and then with the
stranger's child facing greater preventable harm. Ask each package to defend
one *independently motivated* responsibility principle and its defeat conditions
without encoding the desired priority into goodness, making or divine wisdom.
The discriminating result is either justified responsibility/priority from
antecedently defended commitments, a principled divergence, or a precise shared
unexplained principle. Agreement on verdicts alone is not explanatory victory.
A scoped formal comparison becomes useful once that concrete principle is
selected; instantiating an opaque Makes axiom now would only rename the gap.

Coordinator challenge should test whether this token/fundamental distinction
understates the making claim, whether adoptive responsibility is fairly held
fixed across packages, and whether any proposed comparative preference has an
independent warrant. Further primary reading is needed for historical fidelity;
it is not needed to retain this explicitly analyst test. Return for branch
reassessment now because selecting a defended responsibility argument is the
next substantive slice, while the complete proposed outcome awaits challenge.
