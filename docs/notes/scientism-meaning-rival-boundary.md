## Scoped versus universal scientism

`STMT-STRONG-SCIENTISM` is bound to `ScopedScientism E scope`, whose exact type requires empirical establishment only for established claims **in scope**. The original statement/definition explains universal strong scientism as the intended all-claims case, but its formal predicate does not itself supply coverage. The existing strong-dilemma and weak-demarcation results already keep their own-scope premises explicit and remain unchanged. No result here strengthens their conclusions or refutes scientism true but unestablished.

New independent OPEN coverage siblings distinguish:

1. `AllEstablishedInScope`: every established claim is in scope.
2. `NonEmpiricalWitnessesInScope`: every established claim failing empirical establishment is in scope.
3. `SomeNonEmpiricalWitnessInScope`: if some nonempirical established claim exists, some such claim is in scope. The transferred witness need not be the original one.

The first strengthens the second, which strengthens the third, by two checked one-premise implications. These are registered strength relations; none establishes its premise. `SCIENTISM-SCOPE-GUARDED-NONEMPIRICAL-NEGATIVE` uses the third together with the unchanged scoped norm to derive `NoNonEmpiricalEstablished`, the exact negative of the old existential under the SAME epistemic frame. This selected sufficient bridge avoids demanding that every possible counterexample lie in scope; no globally weakest logical formulation is claimed. No witness is created by stipulation: a hypothetical old existential supplies the antecedent, coverage transfers it, and the norm contradicts its failure of empirical establishment.

The old alternative endpoint now uses the exact negative sibling rather than generic scoped scientism. `SCIENTISM-SCOPE-RIVAL-CONTROLS` retains three populated Bool profiles: scoped scientism plus a nonempirical established claim outside scope and an empirical established claim inside; pointwise coverage with an actual nonempirical witness while all-established coverage fails; and existential transfer with an actual nonempirical witness while pointwise coverage fails. Thus both coverage strength steps are strict, and generic scoped scientism can coexist with nonempirical establishment. These are explicit predicate interpretations, not a full epistemology or an empirical calibration of knowledge.

Rejected: silently reading arbitrary `scope` as universal, taking scientism's own formulation in scope to put every nonempirical witness in scope, or using a selfapplication dilemma as an unconditional refutation. The independently applicable interpretation of established/empirical establishment and the intended scope remain OPEN; the new conditional exploration does not require defending them first.
