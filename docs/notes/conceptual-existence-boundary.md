# Conceptual existence boundary

Target: STMT-GOD-EXISTS (GOD_4), through an explicit predication/existence
bridge followed by identification of the existing exemplar with GOD_4.
This batch studies the first bridge; the second remains unencoded and open.
`GodExists IsGod` is `∃ g, IsGod g`, whereas this batch's conclusion explicitly
requires the independently supplied existence predicate as well as the profile.
No instantiation equates these declared types.

Analyst reconstruction, attribution deferred. This is a bounded strongest-case
schema for a perfect-object description: choose whichever nuclear features the
argument counts as perfections, including its alleged existence-entailing feature.
No theorem says that the profile really contains such a feature, or that the
feature really entails actuality. PHIL231 and Meinong are navigation leads only;
this is no new historical claim about either.

`Interpretation` separates a predicate on features (nuclear characterization),
a candidate/feature relation (exemplification), and two candidate predicates
(actuality and actual existence). These are explicit primitive semantic inputs.
A nuclear golden feature is a description; a candidate can exemplify it in a
fictional interpretation without existing. Physical gold exemplification is a
different possible interpretation and requires an explicit reality bridge.
Neither predication relation is transitive or asymmetric: its two argument
sorts differ. No structural metaphysical law is built into the signature.

The three sufficient commitments are Transfer, Anchor and ActualityExists.
Anchor is extension inclusion for at least one selected feature, not the
conclusion disguised as a Boolean flag. The proof chooses that feature, transfers
its predication to the selected candidate, obtains actuality, then existence,
and preserves the whole profile of the same witness. It is a modest conditional
bridge calculation, not independently warranted progress on premise truth.
The finite separation certificate is the discriminating research finding.
Each of the three premises is indispensable relative to the other two in this
signature, not uniquely minimal among all conceivable semantic commitments.
A still weaker candidate-local anchor would suffice; universal transfer or
universal existence-entailing predication is not claimed necessary in general.

The four fixture cases have a genuinely nonempty nuclear profile (`true`).
The positive case exemplifies all features and is actual and existent.
Dropping Transfer gives empty exemplification and existence. Dropping Anchor
leaves a fully exemplifying but nonactual nonexistent candidate. Dropping
ActualityExists leaves an actual fully exemplifying nonexistent candidate.
These interpretations use a nonempty candidate domain but empty actual-existence
extension. They refute the inference in this signature, not real-world possibility
or the historical theory. The last case challenges precisely the existence bridge,
not ordinary usage of the word actuality.

Reuse boundaries: `MeinongianActuality.noActualityWithoutBeing` and `controls`
already examine act/perfection-to-being premises. Their conclusion cannot be
imported as a theorem about this independent exemplification relation without

`IdentificationNeedsBridge` separates necessary existence from most-real
identification; `MostRealIsNecessary` remains universal, without an existential
witness. GOD naming/sense forks likewise distinguish a nominal predicate from
GOD_4. None supplies the missing identification here.

Remaining work requires a substantive interpretation of a proposed nuclear

identification premise. No root premise is discharged. No general object theory
