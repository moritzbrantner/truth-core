# Evil definitions and the force of an objection

This is an analyst reconstruction of the owner's requested comparison: make
worldview-dependent definitions explicit propositions, then determine what
objections remain available. It adds no historical attribution. Existing source
navigation remains in the O'Connor records and [the Rowe investigation](../rowe-evidential-evil.md).

## What already follows, and what does not

`POE-EXTERNAL-PREMISE-VS-EMOTIVISM` already proves that the selected man-made-evil
sentence is not true under **four jointly supplied premises**:

1. moral sentences lack truth-aptness;
2. lacking truth-aptness excludes truth;
3. badness ascriptions are moral;
4. this particular evil sentence ascribes badness.

The new proofs import that theorem. They do not silently extend it to natural
suffering, every evaluative sentence, contemporary expressivism, or Rowe's
whole argument. No objective-truth predicate occurs in this theorem. The
metasemantic conclusion `¬ Holds evilUtterance` does not assert the truth of a
negated moral sentence or establish that suffering does not exist.

The precise limitation called **moot** here is that this sentence cannot serve
as the critic's own true premise in a truth-preserving argument under those
same semantics. It does not establish God or falsehood of every rival worldview.

`STMT-BADNESS-AS-DISAPPROVAL-ANALYSIS` is a separate explicit proposition:

```
∀ φ, SaysBad φ ↔ ExpressesDisapproval φ
```

It is an analyst affective analysis, not a complete historical definition of
emotivism. It does not entail truthlessness. The truth-affective witness
satisfies it while the selected evil sentence is both true and truth-apt. This
keeps positive expressive meaning distinct from the additional no-truth-value
commitment. It does not establish robust mind-independent moral truth or a
complete quasi-realist theory, and does not resolve Frege–Geach.

## A checked fork with an explicit remainder

For one interpreted critic's selected sentence and one target Rowe frame:

| Diagnostic condition | Exact content |
| --- | --- |
| Own true premise | `Holds evilUtterance` in the critic's own interpretation |
| Target-internal challenge | `TheisticBeingExists s → ¬ PermissionConstraintReading s` |
| Affective protest | `ExpressesDisapproval evilUtterance` |
| Remaining use | Negation of all three preceding conditions |

OwnTruePremise is a truth condition, not an encoded act of assertion or assent.
The internal conditional is a target property, not a claim that the critic
actually deploys it. These are conditions of this diagnostic profile, not exclusive speech acts
or a list of all philosophical schools. Internal challenge and protest overlap
in the witness. The internal conditional can be vacuous in a general frame;
the survivor certificate explicitly supplies a narrow-theist witness.

`EVIL-USE-COVERAGE-WITH-REMAINDER` proves their disjunction using the **explicit
excluded-middle premise**. Its coverage is honest because the joint-negation
remainder remains in the type. It does not prove that remainder impossible.

`STMT-EVIL-NAMED-USE-COVERAGE` separately states the disjunction of the first
three conditions. This is an explicit open proposition. The conditional result
`EVIL-NAMED-USE-ELIMINATION` takes it as a premise: strict truthlessness excludes
the own-true-premise branch, leaving internal challenge or protest. Without
that coverage premise, `EVIL-EMOTIVIST-USE-FORK` leaves the explicit remainder
as well. The explicit remainder witness satisfies the four strict semantic
premises while named coverage fails. Search history is not evidence that an
unlisted use is impossible.

The remainder witness does not satisfy the positive affective analysis. This
matters: the strict semantic-denial fragment and a fuller positive theory are
different configurations. No claim is made that every complete emotivist account
contains a use unexplained by the three named conditions.

## Internal critique survives

`EVIL-INTERNAL-PERMISSION-CHALLENGE` reuses `POE-ROWE-DEDUCTIVE-CORE`. Its only
premise is the existing structured `STMT-ROWE-GRATUITOUS-SUFFERING`. Under that
premise, assuming the target's narrow-theism attributes conflicts with the
target permission constraint. The critic's own moral truth predicate does not
appear in this consequence.

This does **not** make the gratuitous-suffering premise a mere report of pain.
It says there is occurring intense suffering which an omniscient omnipotent
being can prevent without the specified unavoidable greater-good loss or
equal/worse-evil cost. Its counterfactual and evaluative commitments remain
open. Likewise, a target's accepting the permission rule is not proved. An
internal criticism can grant these standards hypothetically; asserting that
an actual theist accepts them or that actual suffering satisfies them needs
further evidence.

The explicit survivor separately models a true, non-moral report of the internal
conditional. Its non-moral classification and the bridge identifying its
content with the target conditional are **explicit conjuncts** of the witness,
not a theorem that every embedded moral conditional is non-moral.

## What the model comparison certifies

`EVIL-OBJECTION-BOUNDARY-MODELS` exports all of the following properties:

- One critic satisfies the four strict truthless premises and the positive
  affective analysis. Its moral evil sentence is not true, while a separate,
  non-moral descriptive report tracks actual intense suffering.
- A narrow-theist Rowe frame satisfies the permission constraint. A record
  update changes **only** its `Cost` relation to always false. Occurrences,
  intensity, attributes, powers and outcome classifications stay fixed.
- The cost-free variant still has a theistic witness and has actual gratuitous
  suffering. The true internal report and internal challenge concern this
  variant. They do not assert the same report about the original frame, whose
  permission constraint is satisfied.
- A separate positive-affective interpretation has a true, truth-apt evil
  utterance, showing that affective analysis alone does not block a true premise.
- A separate strict truthless interpretation and target frame inhabit the
  precise remainder, and named-use coverage fails there.

The construction composes the existing Rowe apparent-compatibility witness;
it does not restate its existence as a new premise. The cost-free variant is
an abstract interpretation, not evidence that real prevention costs vanish.
The report of an event is not identified with Catholic privation or voluntary
culpa. The narrow-theism attribute predicate is not the whole Catholic theory.
The three witness clauses establish formal compatibility and non-entailment,
not the truth or completeness of an actual worldview.

## Root connection and remaining work

The cheapest-route goodness leaf `STMT-GOODNESS-IS-ACTUALITY` remains unchanged.
Neither the affective analysis nor the internal conditional identifies Rowe's
`WhollyGood` or comparative outcomes with the Catholic per-perfection predicate.
A bridge would need to specify that shared interpretation; it is not supplied
by the common word “good.” Likewise, this batch supplies no automatic refutation
of the root and no floor approval. The computed root frontier delta is reported
in the PR.

The next philosophical step is to instantiate an actual target's evaluative
standards and test its permission rule, while preserving the strongest internal
critic. The next coverage step is to prove or challenge named coverage for a
specified relevant-use class. Other-worldview definitions can be added as
explicit sibling propositions and tested; no owner choice of a winning meaning
is needed to explore their consequences.
