# Exact robust-truth rival and the moral-domain boundary

The source statements remain unchanged:

- Quasi-realist reading: every moral content is minimally truth-apt and lacks
  robust truth-aptness.
- Robust-universal reading: every moral content is robustly truth-apt.

These use the same moral and robust-aptness predicates. Their incompatibility
requires a moral content. If there are none, both universals hold. Even with a
moral content they are not exhaustive: a content can lack both minimal and
robust aptness. Refuting one named theory therefore does not prove the other.
This is an exact semantic issue, not a dispute settled by a label or source
identity.

The earlier `QUASI-REALISM-DEVIATES-ON-ROBUST-TRUTH` theorem already retains the
full quasi-realist commitment and Geach's moral-content witness. Its Solution
also has both finite countermodels. This batch imports that theorem and those
witnesses rather than inventing new source definitions, a new moral-existence
primitive, or duplicate models.

`QUASI-REALIST-NONEMPTY-IMPLIES-NOT-ROBUST` derives the exact negation of
`RealistRobustTruthAptReading` from the existing quasi-realist reading and
Geach witness. Were robust universal aptness also to hold, the two source
answers would both hold and so be equivalent, contrary to the existing
checked deviation. The moral witness is an explicit premise; minimal aptness
is not silently reinterpreted as robust aptness.

The existing alternative relation now has that exact negative proposition as
its subject. Its historical ID is retained, and its note links the stronger
raw quasi-realist premise and the argument deriving the new endpoint. A
refutation of the raw quasi-realist reading can therefore no longer be used
through this relation as an automatic defense of robust universal aptness.
The full raw rival and attributed source claims remain in the corpus.

`METAETHICS-TRUTH-APT-RIVAL-CONTROLS` exports two earlier finite witnesses with
bound properties. The first has no moral contents and both source universals;
the second has Geach's moral content and neither universal. They concern only
the reading predicates and establish no consistency or adequacy of complete
historical theories. A named-theory exhaustiveness premise is neither supplied
nor inferred. If an exhaustive philosophical selection is later attempted,
that coverage and admissibility question must be a separate explicit input.

No source reading, existing Challenge, owner-approved floor, or meaning of
robust/minimal truth is changed. The logical endpoint is an analyst proposition
with attribution deferred. Its consequences can be studied conditionally;
neither the source premises nor a Catholicism-root premise is established by
this batch.
