`IntendedProfile`. Its necessity and whole-first support are model assignments,
not an intrinsic explanation, metaphysical possibility or reality-fit claim.

explanation. Thin power is not underived power, materiality or necessity. The

## Certified comparison and applicability cost

The one joint result checks all-world P2 and P3 on the exact underlying profile,
necessary concrete existence of the SAME whole, both actual contingent proper
atoms at every world, strong supplementation, pair fusion and disjointness on
each actual domain. No actual proper-part candidate is necessary or proper in
both worlds. There is no empty object in an actual domain. P3 is full-carrier
well-foundedness of `grounds w`, not a different productive priority relation.
The public `noIncomingWhole` helper exposes the cost of whole-first support.

The conclusion separates these commitments from **worldwise actual
part-freeness**, not every existing primitive `IsSimple` predicate or a richer
classical-attributes package. The whole's necessity is assigned, not derived
from its composition or causal activity. Its own obtaining and intrinsic
explanatory adequacy remain open. The fixed-order actual-fusion/cover NE
results are not instantiated under a silently varying order, and no
part-persistence condition is claimed. No necessary whole is inferred merely
from a different whole existing at each world.
