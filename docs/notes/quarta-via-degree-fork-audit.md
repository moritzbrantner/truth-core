## Proposed conditional consequence

The selected background uses the existing `HomogeneousScaleReading` at
`esse`, `MaximumInBeingCausesAllBeingsReading`, and unrestricted
`CauseOfQualityInAnotherIsMoreSuchReading`, all with the same beings,
respects, actuality, comparative and perfection-cause predicates. Under the
homogeneous representation, `MoreIn esse c x` means `x < c` in one preorder
and every represented being has the selected perfection.

An existing maximal member is only `IsMax` or `IsMaximumIn`: nothing is
strictly above it. Neither means greatest, and even Mathlib's `IsGreatest`
allows distinct tied beings in a preorder. The genus premise supplies a
perfection-cause edge from the maximal member to each distinct represented
being; causal ascent then makes that other being strictly lower. Thus the
proposed strict-top conclusion is stronger than the definition of maximality
and is derived from the causal premises. Strict comparison of every distinct
being also excludes ties at the top; it is not antisymmetry smuggled into the
preorder.

`CausesAllInGenus` excludes the cause itself. The existing maximum schema
concludes **no distinct** incoming cause of the same perfection, leaving
self-causation and other causal respects outside its statement. No result
here identifies a maximum with God, a first being under `PriorSimply`, an
efficient cause, or an absolutely uncaused being.

When there is no maximal member, every represented being has a strictly
higher member classically. Extracting a positive higher witness from a
negative maximality statement needs classical scope. On an empty domain the
universal higher-member assertion holds vacuously. Any claim of actual
unbounded gradation therefore needs a population witness, supplied explicitly
if the existing grades-found-in-things statement is used. The proof must not
list a nonemptiness premise which its advertised conclusion never uses.

This proposed fork has content beyond a bare excluded-middle split only
because its horns expose these different causal/order consequences and have
joint background witnesses. Coverage, exclusivity and the claims about the
horns must each match the final frozen type.

## Reuse and rejected strengthening

`TruthCore.Logic.MaximumOrder.System.maximal_has_no_distinct_cause` is the
existing schema for the incoming-cause conclusion.
`HomogeneousScaleReading.causesRaiseDegree` instantiates its relation with
exactly `CausesIn c x esse`, without introducing a new dependence relation.

`AtMostOneMaximeEns.atMostOneMaximeEns` already excludes tied maximal beings
through causal premises without any order axioms. It takes the broad genus
principle for every respect, whereas the present at-being premise is weaker.
Do not strengthen the at-being premise merely to reuse that theorem, or
present causal uniqueness itself as a new finding. The proposed new scope is
an exhaustive consequence with explicit surviving order/causal branches.

A chain of strictly increasing degrees is not a chain of causes. The Nat
control has ordinary `<` and no `CausesIn` edges: the conditional genus
principle is vacuous because there is no maximum, and causal ascent is
vacuous because there are no causes. Do not convert this into a causal
regress, a temporal history, a metaphysical possibility, or a full rival
worldview. Nat is infinite and must not be registered as a finite carrier.

Tied degrees and incomparable maxima are different controls. In the constant
lift preorder, distinct beings are tied and both maximal and greatest, but
neither is strictly above the other. In an equality partial order, distinct
beings are incomparable and maximal, with no greatest member. The existing
`Order.TiedPrimary.beingOrder` is the ordinary Bool order, even though its
degrees of approach are tied. It must not silently supply a homogeneous
tied-degree order: any constant-lift order must be named separately. Its
existing model also violates both genus and unrestricted causal ascent, so
it is not a full-background witness for the proposed fork.

## Materialized source findings

Materialized and read the full pinned passages `ARIST-MET-II-1`,
`AQUINAS-ST-I-Q2-A3`, `AQUINAS-ST-I-Q3-A5`, and `AQUINAS-ST-I-Q13-A5`.

- Aristotle, *Metaphysics* II.1, 993b24–31, speaks of that in virtue of which
  the **synonymous** quality belongs to the others, with fire and heat as his
  example, then concludes about the principles of eternal beings and truth.
  The existing unrestricted cause-more-such formalization drops synonymy.
  That extension remains an explicit, unattributed open strengthening.
- ST I q.2 a.3, fourth way, supplies the grades, the maximum step and the
  maximum-to-cause claim. None makes the homogeneous preorder a sourced
  definition of the whole argument, and the present conditional fork does
  not establish the maximum-existence premise.
- ST I q.3 a.5 ad 2 distinguishes a proportioned measure, homogeneous with
  what it measures, from God, and characterizes the latter measure through
  approach. The order representation must retain that interpretive boundary.
- ST I q.13 a.5 co. denies univocal predication of God and creatures and
  describes analogy through their order to a principle and cause. The
  homogeneous profile here is a stipulative contrast reading, not an
  attribution to Aquinas. A pulled-back order by approach can have the same
  formal order shape when universal possession is supplied separately; order
  shape alone neither establishes univocity nor distinguishes analogy.

Virtual versus formal possession, causes of perfection versus efficient
causes, and simple priority remain the existing explicit sense choices. No
new attributed source claim is added, and no source sentence has been
strengthened to secure the proposed proof. Root-premise delta remains none.
