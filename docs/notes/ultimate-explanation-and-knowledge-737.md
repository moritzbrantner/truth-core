# Ultimate explanation, local prediction and practical expectations (#737)

The established discriminator is **conditional independence of the represented
local performance from the represented ground**, not a solution to induction.
A residual ultimate why-question does not, by logic alone, entail that laws
change, predictions fail, local conditional explanations are false, or every
practical choice is unreasonable. Conversely, a source, a necessary being or
a unique complete mathematical history does not supply an agent's entitlement
to the laws. This advances the #736 → #735/#613 investigation without closing
a #143 premise.

## The end-to-end argument and its strongest challenge

1. First ask which question an account answers. An explanation of an event
   conditional on a law, a starting condition and a mechanism differs from an
   account of why this law and actual complete history obtain. The second
   question can remain open while the conditional inference in the first is
   valid. Correct mechanism interpretation and actual law applicability are
   additional requirements; a valid calculation is not sufficient for every
   kind of scientific explanation.
2. #736 makes a particular **entailing whole-history selection** test precise.
   An accessible different history sharing the whole admitted reason-vector
   defeats every selecting proposition depending only on that vector. The
   source-free and rooted controls both fail this test on constant evidence;
   both pass with an extra internal marker. Its universal adequacy application
   and its independently relevant, non-target-copying explanation remain OPEN.
3. The new composition observes these very histories through a fixed instrument:
   “is either of the two candidate members present at this time?” Both populated
   histories always answer yes; their bearer identity differs. Thus their local
   observable has a common prefix, common copy-law profile and exact local
   predictions, despite the certified whole-history selection failure. This is
   a real lossy observation map, not an epistemic-adequacy Boolean. The rooted
   history has **the same** presence facts; adding its source changes neither
   this observable nor the missing selection information.
4. The same #736 certificate supplies a unique beginningless Int signal under
   independent global gate constraints. Its first component at Nat times is
   exactly the same observed stream. The new theorem derives its predictive
   properties using the imported uniqueness theorem. It does not add a fresh
   uniqueness assumption or identify logical gates with efficient causes. A
   unique global solution can settle mathematical conditional predictions while
   leaving constraint actuality and agent access unestablished.
5. Now hold the observed prefix fixed and relax only future transition
   restrictions. The everywhere-on history and the existing Hume delayed-failure
   trace both satisfy the same supplied open-future law and the same observations
   through n. They differ at n+1. A persistence forecast is no longer exact;
   the same failure occurs alongside a grounded Scope. The issue is which
   continuations a justified law licenses, not simply whether there is a root.
6. In the explicit task, a safe action earns 1 whichever continuation occurs;
   a correct bet earns 2 and a wrong bet 0. No action is optimal in every
   continuation, but the safe action still meets a guaranteed threshold.
   A stronger law restricting the cell to the stable case makes the true-bet
   action optimal. Losing exact prediction therefore differs from losing all
   useful choices. Neither payoff property is called normative rationality by
   definition.
7. To conclude that ultimate incompleteness defeats **epistemic entitlement**,
   one must independently show what entitlement condition the missing ground
   removes, even when locally relevant evidence and process conditions are
   retained. To obtain a theistic advantage, one must also show how its ground
   supplies that condition and how the agent has appropriate access. No such
   condition follows from ground existence or complete-history uniqueness.

The strongest foundational case is that an adequate final account should make
the order and its cognitive accessibility intelligible, rather than merely
repeat their obtaining. Leibniz, *Monadology* §§32, 36–38 and 45, proposes a
sufficient reason for contingent series and an intrinsic necessary reason.
This is a substantial explanatory demand; the #736 no-selection theorem can
constrain its entailing version when its application premises are defended.
But §32 expressly permits reasons unknown to us. An ontic reason's existence
is therefore already distinct from a humanly possessed reason. Aquinas,
ST I q.105 a.5, supplies a stronger positive classical account than occasionalist
replacement: primary action preserves genuine secondary powers and operations.
That can explain why local mechanisms operate, if the account is true. Neither
passage states a theorem that every non-theistic scientist lacks justification,
or that recognizing an ultimate source reveals every future outcome.

A critic can still argue that brute or purely structural reality has an
**explanatory debt**. The result blocks upgrading that debt to empirical
instability or universal skepticism without the additional argument. A rival
can also leave ultimate actuality unexplained without thereby contradicting
its local prediction claims. No self-undermining refutation of naturalism,
Hume, theism or science has been obtained.

The formal conclusion is compatible with each strongest account's positive
proposal. It does not settle the accounts' truth, eliminate every missing
ultimate-reason debt, or imply that their evidential situations are equal.
An account could improve prediction by providing independently supported new
law information, effective mechanisms, calibrated instruments or defeater
removal. The strict refinement subtest demonstrates just such a mathematical
improvement when the stable law is granted. Merely changing a ground assignment
without changing those inputs does not deliver it.

## Exact vocabulary, models and omission controls

`Profile.initial` is a Boolean state, not an adequacy flag; `step` is a supplied
relation between current and next states at a time. `Follows` checks the initial
condition and all transitions. `Cell p observed n` additionally matches the
complete observed prefix **through n inclusive**. `forecast` repeats observed n
for n+1. `Accurate` universally quantifies over the cell and checks output truth.
The local-law and evidence assumptions are explicit in the cell; an agent's
knowledge of them is **not** supplied. Histories are mathematical functions,
not a claim that each admissible trace is metaphysically accessible.

The stable profile has initial true and `next=current`; the on trace inhabits
every evidence cell. Induction from the initial state and transitions derives
truth at each later time. `openFuture n` imposes copying only when
`t+1<n+1`; thereafter either next state is allowed. At every n (including zero),
on and `delayedFailure (n+1)` agree on every observed case and satisfy this
same profile, then disagree at n+1. The stronger stable cell is a proper subset:
the failed-next trace witnesses strictness. This separates observed stability,
determinism of a supplied rule, logical entailment under that rule, and the
unproved warrant for using that rule on unobserved cases.

Scope reuses the exact `Regularity.brute` and `grounded` relations. Both retain
ordinary-process temporal causes; only the totality ground differs. These are
**separable product interpretations** with the trace profiles: no philosophical
ontic-to-epistemic dependence is silently built in. This demonstrates a missing
bridge under that explicit semantics, not that the world must really factor
this way. The product witnesses also do not assert complete ultimate adequacy
of the grounded model or genuine impossibility of every ground in the brute one.
The stronger #736 history composition shares actual encoded presence arenas;
its coarse instrument has the same candidates and law on both sides, rather
than merely two arbitrary perfect-success flags.
