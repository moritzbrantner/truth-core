# Observational authority and holistic justification

## Question and source boundary

The current regress investigation asks what an account supplies beyond a predecessor, consistent network or available sequence. This bounded reading supplies a positive reply rather than an inference from formal graph consistency.

## The positive reply and its limits

| Reading | Precise locator | Contribution to the regress question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Noninferential is not autonomous | VIII §32; §36 | Stops identifying a noninferential report with a premise needing no other epistemic capacities or knowledge. |
| Recognized report authority | VIII §35 | Includes an actual reporting-world constraint and the subject’s recognition of reliability; merely drawing an edge or producing a causal response does not meet the stated demand. |
| Current evidence and earlier development | VIII §37, answering §36 | Blocks the inference from present evidence requirements to an earlier sequence of already knowledgeable episodes. It does not establish the present requirements themselves. |
| Different dimensions of support | VIII §38, concluding paragraphs | Retains observational asymmetry while offering a holistic dependence claim. Different labels alone do not prove that reciprocity is benign. |
| Piecemeal correction | VIII §38, final paragraph | Proposes a practice with revisable commitments rather than arbitrary immunity or simultaneous withdrawal of every claim. Mere revisability does not entail truth. |

This is a reliability-sensitive holistic account with a retained observational role, not a claim that every foundationalism is false or that coherence means formal consistency. The limited section supplies no complete theory of inner episodes: Sellars expressly defers that work to later sections. It therefore cannot be promoted to an independently established sufficient account of empirical knowledge.
