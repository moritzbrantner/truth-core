# Strong-version consumer audit (#1021)

This bounded audit covers the twenty non-mandate consumers that lacked a
`strong_version_needed` disposition at main `5fcedc52`: seventeen uses of
STMT-SECOND-MOVERS-MOVE-ONLY-AS-MOVED and three uses of
STMT-FIRST-UNMOVED-MOVER-CANNOT-BE-LACKING. Strength registrations, the original
absolute PrimaVia result (already documented), and adopted-potency wrappers
(already scoped as reused-proof dependencies) are outside this roster. The
mandate-strength consumer belongs to the separate authority investigation.

The conclusions below concern explicit encoded interfaces. A stronger source
reading remains available in its frozen Challenge. Retaining that interface
for comparison does not mean it is globally necessary, and proving a reduced
conditional bundle does not establish its remaining premises.

## Exact reuse and decisive tests

The existing strengthening registrations are
`PrimaViaStrength.PerSeLink.secondMoversStrongEntailsRelevantRespect` and
`PrimaViaStrength.AbsoluteReading.absoluteReadingEntailsRelative` under
`TruthCore.Results.Aquinas.SummaTheologiae`. Neither runs in reverse. A
replacement that simply sets RelevantTo to True would recover the strong
condition, so it is not used as a purported weakening.

The negative controls reuse `PrimaViaFacts.WeakReadingDoesNotYield` and the
actual `PrimaViaModels.weakModel`, the checked `PrimaViaRelative` result, and
the two Aristotle regress-transfer omission models. Their exact new binding
is PRIMA-VIA-WEAK-CONSUMER-BOUNDARIES:

- The literal being-in-act reduction is true in the weak model too. It does
  not repair the gap from the relevant-respect link to absolute unmovedness.
- For VII.1, the existing infinite Nat successor chain with no per-se links
  extends by relevance false. The weak link and per-se well-foundedness hold,
  while the restricted local moved-mover chain is infinite.
- The corresponding VIII.5 successor interpretation retains III.3 as well;
  relevance is still missing, so its mover-moved-by-another relation need not
  be well founded. These two controls are infinite Nat interpretations, not
  claims about a finite model.
- For generic Stage1, the three-being weak model satisfies the relative
  conclusion, the particular excluded-middle instance, uniqueness, pure-act
  and identification conditionals. Those conditionals concern absolute first
  movers and are vacuous there; IsGod may be false. No Fourth-Way or further
  attribute/historical background is certified by that model.
- The director test retains its *whole* remaining interface, including the
  through-director and unmoved-design bridges and explicit EM. Directs and
  NaturalBody are true in the finite weak interpretation, all beings are
  moved, and cognition/intelligence are false. The unmoved-design bridge is
  vacuous, not an existence premise. The absolutely immobile intelligent
  director conclusion fails. This is a formal interpretation, not a claim
  that its predicates apply to the world.

## Redundant First-Way detours

The full-description Stage1 result already has a Fourth-Way maximum and a
checked theorem naming that maximum God. Its conclusion only asks for *some*
God, not that the witness be the First Way's first mover. The new
CATHOLICISM-STAGE1-FULL-DESCRIPTION-WITHOUT-MOTION therefore removes four
inputs: absolute First-Way non-lacking, its existence-or-not instance, the
all-movers-in-act premise, and created-agent application. Naming remains an
open premise and supplies no maximum on its own.

The same direct maximum supplies the Q11 and equality-decision classical
witnesses. The new witness siblings remove eight inputs: sensible motion,
moved-as-potency, all-movers-in-act, mover reduction, the strong per-se link,
per-se regress, the particular First-Way excluded-middle instance, and
created-agent application. Non-overlap of act/potency stays where attributes
use it. The equality-decision sibling retains its original DecidableEq
interfaces; the Q11 sibling retains its three individuation/nature premises.
Three root siblings compose those witnesses/results with the **existing**
Skeleton theorem, without rewriting its historical/authority deduction.
The full-description root (whose attributes were already supplied as an
input) additionally drops the unused act/potency non-overlap premise.

For three other audited consumers, an exact certified Fourth-Way subset
already existed: CATHOLICISM-QUARTA-VIA-CLASSICAL-WITNESS and
CATHOLICISM-FROM-QUARTA-VIA-WITHOUT-TRUTH-BRIDGE. Their predicate/parameter
roles match the original consumers; no new primitive, naming converse,
semantic identification, or duplicate result is introduced for them.

POE-CATHOLIC-ENTAILS-EVIL-PREMISE never used its motion or God commitments.
POE-EVIL-FROM-DUE-DEFICIENCY isolates the typed privation/deficiency inference.
POE-EVIL-PREMISE-FROM-DUE-DEFICIENCY then retains only those two inputs and the
explicit sentence-truth bridge. Its final bridge application is labelled a
reduced transcription. Neither the bridge nor actual deficiency is established
by the reduction, and no actual divine permission/production claim follows.

- `CATHOLICISM-FROM-PRIMA-VIA-VIA-MAXIME-ENS`: The current composition imports the absolute PrimaVia result and StageOneViaMaximeEns, whose naming predicate God is separate from the skeleton IsGod. No full-bundle weak replacement or countermodel is claimed; the generic weak model does not certify the extra Fourth-Way, attribute, revelation or historical interfaces. This is a current-proof dependency, not a claim that every proof must use the stronger input. See docs/notes/prima-via-strength-consumers.md.
- `CATHOLICISM-FROM-PRIMA-VIA`: The current composition imports absolute PrimaVia, StageOneFromPrimaVia and Skeleton. Its God-identification bridge applies to an absolute first mover. The relative Stage1 countermodel checks that subargument, not the full retained Signature/history/teaching-authority bundle. A full-bundle weaker derivation or countermodel remains open. This is a current-proof dependency, not a claim that every proof must use the stronger input. See docs/notes/prima-via-strength-consumers.md.
- `CATHOLICISM-STAGE1-VIA-MAXIME-ENS`: The frozen proof derives uniqueness for absolute first movers and calls StageOneFromPrimaVia. The Fourth Way names a separate God predicate here, not IsGod; no unrestricted identification between those predicates is supplied. A replacement using only the relative conclusion has not been derived for the full retained bundle. The generic Stage1 countermodel does not certify all of this additional maximum/naming/applied-agent background. This is a current-proof dependency, not a claim that every proof must use the stronger input. See docs/notes/prima-via-strength-consumers.md.
- `POE-CATHOLIC-DENIES-OBJECTION-ONE`: The current proof derives an absolute first mover and applies the naming conjunct of the frozen Catholic bundle to it before contradicting evil exclusion. The weak model refutes the absolute First-Way substep only; it has not been extended here to every privation/truth/divine-goodness/production conjunct of the full Catholic signature. The full-bundle weak replacement remains open; the stronger input is retained for this proof, not declared globally necessary. This is a current-proof dependency, not a claim that every proof must use the stronger input. See docs/notes/prima-via-strength-consumers.md.

No countermodel above is silently extended to these added predicates or
bridges. In particular, `God` and `IsGod` in StageOneViaMaximeEns are different
parameters; naming a maximum with the former does not identify it with the
latter. Conversely, the exact direct maximum route in the full-description
case prevents claiming that absolute motion is needed merely because the old
proof happened to use it.

## Premise and proof boundary

All original Challenges and proofs are unchanged. New Challenges were fixed
before their Solutions, and every actual proof hypothesis is listed in its
argument. No source, passage, claim or floor item is added or altered.
The `strong_version_needed` fields explain why a frozen legacy interface is
retained and point to its disposition; they are not themselves proof evidence.
Actual root-cost effects come only from `corpus.py frontier-diff`, not from
counting warnings eliminated or new connections. The mandate use remains
outside this batch.
