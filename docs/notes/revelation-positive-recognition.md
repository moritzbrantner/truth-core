## Strong positive rival and further commitments

A nonprobabilistic explanatory recognizer may provisionally accept the author
whose account best explains an addressed message's content, timing, relevance
and independently confirmed effects, under public comparison criteria. It can
rationally tolerate a possible imitation: possibility alone does not show the
imitator explains why this message reached this recipient at this occasion.
This is a positive replacement standard, not a defective exact classifier.
Its commitments are a relevance-sensitive explanatory ordering, reasons why
that ordering tracks truth, a fair candidate comparison and a withdrawal rule
when an equally good rival or forged evidence appears. Ties/incomparability
must permit suspension. An unexamined author can defeat the comparison.
No probability or arbitrary score is needed. Conversely, being best among
examined accounts is not deductive source exclusion or infallibility.

The next materially different test is an independently specified addressed
challenge transcript with ordinary forgery/replay alternatives: check both
which transcripts distinguish authors and whether the explanatory ranking
survives adding the replay account. Authorship must not be encoded into the
visible transcript. This needs a justified transmission/access model absent
from the current task inputs, not another renamed Boolean success flag.

### Strongest surviving alternative and premise reassessment

A contemporary ordinary producer can copy a leaked challenge or happen to
produce its token without prior access. Both yield precisely the displayed
addressed reply. Successful guessing is a positive ordinary production account,
not proof-search failure. Its price is rejecting the deterministic access law
“a producer can emit only tokens in its prior access list.” That law is far
stronger than ordinary secrecy, and ordinary agents can emit unlearned tokens.
Thus the law is useful as a conditional protocol restriction but presently
unwarranted as a universal description of ordinary message production. A public
record that “only candidate 0 knew 42” neither supplies actual exclusivity nor
repairs access discipline. Freshness can rule out old-token replay while leaving
this contemporary alternative untouched. Integrity is separately vulnerable to
record insertion or substitution. These are independent burdens, not four
names for a successful-recognition flag.

using independently grounded content relevance, timing and production history.
The identical visible transcript gives the same transcript-fit to genuine,
leaked, inserted, guessed and replayed accounts. A ranking by that fit alone
therefore cannot uniquely select the candidate. A richer ordering needs actual
additional grounds and a truth-relevance defense; neither is supplied here.

equal total explanatory merit. Ties/incomparability require suspension under
