## Strong positive rival and further commitments

A nonprobabilistic explanatory recognizer may provisionally accept the author
whose account best explains an addressed message's content, timing, relevance
and independently confirmed effects, under public comparison criteria. It can
rationally tolerate a possible imitation: possibility alone does not show the
imitator explains why this message reached this recipient at this occasion.
This is a positive replacement standard, not a defective exact classifier.
Its commitments are a relevance-sensitive explanatory ordering, reasons why
that ordering tracks truth, a fair candidate comparison and a withdrawal rule
when an equally good rival or forged evidence appears. Ties/incomparability
must permit suspension. An unexamined author can defeat the comparison.
No probability or arbitrary score is needed. Conversely, being best among
examined accounts is not deductive source exclusion or infallibility.

The next materially different test is an independently specified addressed
challenge transcript with ordinary forgery/replay alternatives: check both
which transcripts distinguish authors and whether the explanatory ranking
survives adding the replay account. Authorship must not be encoded into the
visible transcript. This needs a justified transmission/access model absent
from the current task inputs, not another renamed Boolean success flag.
