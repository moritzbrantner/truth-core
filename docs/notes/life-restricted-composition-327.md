## An affirmative operational candidate that can fail

Independently identify two local productive capacities: substrate processing and
boundary production. Inputs contain no organism, substantial unity, event identity
or soul. `State` records their current occupancies. Under feed `f`, the declared
internal mechanism produces a fresh realizer of **each** role iff feed and both
prior capacities are present: `(f ∧ processing ∧ boundary)` for both outputs.
Old tokens are discarded each tick. A token's tick and role identify material,
not a substance or life. Production, feed and role identification are primitive
physical interpretations of a declared rule; their actual realization is OPEN.

`Reciprocal` requires full occupancy to regenerate, removal of both capacities
to leave no productive occupancy, and removal of either capacity to abolish the
other's next output. Thus the criterion is affirmative and experimentally
sensitive: it rejects the absent and single-capacity states in this mechanism.
It is not a renamed `Alive` flag or a definition that assumes an organism exists.

The comparator mechanism is an external replacer that outputs both capacities
whenever feed is supplied, regardless of local occupancy. At the active full
state it produces exactly the same next occupancy as reciprocal regeneration.
It fails the extinction and knockout tests. Hence one observed steady trajectory
cannot warrant the reciprocal mechanism: the required difference concerns
**intervention responses**. This does not prove every externally supported system
fails the criterion. A sensor-controlled external apparatus could implement the
same truth table. Identifying the *local producer* rather than just the output
correlation remains an independent empirical burden; no causal or grounding
relation is derived from Boolean syntax.

The active `run` retains both capacities at every tick, while no token present
at tick n is present at n+1. Complete turnover is consequently compatible with
continued maintenance. Persistence as one object requires the separate numerical
identity bridge already isolated by ProcessTurnover. Its checked obstruction to
one timeless constituent extension is reused, not redescribed as a refutation
of materialism or time-indexed constitution.

An interruption of feed empties this deliberately fragile mechanism. Restoring
feed alone leaves it empty. This supplies a concrete distinction between active
maintenance and restart after loss of productive organization. It is **not** a
biological death theorem: seeds, dormancy, repair, transplantation and reversible
arrest can preserve capacities this coarse model erases. Equating inactivity
with death was rejected. A dormant-capacity state and tested reactivation
mechanism are required before that extension can be claimed.

## Life-event persistence and the strongest reply

The same maintenance rule and full local state can be placed at all three
admitted Fission1397 occurrences, with the old independently supplied edges from
earlier to each daughter. This checks the local criterion without defining
numerical event identity. For any assignment of life-event labels to these
occurrences, the imported theorem shows that treating **both** edges as sufficient
for ordinary equality excludes distinct daughter events. The certificate does
not derive a biological fission mechanism, adequate resource flow or identity
of an organism from this occurrence fixture. It is a structural boundary on one
proposed persistence inference.

A strong life-restricted reply treats biological life and well-individuated
self-maintaining events as richer than this finite rule. It can retain causal
continuation to both daughters while letting the parent event end and two new
events begin; it can add an independently grounded asymmetry selecting one
continuation; or it can allow indeterminacy. These are positive persistence
accounts, not contradictions. The first predicts no numerical parent survivor
in symmetric division; the second predicts one survivor only when the additional
feature is present; the third withholds a determinate verdict. Nonbranching
continuation may be a qualified sufficient rule but does not by itself settle
fusion or the identification of unique events. Fission1397's checked
solo/split result already exposes the extra cross-context verdict-invariance
burden; no new theorem or biological applicability is claimed for it here.

The strongest objection to the operational candidate is therefore **inadequacy**,
not covert soul commitment: reciprocal active production may be realized by an
artificial system, yet miss living dormant systems and provide no unique maximal
boundary for nested organs/colonies. A biological restriction may answer those
cases, but it must specify positive conditions in terms not already assuming
which organism/event exists. Naming an event “a life” would leave the original
question open. We have made the testable part explicit and located the residual
individuation burden, rather than claiming to have eliminated it.

## Provisional assessment, revision and remaining acceptance

As of2026-11-11, reciprocal regeneration is a useful **conditional operational
candidate** for active maintenance: it distinguishes internally stipulated
cross-production from one explicit external replacer under identical active
occupancy. It presently has insufficient warrant as a necessary and sufficient
criterion of biological life or as an independently complete composition theory.
The strongest standing alternative is the richer biological/event account above,
with explicit indeterminacy or parent-termination at symmetric fission. Its price
is a still-unanswered positive life/event individuation condition, not a formal
contradiction. Universalist, nihilist and soul-as-form ontologies also survive;
none is ranked defeated by this certificate.

This judgment depends on a deliberately small mechanism and intervention scope,
not independent biological observations. It would strengthen with independently
identified producer/boundary criteria and a successful dormant/active and
organism/external-apparatus discrimination; it would weaken if realistic dormant
life systematically lacks an extensible version of the capacities. A grounded
biological account could supersede it. A justified event individuation rule could
resolve persistence while leaving composition/grounding disputed.

#327 receives an affirmative non-soul operational proposal, source-backed
composition/persistence distinction, typed turnover and interruption cases, a
typed structural event-persistence boundary, and all seven rival answer-map
entries. Remaining acceptance: a warranted comprehensive life criterion,
biological event individuation, actual fission/fusion and transplant treatment,
and bacteria/plants/colonies/viruses/seeds/artificial cells/robots/isolated-organs

### An independently specified local capacity test

The proposed test concerns **one operation**, hydrolytic cleavage of the selected
cortex material and its contribution to restart. “Producer” means the located
catalytic material effecting that chemical conversion, not the organism, a
creator of the enzyme, or the ultimate efficient/existential cause. It does not
mean that the same catalyst synthesizes itself. “Capacity retained” means that
the relevant machinery is present in the dormant preparation and can contribute
the operation in specified activation/access conditions without importing a
replacement catalyst. This is a dispositional interpretation with explicit
conditions, not the inference that every detected molecule is functional.

Operationally select the material specimen by its observed layered structure,
separation from the surrounding bath, and recoverable protein fractions. The
candidate boundary is that specimen's measured envelope and associated layers;
it is not defined by a prior verdict about which organism exists. An enzyme
introduced into the bath counts as externally supplied relative to this boundary.
Changing or removing layers changes the experiment and must be recorded. The
selection identifies a reaction compartment, not a unique maximal life boundary.

The evidential standard combines localization, function-sensitive perturbation
and specified supplementation. Detection alone fails to distinguish competent
machinery from an inactive version. A successful endpoint alone fails to locate
its producer. The proposed discrimination therefore asks separately:

| Test | Retained local contribution sought | External completion sought |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locate catalytic material before the activation challenge | Identifiable material associated with the selected preparation | Supplied catalyst initially in the bath |
| Compare competent, missing and catalytically impaired versions | Function depends on more than molecular occupancy | Added competent catalyst can bypass a missing local function where access permits |
| Record catalyst addition and envelope/access treatment | Do not count imported replacement as already retained | Do not confuse failed entry with absent catalytic ability |
| Follow the chosen chemical endpoint and later recovery separately | Attribute only the demonstrated operation | External completion of one step is not whole-system self-maintenance |

This table is an analyst experimental interpretation of the inspected evidence,
not a new certificate, a necessary/sufficient definition of life, or a claim that
every listed control was jointly performed on the same individual specimen.
Genetic comparisons concern different preparations and can affect assembly;
they are not acute removal from an otherwise unchanged dormant specimen. The
interpretation depends on assay specificity, competent controls, comparable
preparation and adequate exclusion of contamination or unintended genetic effects.
These assumptions supply defeasible warrant for a local catalytic contribution,
not a metaphysical dependence axiom. The incomplete activation pathway prevents
claiming a fully specified physical restart mechanism.

The abstraction bridge is correspondingly modest: infer retained competence
for the assayed local operation from jointly adequate molecular and functional
evidence under the listed conditions. Do **not** identify it with either Boolean
occupancy or the complete physical state. No claim is made that the source
specimens instantiate `LifeRestricted327.regenerate`, that dormant and apparatus
cases share its truth table, or that one external rescue supplies a wholly
externally sustained apparatus. The new observations answer where a particular
contribution resides; they leave whole-system classification open.

### Strongest surviving replacement accounts and failed extensions

A bath-fed device with preinstalled catalysts could meet this local criterion.
Its positive explanation is that stable material organization retains conditional
chemical competence, while supplied resources permit operation. Rejecting it
merely for being artificial would assume the disputed life restriction. If an
artificial system also regenerates its machinery and boundaries, that could be
artificial life; the present evidence supplies no exclusion. A system with an
external controller/catalyst source can also imitate selected outputs. Enlarging
the boundary to include its controller changes the system being compared, rather
than deriving a unique organism boundary.

Thus two questions must stay distinct: **is a specified contribution retained
locally?** and **is this the organization sufficient for one biological life?**
The experiment bears on the former. It does not adjudicate a fully internally
stocked apparatus against a dormant organism. A putative general organizational
account would additionally need a justified criterion connecting retained
machinery, its renewal and the selection of the integrated system across active
and dormant phases. Imposing current continuous production would reinstate the
original dormancy gap. Calling a past production history “its own” would assume
the identity to be explained. Neither move is used here.

Van Inwagen's strongest reply remains a complete biological life/event account
with permitted vagueness, not the claim that this single assay settles his
criterion. It may incorporate dormant competence while requiring further
organization and continuity. Such an account is compatible with these local
findings. It still owes independently characterized life and event-individuation
conditions for the original acceptance question; this study neither supplies nor
refutes them. Universalist and nihilist descriptions can retain the same local
chemistry; soul-as-form may accept it while disputing its ontological sufficiency.
The shared answer map above therefore receives physical detail for Q-LIFE-SOUL
and Q-EXPLANATION, without a new verdict for Q-COMPOSITION or Q-IDENTITY.
