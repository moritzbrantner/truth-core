# Cogito and the adopted esse criterion (#1113)

The owner [adopted the existence/esse bridge](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/truth-core/issues/1113#issuecomment-6684716741): an individual existing in re is in act of esse, and therefore concrete in the adopted sense. Concreteness adds no further restriction to real existence. This is an intended interpretation, not a theorem about arbitrary predicates in every legacy `Ontic` or grounding `Profile`.

`TruthCore.Logic.CogitoEsse.profile` retains the original ontic signature and grounding edges exactly, fixing only `concrete := HasEsseInRe InAct esse`. The selected-world `ExistingInActAt` contract remains explicit in the theorem types. It is not marked as a new self-evident floor item. It asserts no converse, causation/grounding identification, causal endpoint presence, modal rule, or actual occurrence of thought.

## Conditional connections

`bearerConcrete` composes the existing bearer-existence bridge E with `ExistingInActAt`: an occurring thought's bearer exists at the selected world, so it is in act of esse and concrete. The result C is conditional on these two premises, rather than independently defended or established. No new bare premise replaces C: both premises already belong to the existing case.

The inhabited-world, ancestry-fork and necessary-ancestor results import their original Solutions and supply this derived C. The same-witness essence composition uses the q3 profile's own `InAct` and `esse`, so its concrete ancestor, actual existence, typed cause, support and essence identity concern the same profile and same witness throughout. Its other premises remain unchanged. The original generic Challenges and omission controls are frozen. The profile reparameterization does not itself certify the paradigm Anchors required of a full intended interpretation; no model witness or nonconstant classification is advertised. Those independent semantic requirements are not hidden as unused hypotheses in the conditional theorems.

`actualConcreteGround_iff` makes the interpretation's effect explicit: under the selected-world contract, an actual concrete ground-source is exactly an existing source with the given grounding edge. The concrete guard contributes no extra condition for such a source. This says nothing about nonexisting candidate grounds or a grounding edge's independent real-world applicability.

The old omission model with an existing but nonconcrete bearer rejects this contract. It is still a valid countermodel for the old arbitrary-predicate signature, but not for the adopted specialization. A nonexisting abstract candidate is still permitted, and existence in the candidate type is never confused with existence in re.

## Operation route and its precise gap

The existing q75 declaration `OperationOnlyOfBeingInActReading` is restricted to `Ontic.Causes`. It cannot be applied to a bearer merely because that bearer thinks. In particular, the established weak causal-concreteness floor item does not make thinking an efficient causal act.

The additional checked theorem `bearerConcreteOfOperation` encodes the requested operation route with its inputs visible:

1. `BorneThoughtExercisesIntellect` (`STMT-COGITO-THOUGHT-IS-INTELLECTUAL-EXERCISE`): the bearer of each occurring episode is attributed that intellectual exercise, using the existing q75 `OperationAttribution` vocabulary.
2. `IntellectualExerciseRequiresAct` (`STMT-INTELLECTUAL-EXERCISE-REQUIRES-ACT`): such an exercise requires the bearer to be in act in some respect.
3. The existing `ActualityOnlyInsofarAsBeingReading`: actuality in any respect requires act of esse.

The conclusion is C in the adopted esse sense. These two operation interfaces are analyst formulations, with exact historical attribution deferred. They are not asserted by the meanings of `Occurs` or `Bears`, not inferred from the causation-only q75 premise, and not registered as extra open root premises. The Comparator configuration for the existence route checks this conditional theorem as well. The remaining blocker to using this as an independently recorded root derivation is justification/interpretation of the occurrence-to-intellectual-exercise bridge and the exact q75 operation instance; no new source claim has been invented to fill it. The source lead remains q75 a2, already available through the repository's operation-attribution records.

No thinking episode, bearer, existence assignment, grounding premise, source attribution, or Catholicism conclusion is established by this batch. The computed frontier comparison in the PR is the authority for its actual premise effects.
