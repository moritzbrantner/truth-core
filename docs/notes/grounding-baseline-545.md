# Modal contingency, complete-ground candidates and enabling cycles (#545 A–D)

This is the approved formal baseline of #545, not delivery of its later P2-B
argument from adequate explanation to real existential support. Every relation
below is an analyst configuration. No historical reading or philosophical
premise is established by compiling it. Source-derived claims are unchanged.

## Shared question and exact reuse

Does modal contingency itself supply dependence, and what remains if complete
grounds must terminate and necessitate their targets? The strongest surviving
responses are a brute contingent terminus, nonnecessitating full explanation,
a non-well-founded complete dependency structure, contingent enabling
conditions, and denial of objective contingency. A contradictory package does
not select which member should be rejected.

The implementation reuses the actual `Ontic`, `Modal.Formula` and `Modal.Holds`
for the first comparison, `Modal.axiomK_valid` for the three-input core,
`ExplanatoryAdequacy.forced` for contingent descent, `Graph.Map.wellFounded`
for its explicit ordering projection, and `CondensationSufficiency.comp_eq_iff`
for the Mathlib SCC assertion. `StoppingPoints.NecessaryFact` is exported, not
reinvented. NecessaryFactBoundary's positive producer has stronger
ReasonClosed/nonCopy/coverage inputs and a different output; adding those
premises to the plural-set terminus merely to call that producer would be a
false strengthening. AutonomousGroundNecessity and JointGroundEffectContingency
operate on selected positive-bearer/group records; they are related boundaries,
not proofs of the unrestricted fact-set claim here. Their original Challenges
and sources are untouched.

## A: the modal reduct leaves grounding independent

`Extension` retains one Ontic object (including its efficient-cause relation)
and adds an independent `grounds w r f` relation. This candidate relation is
read as an immediate existential grounding arrow at that world. It is not
`Ontic.Causes`, temporal production or an assertion that modal nonnecessity is
passive potency. Endpoint truth/distinctness and local WF are tested separately,
not built into a predicate called "grounding".

`SameModal` requires pointwise-equivalent accessibility and candidate existence;
rigid identity is the literal equality of the same carrier. Induction on the
**existing propositional modal language**, with existence and identity atoms,
preserves truth. No new first-order object language is introduced. The separately frozen
GENERAL-MODAL-REDUCT-INVARIANCE companion covers any SAME semantic query
function receiving only access, existence and rigid identity. Such a function
may quantify internally; extensional equivalence of these relations makes its
two values identical. It has no varying ground argument. A ground-sensitive
query evaluated with differently captured ground relations does not satisfy
this factorization condition. The language-specific theorem remains constructive;
the semantic companion uses the approved extensional mathematical axioms. The exact two witness extensions literally
share the same Ontic reduct. The selected candidate exists at world false and
not at world true; another exists at both. One extension has no grounding
arrows, the other has only necessary-candidate→contingent-candidate at actual
false. Both have existing distinct endpoints and local WF, and both have
empty efficient causation. The contingent candidate is grounded only in the
second. These are coherent formal interpretations, not established real
possibilities or a denial of an independently defended modal-to-ground bridge.

## B: three-policy necessity/contingency incompatibility

With a fixed arena, actual world a, target p and supplied total condition q:

- C: p is true at a and false at an accessible world.
- N: q is true at every accessible world.
- S: at every accessible world, q implies p.

Normal modal K yields boxed p from N and S, contradicting C. No S5,
reflexivity, actual truth of q or independent PSR is required. Reading q as a
*complete* ground must include every relevant enabling condition; the schema
itself does not certify completeness or explanatory adequacy.

The three Bool-world deletion controls all have actual p and q true. Dropping
C makes both necessary; dropping N makes q vary with p; dropping S retains a
necessary q and contingent p. Thus every two-policy subset is model-witnessed
and the three-policy set is incompatible under these exact semantics.

`CONFIG-GROUNDING-MODAL-CONDITION-CORE` registers the exact core. The current
configuration tool accepts raw Frame/valuation roles, not a three-carrier
Arena role. `conditionArena` is a data-only specialization computing the SAME
basic arena; its separately frozen core simply applies the generic theorem.
All three policies remain present; no dimension was dropped to evade a bound.

## C: a contingent fact without any admitted complete ground

An admitted candidate is an arbitrary predicate-valued set S and a fact f,
recorded by `G S f`. Admission itself implies none of these independent inputs:

1. every member of every admitted S is actually true;
2. every member r of S is an immediate R-predecessor of f;
3. truth of ALL members at an accessible world suffices for f there;
4. R is well-founded.

The theorem additionally takes an actually contingent fact x and explicit
excluded middle. It concludes that **some** actually contingent fact has **no**
admitted complete-ground set. It need not be x, and "no complete ground" does
not mean no partial reason, no efficient cause or no nonnecessitating account.
All admitted sets are quantified over, including infinite and empty ones;
there is no privileged selected witness whose failure is advertised as universal.

Proof: project R explicitly into an admitted-reason arena, preserving the same
worlds and truth. The existing `forced` descent lemma finds a contingent member
with no contingent predecessor. If any candidate complete set were admitted
there, its actual members would all be noncontingent, hence boxed true under
EM. Joint sufficiency would make that selected fact boxed true, contradicting
its accessible failure. This projection uses predecessors as structural/partial
reasons only. No member is individually asserted sufficient; sufficiency belongs
only to the WHOLE set. The original `ReasonClosed`, `nonCopy` and reason-coverage
hypotheses are unnecessary. No universal PSR is inserted.

The policy omissions retain a contingent target and an admitted full candidate
for every actual contingent fact:

| Omitted policy | Exact control | Why the derivation stops |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Actual member truth | a never-true member supports the contingent target under a vacuous conditional | Noncontingency without actual truth gives no boxed truth. |
| Membership→R linkage | every fact is admitted as its own singleton ground while R is empty | Empty R says nothing about the unlinked self-member. |
| Joint necessitation | empty candidate sets for contingent facts | Actuality and linkage are vacuous; the empty conjunction does not necessitate a contingent target. |
| WF | Nat successor predecessors, all facts true in one world and false in another | Every candidate singleton has an actual contingent predecessor; no termination. |

A separate one-fact brute-relative-to-G interpretation satisfies the theorem's
background with an actual contingent fact and no admitted ground. The chain's
facts are modally coextensive and its candidate grounds need not satisfy
independent relevance/essence/adequacy constraints. The false-member and
self-ground controls deliberately drop a policy; they are not endorsed complete
metaphysical explanations. No weakest philosophical premise set is claimed.

## D: enablers can hide a dependency cycle

Four distinct event labels have candidate ground-rule links baseA→effectA and
baseB→effectB, and enabling links effectB→effectA and effectA→effectB. A local
rule permits an effect exactly when its base and every enabler hold. Bases
remain true in BOTH assignments. All effects on and all effects off satisfy
those same equations. At the designated on world each effect is actually true
and fails at the accessible off world.

The raw ground-rule graph is WF and has no positive cycle. In the full union,
the distinct effects occupy one Mathlib strongly connected component; a positive
return path also directly witnesses non-WF. These are potential rule-dependency
links, not a claim that a ground actually explains an absent effect at the off
world. If enabling conditions are part of a complete explanans, omitting them
from the dependency relation can invalidate C's linkage/completeness reading.
If they are included, this profile loses WF. If a rival treats enabling as
nonexplanatory, it owes a different sufficiency account; the cycle does not
refute that rival by definition. Neither fixed assignment explains why that
assignment obtains, supplies a productive realization or establishes a temporal
causal loop. Transitivity is not assumed of immediate links. WF and no-cycles
are proved only of the named raw rule graph, not of all actual grounding.

A hypothetical lamp with an energy source and a closed switch illustrates why
multiple conditions can jointly matter, but no real lamp model or metaphysical
abstraction bridge is established here. The formal examples are the explicit
finite rules and omission interpretations above. Whether causal/grounding
relations in reality obey these policies remains OPEN.
