# Explanatory selection of a complete history (#736)

This investigation advances the explanatory-selection question on #613/#735,
with an informational discriminator and a stronger global-structure survivor.
It does **not** establish that an infinite regress is impossible, that an
ultimate adequate explanation must entail its outcome, or that metaphysical
incompleteness defeats induction (#737).

## Arena and exact new consequence

The shared `TemporalHistory.History W B T` fixes alternative indices, rigid
candidate identities, supplied times, a presence extension and a designated
now. `SameHistory` compares the entire supplied presence extension, not every
qualitative property of reality. A world variable designates actuality in a
model; it does not cause or explain it. Accessibility is supplied, not proved
to represent metaphysical possibility. No aggregate being is introduced.

The new `SameEvidence truth basis v w` compares **all** admitted token truth
values; `R` and `basis` have no finiteness bound. `EvidenceOnly p` means that
p's truth cannot vary while this entire vector stays unchanged. This permits
arbitrary infinitary recombination. It is not a restriction to a finite
observer, computable rule or inductive proof tree. `Determines` says that every
accessible world sharing that vector has the actual supplied history.
`Selects` reuses the existing entailing `Discriminates` standard.

`HISTORY-SELECTION-CERTIFICATE`, bound to
`TruthCore.Results.Explanation.HistorySelection.certificate`, certifies:

1. `Determines h truth basis a` iff some evidence-only proposition p is true
   at a and excludes every accessible different supplied history.
2. If an accessible different history shares the whole vector, **no** such p
   exists. Thus taking arbitrarily many invariant local reasons together,
   or applying a new truth-functional construction to them, cannot create an
   entailing answer to that contrast.
3. The controls below coexist with these constraints in their specified
   interpretations.

The forward proof chooses the evidence-equivalence class of a. Transitivity
and symmetry of pointwise biconditionals show that this candidate depends only
on the vector. The reverse proof transports its actual truth to any world
sharing that vector, then applies discrimination. The exclusion follows by
applying the characterization to the explicitly different accessible rival.
This is a consequence about arbitrary joint information, beyond #467's failure
of one constant token. It is not a classification of all possible explanations.
The converse also shows why an actuality marker or target copy can pass:
formal discriminatory success is **not sufficient for explanatory adequacy**.

## Decisive cases under one demand

### Local completeness and multiple histories

The existing `EssentialPowerUnderdetermined` certificate supplies distinct
all-on and all-off infinite transmission solutions. It is imported and used,
not reproved. Off is genuinely empty activity; it is not advertised as another
populated concrete world. The independent `HistoryContrastive.chains` controls
supply two populated Bool-tagged infinite member chains, with universal
accessibility, sound local reason coverage and an upstream cause for every
present member. Their presence histories differ. For constant reason truth,
the complete admitted vector agrees, so the new theorem excludes **every**
evidence-only entailing candidate, not just the original constant token.

These are abstract local laws and guarded relation assignments. Their causal
interpretation is a predecessor supplying an existing member; the certificate
does not establish that real biological, instrumental or metaphysical causes
have this form. The chicken/lizard contrast is represented only by different
supplied presence extensions; the model is not a theory of actual organisms.
Mathematical nonuniqueness does not establish metaphysical accessibility,
actual-world contingency, a common qualitative law across species, or actual
causal completeness. Those would be additional application premises.

### Unique global solution with no first stage

`GlobalLaw signal` imposes the already certified two-gate equations at **every
integer time**. The old unique-feedback theorem supplies consistency and
uniqueness pointwise; function extensionality gives exactly one complete signal,
`fixedSignal t = (true,false)`. For every integer time there is an earlier time.
The successor upstream graph has an upstream node at each stage and no source,
and the fixed signal copies unchanged along each link. There is no initial
stage or observed initial value.

This is the strongest supplied **structural** survivor: a nonempty,
beginningless, source-free copying signal, selected uniquely by an independent
global restriction. The copying equation alone does not imply that restriction.
The Boolean gates are logical constraints, **not** efficient causes or a
certified grounding mechanism. No first temporal stage does not establish
absence of a foundational mechanism. Nor does the theorem prove absence of
all alternative laws, that the supplied law is necessary, or that it is actual.

What has been answered is “which signal satisfies these constraints?” The
question “why do these constraints actually govern anything?” remains open.
A rival may defend the constraint structure's intrinsic explanatory status,
its necessity, or a non-well-founded realization. A critic needs an argument
against that account, rather than the false claim that every source-free rule
has multiple solutions. Conversely, writing down a unique solution cannot by
itself establish the actuality or adequacy of its rule.

### Grounded alternative under the same demand

`rooted` keeps **exactly** `chains.IsAt`, its now, time and candidate domains,
accessibility and both reason-truth assignments. Its only changed history field
is Causes: at world w, `(w,0)` exists, has no incoming cause, and is related as
cause to every other present node. Both endpoints of every edge exist. The
source and its outgoing relation are stipulated model structure, not an
independently defended efficient-causal interpretation.

With constant evidence truth, neither rooted nor source-free history is
`Determines`. With the existing internal contingent marker, **both** are
`Determines`. This is an actual composed comparison under identical supplied
explanandum and demand. A source is not automatically an answer to why its
own presence, nature, activity, law or selected outputs obtain. If its source
is brute, the remaining actuality question is explicitly unexplained. If its
source has an intrinsic sufficient reason, that reason needs independent
characterization and justification, under the same scope as the infinitist
rival. A proposed necessary God receives no exemption.

The certificate also imports #634's exact source-plus-infinite-branch witness.
A source reached by finite paths can coexist with an injective infinite
upstream branch. This is a separate existing interpretation, not a claim that
the new rooted star contains such a branch. Source existence, finite access to
a source, termination of all branches and explanatory adequacy remain distinct.

## What is independently defensible about a contrastive demand?

The modest argument is about **answer relevance**, rather than universal PSR:

- First fix a contrast: why this supplied obtaining arrangement rather than
  that one? This does not demand a causal producer of a set. A structured
  obtaining fact can be investigated through the arrangement and relations
  of its members, without another concrete object over and above them.
- If an account claims to answer that contrast completely, it owes something
  relevant to the difference. Repeating an account unchanged under substitution
  of the contrast has not yet displayed why the selected difference obtains.
  In an ordinary switch example, the wiring rule can explain transmission
  conditional on either setting; a setting, a bias, or an appropriate causal
  intervention may answer a different, more specific question.
- Under an **entailing** interpretation of complete selection, the theorem
  makes that relevance burden exact. No new construction depending only on
  the unchanged whole truth-vector can exclude the rival. A real further
  constraint, boundary condition, intrinsic nature or realized selector must
  add the missing discrimination, or the account must decline that standard.

This supports an objection to presenting mere invariant copying rules as an
already completed **entailing selection** answer. It does not independently
establish a universal metaphysical requirement that every good explanation
entail actuality. The ordinary switch case motivates the relevance demand in
that case; projecting it to every actual complete history remains **OPEN**.
The inference from local nonuniqueness to inadequacy is therefore conditional.

The **weakest live philosophical bridge** is: for this particular admitted
actual/rival contrast, a claimed complete ultimate account owes independently
warranted contrast-relevant explanatory content beyond facts that merely
restate the selected outcome. This is weaker than requiring that every
explanation necessitate its effect, that every fact have an explanation, or
that the ultimate reason be external, finite, necessary or a first cause.
It is a proposed obligation, not a proved mathematically weakest principle.
Whether the contrast is legitimate and the relevance demand applies universally
are disputed. To use the certified entailing discriminator one still needs the
**stronger specialization** that such a complete answer supplies an evidence-only
entailing candidate. The general relevance norm alone does not imply this.

This distinction matters for nonnecessitating explanations. A law can favor H
while allowing H', or identify a causal process that actually produced H, without
logically excluding H'. `HISTORY-SELECTION-OMISSIONS` supplies positive formal
weights 2 and 1: comparative favor and a surviving rival are compatible.
Those numbers do not establish real chances or explanatory adequacy. A full
chance/causal account must defend its law, direction, actuality and relevance.
Its comparative/intensional structure is not represented merely by which
reason tokens are true at worlds. Thus the truth-vector theorem does not
silently refute it. Conversely, if it answers only why H was favored, an
additional why-this-actual-outcome question may remain; defending that further
demand is philosophical work, not an arithmetic consequence.

No structural property of metaphysical explanation is silently assumed:
transitivity, asymmetry and WF do not occur in the informational criterion.
SameEvidence is symmetric/transitive because biconditionals are, not because
causation is. Rooted causal sourcehood is graph-relative; its relation is not
identified with explanatory sufficiency or the gates' logical dependency.
Ordinary examples motivate local interpretation only; their universalization
remains open. Serious rivals can deny entailing adequacy without inconsistency.

## Omission, vacuity and claim/type self-sweep

The second certificate, `HISTORY-SELECTION-OMISSIONS`, proves the accessibility,
distinctness, information-dependence and actual-truth controls. Removing the
accessible-rival condition allows the identity-accessibility model to determine
the actual component despite a different disconnected history. Removing
history difference permits the fully populated repeated-history model. Allowing
an additional target marker supplies selection but violates constant-vector
EvidenceOnly. Omitting actual reason truth lets False satisfy the discriminator's
implication vacuously; `Selects` correctly rejects it. These are explicit
omissions, not a claim that all hypotheses form a philosophically minimal core.

The generic exclusion supplies a and v; its different-history premise excludes
identical presence extensions. Concrete Bool models have populated chains and
universally accessible rivals, including reflexivity/seriality; their candidates
and times are inhabited. Identity accessibility is serial but the rival is
outside the actual component. No existence predicate is inferred from carrier
inhabitedness. The off activity control is empty activity, explicitly so; it is
not substituted for a populated alternate reality. The unique signal is a
mathematical function and not a real concrete instantiation. The source-overlap
model is a separate imported interpretation; witnesses from it are not combined
with the rooted star to claim an unproved joint model.

Claim/type comparison: the new universal quantifiers range over supplied typed
histories and truth vectors; no metaphysical modality or adequate-explanation
predicate is hidden. The iff proves existence of some selecting proposition,
not a unique independent explanatory nature or a particular source. The
actuality requirement includes actual candidate truth. The infinite recombination
claim follows from universal EvidenceOnly, not merely a per-token result.
The law's solution is unique **under its law**; only the Int time-order and
copying graph have the no-first/source-free properties. Sourcehood is proved
for the new rooted cause relation; no source's intrinsic adequacy is asserted.
Positive weights preserve two outcomes and prove no chance law or adequacy.
The linked definitions and disambiguations cover these readings. No attribution
is strengthened into a new open philosophical axiom.
