# Epistemic underminers and their scope

The common target is F6, `STMT-THERE-ARE-EPISTEMIC-REASONS`, which is the
existence of an agent and content related by the supplied thin reason predicate.
The existing corpus puts F6 on routes to `STMT-CATHOLICISM-TRUE`. The results
below assess challenges to reasons or reasoning. Only case 9 uses F6 itself as
a theorem premise. Each further link to F6 requires the specified bridge.
These are analyst readings. Existing source records retain their narrower
attributions.

| Case | Conditional consequence or exact reuse | Surviving alternative and condition for reassessment |

| 9. No epistemic reasons | The canonical `EpistemicNihilismRetorsion.nihilismSelfUndermining` excludes a reason to believe the universal denial if it holds. `f6ExcludesNihilism` instead uses an F6 witness to contradict the universal denial in that very relation. | The canonical one-agent model makes nihilism true without a reason to believe it. Dropping F6 preserves that model. The question of independent warrant for thin F6 is prior to using the incompatibility as a philosophical rebuttal. |
| 10. No justified beliefs | `eligibleReasonUptakeYieldsWarrant` transfers an eligible instance of uptake into actual warranted belief under the explicit calibration. `justificationOmissionControl` combines thin F6, actual belief and the calibration with no warranted belief. | A weak or defeated reason can favor a belief that fails the warrant standard. Establish an actual eligible uptake case and justify the warrant predicate's calibration before projecting to ordinary justification. A claim to have warrant would challenge universal absence only if the warrant were present. |
| 11. All reasoning unreliable | `reliableWitnessExcludesUnreliability` uses a single method clearing the selected nonempty benchmark. Distinguish a benchmark result from reliability across relevant cases and from the subject's warrant to trust a method. The missing link to F6 is a calibrated actual method or evidence whose performance counts in favor of a belief. |

| 13. The general retorsion distinction is already explicit in
`TruthCore.Alternatives.Retorsion.PresuppositionLink`. A performance can refute
the denial of its own sort only if the token belongs to that sort. For example,
uttering the denial of warranted belief does not supply a warranted belief.
Likewise the production of an inferential token does not certify the token's
reliability or the validity of its inference. A reflective concession can be
tested where the relevant status is actually claimed and warranted.

For the warrant and reliability bridges, a discriminating comparison is an
otherwise comparable competent count versus an attractive but uninformative
prompt, as in the [F6 investigation](epistemic-reasons-preconditions.md).
Sufficient evidence bearing on the answer supplies a candidate for warrant.
The constant-answer model explains why high observed accuracy alone leaves the
connection open. For demonstrative knowledge, a further test is to specify
what certifies an independently known premise or how a nonterminating chain
confers warrant, then check that account against the same dependency and
knowledge predicates. See the existing [justification introspection
investigation](carneades-epistemology-introspection.md) for the separate
question of knowing one's justification and [the Wittgenstein note](../wittgenstein.md)
for the hinge readings.

The narrow incompatibilities stand within their respective profiles. The
stronger claim that these profiles establish actual justification, global
method reliability, categorical standards, or a warrant-bearing hinge remains
open. The strongest rival depends on the scope. It is a reasonless truth for
case 9 without F6, thin pro tanto reasons without warranted beliefs for case
10, aim-relative merit for case 13, strong infinitism for case 15, and
suspension without active universal doubt for case 16.
