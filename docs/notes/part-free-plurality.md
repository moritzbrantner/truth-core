## Decisive test

`TruthCore.Causality.PartFreePlurality.JointControl` is one fixed interpretation
of the existing generic interfaces. Its five beings are `left`, `right`,
`constituent`, `instrument`, `effect`, with ranks 0, 0, 1, 2, 3. Strict rank increase
is selected as simple priority and ordinary efficient cause/support. A component
additionally has positive rank. Thus the constituent is a component of instrument
and effect, and the instrument a component of effect. Neither root is a
component of anything. Both composites remain actual distinct-esse subjects
with distinct actual efficient causes. Components themselves have predecessors,
so the universal q.3 a.8 no-component-is-first-being condition holds.

The carrier is finite assignment data, not new philosophical vocabulary. The
three respects and motion/act/per-se predicates are reused from #681 by an
explicit erasure mapping constituent to instrument. Both constituent and instrument
are moved in drive and move the effect in heat. This preserves a nonempty
per-se relation and its well-foundedness. A finite rank proves well-foundedness;
no empty motion relation or absent dependent population does the work. The extra `constituent` is assigned the existing modeled body/potency predicates
through the explicit projection; its label supplies no doctrine of prime matter. Its represented essence and esse agree,
although it has efficient predecessors: no converse of q.3 a.4 is smuggled in.

Separating efficient cause from component is warranted by the existing types:
q.3 a.7's component-priority premise has two independent relation parameters,
and q.3 a.8 constrains components, not every efficient cause. This is an
interpretation of those interfaces, not an independently sourced thesis that
arbitrary rank relations correctly explain causation or parthood.
