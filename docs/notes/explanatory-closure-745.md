# Must a complete cause account for its indispensable conditions? (#745)

Result `EXPLANATORY-CLOSURE-CERTIFICATE`, module `TruthCore.Explanation.ExplanatoryClosure`.
Semantic profile: the #733 `Exercise` records, #734 `Supply`, the #755 arena. No source
reading is claimed here. Rival names below orient the reader and are not attributions.

## Finding

The demand splits by scope. Applied to every contingent event it is the contingent-fact
PSR of the record arena (`Collapse`, given coherence and records with a member). Applied
to one target it is weaker (`bare` has `a` closed and no PSR), unless that target's records
list every other contingent event (`TotalTarget`). It does not follow from completeness of
one record (`bare`), and it does not give foundation (`chain`, `cycle`).

## Meaning of the relations

| Term | Ordinary sense | Example | Near miss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Complete cause | Given these, the effect follows in the supplied scenarios | Spark, fuel and oxygen for a fire | Conditional: says nothing about where the oxygen came from |
| Indispensable condition | Remove it and the rest can occur with no effect | Oxygen, in scenarios where it can be absent | Relative to the scenario space; a fixed background is not indispensable |
| Closed | Every condition in the account is accounted for inside it, or stops | A fire account that also traces spark, fuel and oxygen | Permits a circle or an endless regress |
| Inductive closure | Every condition derives from stops by a well-founded derivation | A blueprint built up from named starting points | Stops may be arbitrary, as the `p` stop shows |
| Stop | Where this account asks no further | Scenario necessity, or a stated initial condition | Being a stop does not make it self-explanatory |

Counterexample to reading a record as production: an analyst can list an idle event as a
member, and the table will not object (#744). Hence closure is a statement about listed
records and the records stay interpreted data.

Structure: nothing assumes the reason relation transitive, asymmetric or well-founded.
Foundation enters only as `FullSupply`, and `Ladder` and `Fork` state exactly what separates it from
closure. Generalizing from ordinary explanation is not licensed: ordinary science stops at
stated conditions, which here is the stop predicate containing `p`.

## Strongest cases

- *For the demand:* an ultimate explanation, by the meaning of "ultimate", leaves no
  contingent condition open. Then failure of closure at a target removes that label, and
  `bare` is the case. This makes the demand a definition of the label, and says nothing on whether
  anything has an ultimate explanation.
- *Against:* conditional explanation is the only kind in use, and a demand beyond it needs a
  reason. The memberwise reply (each member explained, nothing more owed) passes closure in
  `chain`, so closure alone does not defeat it.

## The recursive step

Candidates for a justification independent of a PSR, and what each gives:

1. *Definition of "ultimate".* Gives the label only.
2. *PSR.* In the universal scope this is `Collapse`, so it is not independent of a PSR.
3. *Transmission of borrowed power (#734).* Concerns contributors. An enabler need not exercise
   power, so it does not reach `p`.
4. *Scenario indispensability.* Restricting the same records to the actual scenario removes it
   (`narrowBare`), and equal tables with different records change the verdict.
5. *Treating a cycle as one unit (#752).* Condensation gives the component `Supply` with no
   intrinsic event, while `FullSupply` fails in the original records.

None was found to justify the step independently. This is a report on these candidates, not
an impossibility claim. No premise demanding closure is added.

## Trilemma and its costs

For a contingent event of a coherent exercise, with stops at scenario necessity: a contingent
ancestor without a record; an infinite descent in an accounted set; or an incomplete ancestor
record (`Trilemma`). Branches may overlap. `TrilemmaControls` has each alone. `LinkCost` is the
joint-record relative of #750's necessitation clause: reasons there necessitate one by one,
whereas here members need only do so jointly. It forces no choice among the branches.

## Remaining gaps

- Whether closure is owed by ultimate explanation is open and unproved in either direction.
- Productive membership is undefended, so a closed account may list idle events.
- Bearer existence is untreated; a power enters only as a listed event.
- Stop predicates other than scenario necessity, and which are legitimate, are not ranked.
- `StoppingPoints` (#770) and `DerivativeReply` (#776) were not on main when this started.
