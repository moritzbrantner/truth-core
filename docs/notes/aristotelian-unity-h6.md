# Aristotle's H.6 unity explanation and the causal bridge

## Arena and witness

This bounded comparison treats Aristotelianism as a positive explanatory proposal,
not as a synonym for Thomism or a route whose Catholic conclusion is presumed.
The source is the complete Greek *Metaphysics* H.6 (VIII.6), materialized as
`ARIST-MET-VIII-6` from the existing immutable Ross/Perseus edition
`urn:cts:greekLit:tlg0086.tlg025.perseus-grc2`. The new passage manifest pins the
same repository revision/blob used elsewhere; no translation or modern
commentator is substituted for the consulted witness. The whole chapter was read,
including its opening puzzle and closing qualification. The four claims are
prose source readings: no theorem, proof of source fidelity, or established
metaphysical sufficiency is advertised.

## What the argument commits to

1. **An explanandum, with a condition** (1045a8–14). Where parts make a whole
   that is not a heap but something beyond its parts, Aristotle asks for the
   cause of its unity. Contact and stickiness already illustrate that the word
   *cause* is not here automatically a distinct transcendent agent. This is not
   the unrestricted claim that every mathematical fusion needs an external cause.
2. **A particular rival's missing answer** (1045a14–25). Separate Animal-itself
   and Biped-itself, appealed to in the customary way, leave the question why a
   human is one rather than two. This challenges an explanatory proposal, not
   every possible Platonist reply or all separate Forms. A Platonist may defend
   a stronger account of participation and unity; that defense is still owed.
3. **A positive replacement** (1045a23–33; 1045b17–21). Proximate matter and form
   are one as potentiality and actuality. The proposed solution does not begin
   with two independent actual beings and then add a third tie. It retains a
   material/formal distinction without unqualified identity of every matter and
   form, and does not stipulate any observed arrangement to be a substance.
4. **The causal qualification** (1045a29–33; 1045b17–23). The producer remains in
   cases of generation, and the closing answer allows a mover from potency to
   actuality. Thus “no further cause” cannot be quoted as “the generated whole
   is uncaused.” Conversely the passage does not by itself yield an additional,
   numerically distinct actual cause sustaining the whole's *esse*.

The chapter also extends the puzzle to intelligible matter and definitions;
its claim is not restricted to living organisms. Its immaterial cases at
1045a35–b7 are not a proof here that particular immaterial beings actually exist.
No Thomistic essence–esse distinction is attributed to this argument.

## Exact reuse and the remaining bridge

`STMT-FORM-MORE-NATURE-THAN-MATTER` is bound to
`TruthCore.Greek.Aristotle.Physics.FormMoreNatureThanMatterReading` (Physics II.1,
193b6–8): comparative priority of form through actuality, not elimination of
matter. H.6 supplies a distinct unity argument, so it is not silently assigned
that statement's identity.

`STMT-MEREO-SOUL-UNITY-INDEPENDENCE` is certified by
`TruthCore.Results.Mereology.SoulUnityIndependence.soulUnityIndependence`.
Its finite scenarios preserve operational life/organization while varying
substantial unity and irreducible soul. It establishes underdetermination in
that language, not an Aristotelian doctrine or a refutation of immanent form.
H.6's positive potential/actual proposal must explain more than a name for an
unexplained observable pattern; the scenario does not automatically disprove it.

`STMT-COMPOSITE-HAS-A-UNITING-CAUSE` names
`TruthCore.Latin.Aquinas.SummaTheologiae.CompositeHasAUnitingCauseReading`.
Its existing source interpretation takes the cause to be other than the whole
and to cause its being as one thing, represented by `CausesIn` at *esse*.
The H.6 opening does not alone prove that stronger causal-role and distinctness
reading, nor does its closing reply refute every such reading. The precise
missing mappings are: the admitted bearer and relevant composition; the
explanatory role of matter/form; the relation of a producer/mover to a cause
in *esse*; and any actuality/same-world causal interface used in the modern
regress model. No mapping is installed as a new premise for convenience.

These proof boundaries suffice for this source comparison. A fresh theorem with
opaque unity/cause predicates would merely repackage the missing bridge, so none
is added. `ARISTOTELIAN-UNITY-CAUSE-IN-ESSE-BRIDGE` records the next substantive
investigation rather than pretending the question has been settled.

## Shared questions and strong alternatives

| Question | Aristotelian H.6 reply | Strong alternative and remaining test |
|---|---|---|
| Q-COMPOSITION | Matter/form, potential/actual, explain the specified whole without treating them as two independently actual objects. | A simple-arrangement account can offer organized dynamics without an extra material bearer. Whether that exhausts the relevant explanation remains open on both sides. |
| Q-UNITY | The proximate matter/form account replaces a further tie; the initial nonheap condition is substantive. | A Platonist can enrich participation; a structural account can defend an independently warranted organization criterion. Neither is eliminated by a verbal puzzle alone. |
| Q-LIFE-SOUL | This chapter uses living and nonliving examples; it does not establish an irreducible soul, or restrict all unity to life. | Material-process, vitalist and non-soul substantial readings must answer the same life/unity question. Existing finite controls preserve their distinction. |
| Q-IDENTITY | Being one through potential/actual aspects is not a cross-time persistence criterion. | Material or process continuation theories remain open; H.6 does not decide the Theseus puzzle. |
| Q-GROUNDING | A positive account of formal/material unity, retaining a mover exception. | Fusion laws, causal priority and metaphysical grounding remain separate. No efficient-cause interface follows from the word *form*. |
| Q-EXPLANATION | The account claims to dissolve a unity question by its positive ontology. | Its adequacy must be defended against the objection that potential/actual description merely relocates the question; rival arrangements/participation face equivalent adequacy tests. |
| Q-ULTIMATE | No conclusion here establishes one ultimate ground, actual necessary being, or God. | Platonist, materialist, pluralist and theistic ultimate accounts are unresolved by this passage alone. |

## Premise comparison and fidelity sweep

The four new statements preserve source conditions and interpretive scope.
The claims neither close their targets nor attach new inferences to the root.
All existing root premises are retained; no premise is added to or discharged
from a root argument. This comparison identifies the open causal bridge and
rejects two overreadings, not a whole philosophical system. No “source asserts
it” step is presented as proof of a premise's truth.

No matter/form identity is inferred without qualification; no existential
witness is obtained from a general explanatory account; generation and ongoing
existence are distinct; and the source's mover exception is preserved. The
old causal-source proofs are not re-run or relabeled. Final CI owns integration
checks of the delivered source/record revision.
