The main distinction is between a consequential choice of meaning and an already explicit mathematical/interface choice. It supplies neither reductive identity nor a perception/justification theorem. - Moral/descriptive/propositional roles: descriptive and permission-plan fields are independent; material support is stipulated, not Gibbard indicative semantics. Free-standing attitude expression is distinct from reporting an attitude. Primitive validity markers are never inferred from meaning constancy; the existing guarded loss route and nonempty overlap controls retain the distinction. - Scoring and hard cases: positive goods, factual harm, prohibition, categorical reason, feasibility and ideal successor duty remain separate. Common cardinal interpersonal scales and representation invariance are proposed standards, not derived normative authority. Rowe's apparent/actual blocking costs and balance-neutral refinement retain distinct premises.

```lean
CorrectAccountExists s →
  ∃ d, s.admissible d ∧ CorrectAccount s d ∧ SemanticallyNamed s d
```
