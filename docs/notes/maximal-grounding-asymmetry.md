# Grounding asymmetry at the maximum actually used

The actual-use audit of `DEF-ACTUAL-GROUNDING-ASYMMETRIC` inspected the exact
maximum/root helper in `WHOLE-ROSTER-FROM-MAXIMUM-ASYMMETRY` and both downstream
consumers: `DIRECT-ESSE-ROOT-UNIQUENESS-FROM-ASYMMETRY` and
`NECESSARY-CLASSICAL-GOD-ROOT-FROM-ASYMMETRY`. Their asymmetry applications always
involve the SAME maximal-in-esse candidate supplied by the other premises.
None needs to exclude reciprocal links between every pair of dependents.

`STMT-ACTUAL-GROUNDING-ASYMMETRIC-AT-MAXIMAL-TARGET` is a separate weaker
framework input. For actual concrete a,b at the selected world, a grounding
arrow a→b cannot have its reverse when b is maximal under the SAME supplied
`InAct`, `MoreIn` and `esse` dictionary. All other target pairs are unconstrained.
The sole-premise strength theorem restricts the old universal policy. This is
not a redefinition of grounding, maximality, roothood or God. The criterion is
independent of which being is eventually shown to be a root; it is the same
maximum predicate already consumed by genus causality and grades-to-maximum.
No truth or independent warrant is claimed for either asymmetry policy.

The proof first obtains a maximum using the existing `actualFork` and explicit
EM, population/existing-to-esse and fixed-esse grades-to-maximum. If it differs
from the original actual witness, genus causality and the direct esse-to-ground
bridge make the SAME maximum actual concrete. Any incoming actual ground to
that maximum either is a self-edge, excluded by the weak policy, or obtains a
reverse arrow from the same genus/esse bridge and is again excluded. The maximum
is therefore an actual selected root. This structural step explicitly adapts
the old maximum/root proof; its stronger theorem is not misapplied to a weak
premise.
