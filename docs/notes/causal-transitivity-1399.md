## Selected meanings and exclusions

`Variable` indexes magnitudes X,Y,Z, not three substances, existence facts or
causal powers. `Model` supplies one fixed background, actual X, and total Boolean
functions f and g with recursive equations Y=f(X), Z=g(X,Y). There is no noise,
feedback, temporal dynamics or unspecified further parent. `actual` evaluates
these equations. `intervene v b` replaces v's equation with the constant b,
then recomputes descendants; it does not hold descendants at their actual values.
Intervening on Y leaves X actual. Intervening on Z leaves both earlier equations
in force. This is an endogenous equation replacement, not removal of a bearer,
replacement of an event token, or a change of exogenous background.

`ButFor m a b` is its own Prop structure: a and b differ, and replacing a by the
opposite of its actual Boolean value changes b from its actual value. The actual
state is model-indexed and shared by both links. A diagonal test is excluded
rather than treating self-intervention as self-causation. The Boolean complement
is stipulated; multivalued contrasts require a specified alternative policy.

A total X effect compares g(x,f(x)) with g(!x,f(!x)). A controlled direct effect
at y' compares g(x,y') with g(!x,y'), retaining that same mediator value. The
mediated link at actual x compares g(x,y) with g(x,!y). These are distinct
comparisons, not a numerical decomposition of productive power. `inputGraph`
uses canonical `Graph`; declared X→Y, X→Z, Y→Z slots may be functionally idle.
Its ancestry uses the existing canonical nonempty closure. Reachability therefore
need not be effective dependence even when direction and recursion are fixed.

A switch arrangement motivates response dependence; an independent parallel
route can mask it. Those are illustrations, not verified physical realizations
of this table. Productive causation instead asks which concrete process or
exercise brings about the effect. Existential grounding asks on what a thing's
being depends, possibly with impossible absence antecedents. Neither is defined
by this intervention structure. Canonical #733 groups retain conjunctive
contributors and separate enablers; their projection cannot assert that each
arrow suffices individually. This family has no new primitive claiming actual
production, grounding, exercise or explanatory adequacy.

## Strong positive rivals and argument walk

A dependency-chain account can define indirect causation as a finite chain of
local dependencies. It preserves the positive claim that an effect can be
causally downstream even if another path cancels its total response. Transitivity
then follows by chain concatenation. Its price is explicit: indirect causation
is no longer identical to endpoint but-for dependence. The XNOR model survives
both accounts with different answers to different questions. To count ancestry
as productive, the account additionally owes evidence that each edge represents
actual exercise and that the composed links transmit the relevant production
rather than mere recording, enabling or correlation. Concatenating edge witnesses
alone does not prove that semantic transmission principle.

A productive-powers account locates causation in exercised capacities and
organized transmission, potentially allowing redundant or masked causes. Its
positive replacement is an account of how the outcome is produced, not merely
what would differ under equation replacement. It can retain transitivity for
appropriately composed productive dependence under independent premises of
exercise, same respect, same effect and composition. Objection: cancellation,
preemption and joint contributors make an unrestricted individual-arrow rule
suspect. Reply: distinguish productive contribution from total difference-making,
and retain whole groups where required. OPEN: an operational or philosophical
criterion of exercise that discriminates a producing arrangement from idle
co-occurrence; #733/#752 show why topology alone cannot provide it.

An existential-grounding account explains why an existing bearer has being,
rather than why a magnitude takes one value. It may defend transitivity through
composition of full grounds; partial grounds and mixed causal/grounding links
need separate treatment. Positive motivation: dependent exercise presupposes
an existing exerciser and instruments may require concurrent sustaining support.
Objection: the continued existence of an instrument does not show that its
upstream signal source sustains its being. Reply requires an explicit
existential support premise, not renaming the signal arrow. A lamp can remain
an existing object when switched off, illustrating the missing identification
without claiming a physical countermodel to all grounding theories.

Descending to the target premise exposes three distinct gaps: (1) map event or
variable tokens to concrete bearers and identify the relevant efficient cause
in the same respect; (2) show the selected cause sustains the effect's being,
not merely its value or coming-to-be; (3) justify the absence counterfactual,
including whether it is an admissible intervention and what it means if the
cause cannot be absent. An opposite Boolean value retains the variable and its
carrier, so it cannot fill (2) or (3). No amount of further Boolean enumeration
would supply that abstraction bridge. Neither transitivity nor its failure
implies well-foundedness, a unique foundation, PSR or a necessary being.

Historical Lewis/Hall/Hitchcock leads in #1399 remain leads, not authenticated
claims made here. Their acquisition is not on the logic critical path.

Under classical reasoning the loss principle and absence exclude cessation;
this is the previously recorded contraposition, not a new nontranscription
result. Importantly, those premises do NOT assert absence of a conserver.
History can be an explanation while conservation also occurs. To infer that
there is no concurrent source needs a separate exclusion argument. To say
history is sufficient without *invoking* that source is a weaker epistemic
claim; to say its law holds independently of any such source is a substantive
metaphysical strengthening. The common same-bearer assumptions imply neither.

**Testing the law rather than its label.** A record of b's past qualitative
states alone gives no rule licensing its continuation. Adding a law can add
content when its domain and loss conditions are specified independently of
this later outcome: it licenses further unobserved continuations and excludes
otherwise admissible uncaused cessations. Its truth conditions then exceed
“b exists at t1”. This distinguishes an explanatory candidate from a disguised
restatement; it does not establish its truth or explanatory adequacy. The
inspected materials supply an argument from the causal requirement on change,
not independent evidence that this requirement covers loss of received esse.
Extending it from changes *of* a subject to cessation *of* that subject is the
precise burden. Observed persistence alone cannot discriminate: the conservation
account predicts persistence too. An independently supported law could explain
nomologically while leaving its own ground unexplained; whether that suffices
for the present question depends on whether the demand is for this continuation,
the law's ground, or an ultimate explanation. Those demands cannot be exchanged
mid-argument. No function called “adequate” is evidence for any of them.

**Strongest conservation reply and its limit.** Feser's recorded reply is not
that history is irrelevant. It challenges whether prior actual combination
accounts for actual combination now; q.104 adds the participated-esse reason
why naturally stable form does not supply its own esse. On the shared case,
this provides a positive constitutive question: what accounts for the actuality
of this recipient, rather than just its determination as this kind of thing?
A reasoned conservation inference needs (a) present received actuality is
currently derivative, (b) its adequate dependence account must be efficient
actualization, and (c) the giving is concurrent with possession. Essence–esse
nonidentity and receipt at t0 do not alone establish (a)–(c). Treating all later
actuality as a fresh transition from sheer potency would beg the history rival's
objection; treating conservation as continued giving avoids recreation but
still requires these premises. Constitutive dependence may instead be on a
form/organization or a non-efficient ground; those accounts owe an account of
actuality and cannot be converted into a producing agent by terminological
substitution. A causal-generative explanation of origin, a law-based explanation
of continuation, and a constitutive account of present unity answer different
questions and can coexist. Their scopes do not by themselves decide sufficiency.

The persistence-power objection has genuine restricted force: if a power's
exercise ontologically requires b's existence, it cannot, under the same
asymmetric dependence account, efficiently produce that prerequisite existence.
The extrinsic historical-law account does not posit that exercise and therefore
does not inherit that circularity. A further argument that laws require
currently exercised bearer powers could restore the objection, but neither
the bare law nor the inspected circularity argument supplies it. Conversely,
calling the law extrinsic does not prove that it governs received esse without
concurrent production. Neither reply defeats its strongest opponent here.

**Full proposed assessment and disposition.** The test clarifies a genuine
criterion: a law independently delimited across cases has more explanatory
content than a prior qualitative record or an outcome-specific no-change
sentence. No such independently warranted existential law is established by
the inspected evidence. Conservation's current-actualization requirement also
remains substantive, not demonstrated by rejecting self-producing powers.
Retain underdetermination between these explanations on the SAME received
bearer. Do not infer a winner from explanatory vocabulary, PSR alone, origin,
or observed survival. The earlier preference for received-act as the strongest
available *conditional conservation rationale* remains that restricted ranking;
it is not comparative defeat of the historical account. No argument here
settles impossible-absence semantics or c0d99549's separate every-r obligation.
