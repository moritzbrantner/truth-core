## Strong case, rivals and exact target

The strongest inherited case is not merely that nature looks orderly. There
are natural bodies regularly attaining what is best; regularity is not chance;
the best regular outcome is either chance or tending-to-an-end; a body without
cognition tends only under a knowing intelligent director. Reduction supplies
an unmoved director for that same natural body and end, and directing-as-moving
then supplies its actual moving. The new proof imports `eachDirected`, chooses
one observed natural body, and applies those two bridge premises. It never
compares two candidate directors, so uniqueness is not used.

The result is the existing first mover moved in no respect: a witness that
moves something and is moved in none of the supplied respects. It establishes
neither modal necessary existence nor concreteness, one universal director,
classical attributes, Christianity or Catholicism. The later root routes still
retain their identification and attribute obligations. The separate
`governorProjects` assembly exposes the same witness in the existing stronger
governor conclusion, making that previously idle result available to the case.
Its result is labelled transcription/assembly; the projection by itself is not
a new philosophical discharge.

The serious rivals attack different steps. A selection/necessity interpretation
can grant regular best outcomes while denying the chance-or-intention dichotomy.
An immanent-teleology/teleonomy interpretation can grant tending while denying a
knowing external director. Mutable directors undermine reduction to an unmoved
one; a directing relation that is not the supplied motion relation undermines
the bridge to the first-mover target. Several unmoved directors challenge
one-governor scope but need not challenge existential first-mover status.
Neither the archer analogy nor these finite interpretations establishes any
empirical biological or cosmological interpretation.
