# Modal grounding: truth necessity, relation persistence and common priority (#950)

The question is whether all possible existential-support arrows among the SAME
rigid necessary bearers must fit one well-founded priority order. The selected
primary texts discuss materially different modal constraints. None of the selected
arguments supplies the missing common-rank bridge, or the no-contingent-supporter
premise. This is a result of these readings, not a proof that such a defense is
impossible or that the authors reject common rank.

## Semantic grounding and limits

`supportsEsse` is the existing primitive: a direct contributor to a bearer's
existence, not an ordinary efficient cause, a temporal ancestor, an explanation
token, a true proposition or an essence. Its applicability remains OPEN. Examples
of the intended contrast are continuing existential support versus an earlier
producer after which an effect persists; neither everyday example proves that
the intended support interpretation applies metaphysically.

A common rank means that one world-independent priority assignment decreases on
every possible necessary-to-necessary support edge. It is compatible with varying
edges (varying model). World-relative rankings alone allow reversal (rotating) or
a cycle-free infinite combined regress (parity). The codomain's well-foundedness
is explicit; transitivity or totality of support is not assumed. A rank need not
be injective; its codomain relation need not be a linear order. No temporal or
causal actuality of mixed-world paths is claimed. Unrestricted union-WF includes
no source attribution and no independent warrant.

TruthNecessitation is an analyst existence-fact view: if the edge occurs at w,
source existence entails target existence in every world accessible FROM w. This
constrains truth values only. It neither establishes the edge's persistence nor
defines its explanatory fullness. Our controls have universal accessibility and
all bearers present, so target implication is immediate. A type-level proposition
or a mathematical support edge is not a full ground merely because this test
holds. Translating plural fact-grounding to single-bearer existential support,
including completeness and enabling conditions, remains OPEN.

NecessarySupportStable is a separately stipulated persistence policy on edges
between necessary bearers, guarded by existence of both relata in the destination
world. It plus actual WF and selected-world reflexivity entails union-WF by the
old `union_wellFounded_of_actual_wellFounded`; no contingent-supporter premise is
used for that structural edge. It is a specialization, not a faithful identity
with any source's full plural-fact account.

## Primary readings and rejected conflations

All three whole PDF witnesses were pinned and materialized. The readings below
are confined to the listed argument contexts; references or unexamined book
arguments are not represented as read. Source statements are prose-only. Formal
support policies are openly analyst configurations, not what a source literally
states about typed Ontic bearers.

* **Trogdon, Grounding: Necessary or Contingent? (2013), pp.466–467 (PDF2–3).**
  His target is (iii): full grounds' propositions modally entail target truth,
  distinct from (i), persistence of the grounding claim itself, and (ii), some
  grounded fact. At p.467 he explicitly observes that a necessary target can
  have merely partial grounds despite modal entailment. The full/partial
  distinction is explanatory, not purely modal. His proposed route through
  significance, essence and necessity of essential truths adds substantive
  principles; it is not a common-rank argument. The pp.478–479 response to
  purported counterexamples looks for additional explanatorily relevant enabling
  or totality facts. It does not license silently adding them to our support
  relation. Formal TruthNecessitation is only an existence-fact specialization
  of a modal condition, not an implementation or refutation of his full account.
* **Leuenberger, Grounding and Necessity (2014), §I (PDF7–8), §III (PDF18–19),
  §IV (PDF20).** Entailment and Internality are explicitly distinct and claimed
  logically independent even given Factivity. The blocker/blocker-blocker cases
  target them separately. Supervenience of Grounding instead fixes grounding
  when ALL facts agree; it does not fix it when only bearer-existence facts agree.
  He excludes grounding-relation instances from his fact base, avoiding a trivial
  supervenience claim. Our rotating existence reduct is not thereby a model of
  his preferred whole-fact-base supervenience: other facts may distinguish its
  worlds. No checked refutation of this preferred account is advertised.
* **Richardson, Grounding is Necessary and Contingent (2021), §6.1 (PDF14–18;
  pp.465–469), §6.2 (PDF19–21; pp.470–472), conclusion (PDF26–27).** What-grounding
  is connected to essential real definitions, stronger than mere necessary
  coextension and not an endorsement of every example offered. Why-grounding
  admits nonnecessitation with contrastive difference-making conditions; its
  correspondence to metaphysical why-explanation is a working hypothesis.
  Arbitrary rotating support need not satisfy those conditions. His pluralist
  distinction blocks treating every use of 'grounding' as one relation, but
  does not independently defend a common WF cross-world rank.

The source dossier thus supports separating questions, not declaring one school
refuted. Relations with essence, real definition, relevance, completeness or
whole-fact supervenience may impose further restrictions; their precise bridge
to the selected necessary-bearer support union remains to be independently
argued or separately stipulated.
