# Occurrence, necessary ancestry and the same underived individual (#701)

## Exact bridge and result

`OCCURRENCE-SAME-WITNESS-ESSENCE` joins the #536/#564 thinker-local
route to #680's **named** q3 helper, rather than composing two existential
conclusions and identifying their witnesses. One `BruteFoundationBoundary.Profile`
supplies the selected world, rigid candidate identity, existence, concreteness
and grounding. The q3 profile uses that same individual type; its represented
Principle and Respect sorts remain separate. No actual-world warrant is inferred
from designating a world or constructing a model.

The original #564 `ancestryRootFork` already descends by `Acc` until it finds
an actual concrete ancestor with no actual concrete ground. Its old necessary
horn discarded that last fact. `ancestryRootForkWithRoot` retains it;
`necessaryConcreteRootAncestor` closes the contingent-root horn using the
**existing** thinker-local anti-brute premise. The two old advertised theorems
now project their original conclusions from these shared helpers. Their frozen
Challenges, hypotheses and conclusions are unchanged; the descent is not copied
into a new parallel necessity proof.

The new, explicitly open `ActualSupportIsConcreteGround` says that every
**existing** R-supporter of an existing concrete target at this world is itself
concrete and grounds that target there. Minimality then excludes actual incoming
R-support to the selected n. `actual_not_not_identity_direct` from #680 is
imported and applied to **that very n** with its actual existence and absence of
support. Its three existing actuality/cause interfaces and two q3 commitments
are retained. Explicit excluded middle gives positive represented equality.
The output carries n's ancestry, concreteness, actual existence, accessibility-
relative necessity, ground minimality, actual R-underivedness and identity in
one tuple. The resulting equality is not an explanation of actuality, a
uniqueness theorem, pure actuality or an identification with God.

The sufficient bridge has real content: it excludes abstract supporters of
concrete targets and support omitted by the chosen grounding relation. A theory
with abstract grounds, or a narrower reading of concrete grounding than
existential support, can deny it. Conversely an ontology in which R is already
actual concrete existential grounding may defend it, but must independently
warrant this interpretation and the concrete status of its supporters. Merely
naming an arbitrary edge 'support' cannot do that. No claim is made that this
bridge is the uniquely weakest philosophical bridge.

witness of its existential conclusion. #686's `actual_source_not_not` can
produce another source from necessary population; identifying that output
with an independently selected necessary ancestor would be invalid. Neither
the new proof nor its premise list takes this shortcut. The original
`from_union_direct` remains correct for its own selected source under its exact
union-route hypotheses, and is not silently applied to the thinker witness.

`OCCURRENCE-NECESSARY-RECIPIENT-CONTROL` reuses #680's `varying` ontology,
`varyingSupport` and `pairProfile`. Both rigid individuals exist at both
universally accessible worlds. At the selected world true, false supports true.
An occurring thought is borne by true. Both are concrete in this supplied
interpretation, its grounding is well-founded, and every candidate is necessary,
so the local anti-brute-contingent premise holds non-vacuously with respect to
the inhabited ontology (there are no contingent ancestors).

All six non-logical #564 premises hold. The recipient true **itself** witnesses
the old necessary-ancestor conclusion using reflexive ancestry, but has incoming
actual support and distinct represented esse. The SAME interpretation contains
source false, with no actual incoming support and represented identity. Thus
necessity of the chosen recipient cannot replace minimality; the new refined
walk selects the source instead. This does not show that the old theorem's
particular proof term must choose the recipient. It shows that its **type**
allows that witness and therefore does not expose the property needed by the
next proof. The new stronger type and retained shared proof resolve that loss.
These are formal interpretations, not independently established metaphysical
possibilities. The model's universal accessibility is a control choice, not an
assumption of the general theorem.

## Shared vocabulary and composition refactor (#713)

The union-support, selected-world-support and occurrence routes repeated two
pieces of the same composition. Their supporter-exclusion formulas had no
shared pointwise name, and each independently converted the constructive q3
`¬¬` consequence to identity using the already explicit excluded-middle premise.

`NecessarilyUnderived.NoExistingSupportAt m R w n` now names exactly
`∀ c, m.ExistsAt w c → ¬ R w c n`. Its curried guard preserves the original
formulas by definitional equality. It does **not** assert that n exists, is
necessary, or has no supporters at other worlds. Global named underivedness
still quantifies this predicate over **every admitted world**; the selected-world
and occurrence conclusions use it only at w. “Existing” is world-relative
existence, not temporal concurrence. R remains a supplied support relation,
separate from ordinary causes and concrete grounds.

The new `UnderivedEssenceEsse.actual_identity_direct` helper composes the
unchanged constructive `actual_not_not_identity_direct` with the existing
`ExcludedMiddleIffDne.excludedMiddleIffDoubleNegationElimination` theorem.
It still takes the same named n, its existence and supporter exclusion, explicit
excluded middle, and all five existing q3 commitments/interfaces. It proves no
new support interface. The three consumers now finish by applying this helper:
`UnderivedEssenceEsse.from_union_direct`, `ActualUnderivedEssenceEsse.checks`
and `OccurrenceUnderivedEssence.checks`. Their distinct witness-selection steps
remain visible; none may substitute the witness selected by another route.

For example, after selecting the same necessary ancestor and obtaining its

repeats a local double-negation elimination. The ordinary-cause/support

This is a code/vocabulary refactor with **no premise change**. Existing theorem
