# An intrinsic reason for the necessary ground: positive case and proof boundary

This is a bounded source-and-warrant investigation of
`STMT-NECESSARY-GROUND-HAS-INTRINSIC-REASON`
(`a7bbd2fd-242c-5097-a9a3-581d25835890`), under #319's
`BR-INTRINSIC-ADEQUACY` and #1595's typed-explanandum question. It is **not**
the Thomistic claim that an underived being's represented essence equals its
esse. It supplies a stepwise positive support option and exact falsification
tests, **not** a verified derivation, a new attribution, or establishment of
any premise. At this baseline the statement has no registered inference that
consumes it on a #143 root route: the connection is planned, not made.

## Primary witnesses actually consulted

- `LEIBNIZ-MONADOLOGY-31-45`, complete pinned Latta translation, re-materialized
  and read. §32 states a sufficient-reason requirement covering real facts and
  truths, without promising that we know the reasons. §§33–35 describe an
  analytic reason for necessary truths. §§36–37 contend that further contingent
  details, even without a finite endpoint, do not provide the final reason for
  their whole sequence. §38 introduces the proposed necessary substance; §44
  moves from the reality of possibilities to an actual necessary being **in
  whom essence involves existence**; §45 proposes both an a priori possibility
  route and an a posteriori route from contingent existents to the necessary
  being **with its existence reason in itself**. These are distinct inferences,
  not a single inference from `NecessaryAt` to an intrinsic reason. Existing
  source claim `LEIBNIZ-45-INTRINSIC-REASON` preserves the narrow retrospective
  §45 reading; no stronger historical claim is made here.
- `SPINOZA-ETHICS-ELWES-FULL`, complete pinned Elwes translation, re-materialized
  and read at Part I definitions 1,3,7–8, axioms 1–7, propositions 6–8 with
  notes and proposition 11 with three proofs. Definition 1 explicates
  *causa sui* as essence involving existence; it does **not** describe a
  temporally prior self-producer. Definition 3 characterizes substance as in
  and conceived through itself; definition 7 distinguishes existing by one's
  nature from external determination. I6 corollary excludes production of
  substance by another; I7 then claims existence belongs to its nature.
  Crucially, I8 note II supplies an independent explanatory demand for each
  *individual* and explicitly divides its existential cause/reason between
  nature/definition and a reason apart from them. I11's second proof also
  requires a reason for **nonexistence**, and argues against internal
  contradiction or external prevention. These further premises are not
  consequences of I6's exclusion of an external *producer*.

The source's `causa sui` and the analyst's `explains` relation cannot be made
definitionally identical. The former is a historical essential-existence
formulation; the latter can register arbitrary token assignments. The
`Reason.intrinsic x` constructor in `Explanation.ExistencePSR` is deliberately
only a candidate. The existing `ExistenceFact.fact x` is the relevant
**world-relative positive existence fact**, not a property fact or a proof
that x exists; mathematical essential truths are another explanandum.

## Positive argument, with each premise defended and limited

Fix one **independently actual** candidate `n` proposed to terminate the
explanation of contingent existents. Read “reason in itself” as a nonproductive
metaphysical answer why this very being exists: its independently characterized
real nature makes its existence intelligible. It is **not** an arrow from the
same existence fact back to itself, not efficient self-causation, not its mere
absence of an incoming causal edge, and not knowledge of a formal definition
alone. The positive case has two complementary routes.

### A posteriori explanatory route

1. **There is a target individual actually existing.** This is an input from a
   separate existential/regress result, not from the proposed nature's
   definition. Starting with an actual bearer to explain its actuality is not
   itself circular: a mathematical demonstration may assume an actual figure
   when explaining its area. But that analogy does **not** explain why a figure
   exists; the two explananda remain typed separately. The #319 same-witness
   obligation ensures the bearer explained here is the independently derived
   final ground rather than a different possible object.
2. **Its positive existence fact remains a legitimate why-question.** §32's
   explanatory scope is not restricted to contingent facts. §§33–35 themselves
   discuss reasons for necessities, so “necessary” cannot alone be a principled
   PSR exemption inside this source's framework. The positive warrant is
   explanatory parity: if ordinary facts require reasons, merely changing a
   fact's modal distribution does not say what accounts for it. This motivates
   existence-PSR for the necessary bearer without *proving* unrestricted PSR.
   A primitive-necessary-fact account can consistently deny the demand.
3. **A final ground cannot have the same sort of unfulfilled external
   existential dependency as its derivative effects.** In §§36–38, adding
   further contingent conditions leaves the *whole contingent sequence's*
   sufficient-reason question unanswered. If the proposed final ground's
   existence required another distinct actualizing source in that very
   respect, the alleged terminal answer would inherit the problem. This gives
   genuine explanatory motivation for actual existential underivedness,
   **conditional** on the whole-series scope and insufficiency of merely
   enumerating local dependencies. It does not exclude a noncausal external
   mathematical law, a global structure, or a necessary reason token merely
   because the ground has no incoming efficient-cause edge.
4. **A genuine existence reason must be independently informative and adequate
   for this individual.** Spinoza I8 note II observes that a generic account
   of human nature does not explain why precisely twenty humans exist and a
   triangle's definition does not specify any number of actual triangles.
   His I11 contrasts the triangle's *figure* with the extended order that
   accounts for its existence. These are positive discrimination tests: a
   reason must reach the actual individual's existence and, where relevant,
   individuation/multiplicity; explaining a property conditional on
   instantiation, repeating “n exists”, or storing a stable intrinsic tag
   fails. This is not a mechanical adequacy predicate supplied by either
   source; independent characterization and explanatory direction remain
   disputed.
5. **An external-or-internal reason exhaustion is proposed, not licensed by
   absence of productive causation.** Spinoza I8 note II and I11 give a
   positive source for classifying an individual's reason as in its nature or
   apart from that nature. If this classification covers *complete adequate*
   explanations in the chosen existential respect, and separately defended
   explanatory ultimacy eliminates all apart-from-nature sufficient bases,
   PSR and actualness yield an own-nature reason. This is a valid elimination
   pattern with **two strong open premises**: exhaustive classification and
   exclusion of every external *reason*, not just external *efficient
   producers*. Otherwise the inference would confuse the scopes. Global
   structural/non-well-founded reasons are the concrete third-candidate test
   of classification, not dismissed by a word definition.
6. **The proposed nature must *supply* existence-content, not just be called
   “underived”.** Spinoza definition 1 and I7 offer essential existence as the
   positive filling of the internal horn. For a bearer already actual,
   nonreceived/subsistent actuality is a candidate reason why no further
   actualizer is needed; this explains a dependence profile. To answer *why it
   exists at all* one still needs the stronger, defended claim that the
   nature's positive content entails (or otherwise adequately accounts for)
   this individual existing. Equating this stronger claim with `essenceOf n =
   esseOf n` would simply rename the very disputed E→C step. The latter
   identity is at most a promising constraint on an intrinsic explanation,
   not its demonstrated explanatory sufficiency.

**Conditional upshot:** given an independently actual, same-witness final
ground, adequate existence-PSR, an exhaustive reason classification,
exclusion of adequate apart-from-nature explanations, and an independently
characterized nature with genuinely sufficient existential content, the ground
has a nonproductive intrinsic reason for its *individual* existence. The last
premise is not assumed away by applying a constructor named `intrinsic`.
This route is philosophically stronger than “necessity implies intrinsic
reason” but presently **not** a closed proof of the historical statement.

### Independent a priori/essence route

1. Leibniz §44 starts from real eternal truths/possibilities and proposes
   that their reality needs an actually existing ultimate basis; this is an
   additional explanatory bridge, not an instance of modal logic alone.
2. He locates the basis in a necessary being whose essence involves existence.
   That proposal is more informative than merely `NecessaryAt n`, but whether
   the independent nature is coherent and existentially sufficient is open.
3. §45 says possibility suffices for the proposed unlimited being's actuality;
   it additionally reasons that unlimited nature is free of contradiction.
   Both **real possibility → actuality** and the claimed consistency of this
   specific nature are substantive. Calling a collection of attributes
   “unlimited” does not by itself settle their joint consistency.
4. If the instance and the nature-content claims are both warranted, the
   reason is internal to the actual being rather than an event that causes
   it to begin existing. This offers a second support path rather than
   reusing underived→essence–esse identity as the sole positive argument.

Spinoza I11 offers variants of the second route. Its first proof uses I7;
the second additionally demands an explanation of nonexistence and excludes
internal contradiction or external prevention; the third argues from degree
of reality to existence-power. These premises must not be attributed to I7
or imported as a general rule from ordinary observation. In particular,
I11's triangle example blocks inferring actual instance from generic nature
unless the stronger nature-to-existence bridge is defended.

## Strongest standing rival and discriminatory burden

**An independent global-structural reason** is a serious positive replacement
for the fifth premise, not merely a denial. It proposes a real, actual whole
structure whose governing relations and instantiated full conditions explain
the existence of its members, including the alleged “necessary ground”, with
no member needing a productive external efficient cause. This may be
non-well-founded at the member-dependence level. Its support is the familiar
explanatory success of relational/structural constraints for properties and
local events and the formal possibility of a joint account rather than a
chain of unrelated conditionals. **But** success for those explananda does
not yet justify a whole-structure reason for *existence*. It owes the same
independent characterization, actual instantiation, completeness, and
directionality standard as the nature-based proposal. The existing
`TargetCopyExpansion` control is a warning against making either rival's
“complete explanation” merely truth-conditional duplication. No certified
global account of actual existential sufficiency or metaphysical exclusion
of it has been provided.

A **brute/ungroundable necessary fact** is distinct: it rejects step 2's
unrestricted reason demand, possibly by proposing that a fundamental
essential fact is not apt for further constitutive grounding (compare the
already studied Dasgupta §§2–5). This rival offers a positive stopping rule
for explanatory questions, not an intrinsic ground of the same fact. It owes
an independently justified criterion for which facts are exempt; merely
labelling a preferred being “necessary” is not such a criterion. Its existence
is not logically inconsistent with the typed PSR controls because their
`explains` assignments need not be metaphysically adequate. Neither rival
has been refuted by the materialized readings or finite assignments.

## Typed conditional and exact omission tests

The module `TruthCore.Explanation.IntrinsicReasonBoundary` reuses
`ExistencePSR.ExistenceFact.fact`, `JointGroups.Groups.members` (contributors
**and** enablers), `NecessarilyUnderived.NoExistingSupportAt` and the Kant
`Ontic`/`NecessaryAt` vocabulary. It is compiled in the library build but
**unregistered**: it has no Challenge, argument, inference, result or
Comparator configuration, so none of its theorems is certified and nothing
binds to the frontier. It implements a candidate-level conditional about
**one witnessed entire record** for `ExistenceFact.fact n`. It does not
introduce an opaque implication to the legacy statement. Its added data is
deliberately limited:

- reason ingredients with constructors `nature b feature`,
  `external b feature`, `global s`. `nature` and `external` carry the same
  data (`Being × Feature`), so for one bearer the nature/external split is a
  constructor **tag**, not a typed difference of content; the nature contents
  are not independently specified. Exhaustiveness of the three-way
  classification is fixed by the closed inductive type (with `global` over an
  arbitrary `Structure` as catch-all), so it is **not** a premise of the
  theorem; whether this classification covers real reasons is the open
  interpretive question of step 5 above;
- a single joint candidate record with effect `fact n`, a nonempty set of members, its
  admitted status, truth of every member at the actual world, and a separately
  checked **worldwise sufficiency** condition over the `ExistsAt` fact.

Not encoded, and open: an **explanatory direction/relevance** requirement
distinguishing full ground from reversed implication or target-copy. Modal
truth-consequence cannot on its own certify it.

**Limited theorem `selectedNatureRecord`:** its four hypotheses are (i) some
`ActualRecord` for `fact n` at `w` (admitted, nonempty, every member true at
`w`, `n` exists at `w`, worldwise sufficient); (ii) `NoExistingSupportAt`:
no existing being stands in `support` to `n` at `w`; (iii)
`ForeignReasonSupports`: in any actual record, every `external b _` member and
every `nature b _` member with `b ≠ n` yields an existing `b` with
`support w b n`; and (iv) `NoFactOrGlobalMember`: no actual record contains a
`fact` or `global` member. Its conclusion `IntrinsicOnlyRecord` is that some
actual record has at least one member and **every** member of that record is
`nature n feature`. External members are excluded only via (iii) with (ii),
and the quantification is over actual records, not all sufficient records.
Classification of every member is unconditional, because `Item` is a closed
type. This is a constructor-tag classification, not yet
`STMT-NECESSARY-GROUND-HAS-INTRINSIC-REASON`: `holds` is arbitrary, so the
record may only entail existence, not explain it. Do not register the legacy
conclusion from this lemma until the relevance bridge is independently
warranted.

**Four necessary omission checks; one populated arena for the first two.**
Each written control proves in one model every other hypothesis of
`selectedNatureRecord`, the failure of the omitted hypothesis, and the failure
of the conclusion.

1. With modal necessity and an empty `support` relation, give a true
   candidate **external** ingredient as the sole member of an actual record.
   `foreignExternalWithoutSupportBridge` uses two beings, with the external
   ingredient borne by a being other than the target;
   `externalWithoutSupportBridge` is the external-tagged, **self-borne**
   variant (its only bearer is the target). Within these stipulated
   candidate semantics, underivedness in the support relation does not
   exclude an external candidate reason; the record is not claimed to be an
   adequate (or abstract) explanation.
2. With the same actual necessary bearer and full sufficiency, give a
   `global` ingredient as the sole member of the complete record.
   `globalWithoutScopeBridge` constructs it; dropping global-exclusion
   allows this model. It tests the global-exclusion premise, not modal
   necessity.
3. Still to certify in this particular typed arena: with a generic essence rule about `square`'s area (or the figure of a
   triangle), let no bearer of that sort exist. The rule determines a
   **property conditional** on an instance but must not satisfy an
   existence-fact explanation or produce an actual witness.
4. Reuse the already certified `TargetCopyExpansion` result: set an alleged own-nature token's truth equal to `ExistsAt n` and give it
   a sufficiency law. It passes local soundness and modal entailment, yet
   fails any independently defended explanatory-direction test. The existing
   `TargetCopyExpansion` theorem already supplies this counterexample
   pattern; reuse its exact result rather than certify a duplicate.

The new `natureInputNonvacuous` checks the **entire** premise package on a
populated candidate record: one actual necessary bearer, no incoming support,
one true own-nature member, and modal sufficiency. Its nature-content is **not
independently specified**. A stronger positive control must instead introduce
independently specified facts and an inference rule generating the existence-
fact conclusion; removing that rule or the actual witness must defeat the
specific claim. Such a control would test syntax and consequence, not certify
the rule as metaphysically explanatory. The existing
`necessaryReasonAndNecessitation` then proves only **modal necessity of the
same target** if its particular complete reason is itself necessary; it does
not certify why the reason is explanatory, its intrinsicness, actual
instantiation, or individuation. In contrast, **necessity of an effect** is
not a premise in the elimination from a sound actual PSR to intrinsicness.

## Exact bridge and reassessment

The named target is #319 `BR-INTRINSIC-ADEQUACY`, not the existing q3a4
`D→E` theorem. To reach #143, an argument must retain **one same actual
being** through the independently established underived/necessary source,
selected fact `fact n`, and valid own-nature reason; then a separate argument
must connect that adequate source to the attributes of God. No such argument
currently consumes legacy `STMT-NECESSARY-GROUND-HAS-INTRINSIC-REASON`.

Current warrant: **qualified, nontrivial support for the possibility and
structure of noncausal intrinsic explanation**, based on mathematical
property explanation and the primary texts' explicit essential-existence
arguments. **No established adequate intrinsic reason for an actual
necessary individual.** Direct PSR universality, external/global exhaustion,
individual real nature-content and the realization/relevance bridge remain
open. Reopen when (a) the grouped typed control and source-indexed direction
test can be certified, (b) independent evidence warrants the proposed real
nature and its existence-entailing content, or (c) a global account meets the
same test and changes the comparative verdict. The module compiles in CI;
Comparator certification and result registration are not claimed in this
research-only step.

There is no new historical claim, registered result (no
Challenge/result/Comparator), source record, root premise or closure
marking. Intended/actual root-route premise change: none.
