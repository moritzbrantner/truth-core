Rejected identifying the accounts by coextension, claiming historical agreement, or inferring compatibility of entire philosophies. The witness establishes consistency of these exact encoded packages in the ambient metatheory; it is not an interpretation theorem for intuitionist object logic. No independent source defense or applicability is established.
