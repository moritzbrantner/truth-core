# Common predication without a common constituent

This bounded comparison supplies a positive nominalist/conceptualist resource
for the Plato–Aristotle investigation. It is not a complete theory of every
nominalism, a refutation of every realism, or an argument that Catholicism is
true or false. The source readings remain open philosophical commitments.

## Witness and reading limits

The entire Latin of Ockham's *Summa logicae* I.14–17 was read after
`source_evidence.py materialize` of `OCKHAM-SL-I-14` through
`OCKHAM-SL-I-17`. The Logic Museum transcription is pinned to MediaWiki
revisions 14239, 15409, 7248 and 7249 respectively. Each immutable `action=raw`
endpoint was fetched twice with identical bytes and text. These are existing
`web_snapshot` witnesses using the existing `raw_text` extractor, not a new
source kind or a claim to have collated a critical Latin edition.

The raw pages include accompanying English, marked unchecked in I.14–16;
claims here are read from the Latin, not based on that translation. This
distinction matters: the I.15 final English explanation drops the Latin
negation concerning a substance's being predicable; I.17 renders *fatuus*
as “unnecessary.” Neither English sentence is used as evidence. The correct
Latin text is not silently emended to match it. The I.17 final discussion of
different kinds of supposition is also retained as context; it is not
generalized into a complete formal semantics here.

The rendered old-revision HTML changed byte hashes while preserving selected
text, so it was rejected as a reproducible byte witness. The immutable raw
revision endpoints solved that problem without tool changes. A bounded
Wayback CDX lookup of the I.14 raw URL returned no capture; SavePageNow returned
HTTP 520. No fallback is invented for any chapter. The witnesses therefore
retain host-availability risk despite immutable revision identity.

Earlier source leads were rejected rather than treated as readings: the
author-hosted Spade PDF timed out, and the UMass PDF mirror returned 404.
The independently obtained Latin witnesses replace that need; no claim is
based on a search excerpt, those failed PDFs, or an unread bibliography.

## Seven bounded readings

Each row has an independent statement and a claim with a paragraph incipit
locator. These are prose readings, not Lean declarations or verified inferences.

| Source and claim | Positive commitment and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| I.14, `OCKHAM-SL-I14-SINGULAR-SIGN` | One mental intention can be numerically singular while apt to signify and be predicated for many. The first sense of singular means one rather than several; it does not mean inability to be a common sign. Aptness does not assert two actual instances. |
| I.14, `OCKHAM-SL-I14-NATURAL-CONVENTIONAL` | A natural mental sign differs from a spoken sign instituted by convention. The smoke/fire analogy is proportional; it is not an independently proved cognitive acquisition or reliability mechanism. |
| I.15, `OCKHAM-SL-I15-MENTAL-NOT-SUBSTANCE` | A naturally universal sign is a mental intention rather than an extramental substance. Its identity with an act of understanding is explicitly one probable analysis, not an unqualified psychological theorem. |
| I.16 conclusion, `OCKHAM-SL-I16-PARTICULAR-FORMS` | Particular matter, particular form and their compounds remain available in the account of individual substance; the concluding division also allows separate immaterial substance. Denying a real common universal is not denying all forms, souls or immaterial reality. |
| I.17 first two replies, `OCKHAM-SL-I17-SPECIFIC-AGREEMENT` | Socrates and Plato really agree specifically and differ numerically. Greater agreement can concern the individuals and their own forms without requiring one further constituent shared by both. Real resemblance is not reduced to an arbitrary name. |
| I.17 common-name reply, `OCKHAM-SL-I17-ONE-CONCEPT-UNIVOCITY` | The example *homo* signifies particular humans through one imposition subordinated to one concept, and so is univocal despite signifying many. The sign is not the things it signifies. This is not a formal semantics for every expression. |
| I.17 fourth reply, `OCKHAM-SL-I17-EXPRESSES-NATURE` | A universal sign expresses or explicates the nature of a substance rather than being a constituent of its essence. Semantic expression is not automatically metaphysical grounding or efficient production. |

I.15's arguments against common substances remain arguments with substantive
premises. The creation/annihilation reasoning uses divine power and a proposed
shared essential constituent; it is not an unconditional Lean refutation of
realism. I.16's objection to a common nature contracted by an individual
difference also relies on its account of real/formal distinction, identity and
opposed predicates. Its final qualification concerning determinations and
syncategorematic expressions is not dropped. A defender of formal distinction
may contest the substitution or opposition analysis. That reply is an explicit
comparison option here, not an attributed Scotist claim from an unread source.

I.17 additionally answers the measure objection: a particular may measure
members of another genus or many members of one species without measuring
every member of its own species. This does not prove or refute the Fourth
Way's causal or maximum premises. Measurement, common predication and
universal causation require separate arguments.

## The same explanatory questions

The table uses the distinctions already present in
[the participation investigation](../participation-grounding.md). None of the
answers becomes sufficient merely because it is sourced.

| Question | Positive nominalist answer in this range | What remains open, including for rivals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How can many particulars receive a common predicate? | One naturally common mental sign, with common vocal expressions subordinated to it; no numerically shared constituent is required by the proposed semantics. | The sign's correct range, its acquisition, and its relation to different speakers' concepts. A common sign is not by itself an explanation of being F. |
| Is genuine similarity admitted? | Yes: specific agreement and numerical difference coexist; individuals and their own particular forms supply the proposed basis. | Why this resemblance rather than another is relevant, and whether those particular resources explain the classification without a further common nature. |
| Why are these bearers F? | Their own natures/forms, rather than the linguistic token or an additional common constituent, are the proposed resource. | A complete independent account of the relevant natures and the bridge from them to the chosen classification. Naming a nature does not establish that bridge. |
| What produces or sustains a bearer? | These chapters retain individual substances, particular forms and theological possibilities; they do not offer a complete production mechanism. | Actual causal dependence and the scope of any uniting-cause principle. No inference from “no real common universal” to “no form,” “uncaused,” or “materialism.” |
| How does a knower acquire warranted general knowledge? | Natural mental signification and univocal common predication offer semantic resources. | These chapters do not supply a full account of truth-tracking, induction, scientific justification or error correction. Separate Forms and immanent forms owe their own access/adequacy accounts too. |
| Must a general term denote a further universal substance? | The common-name reply says no: the term signifies the particular humans under one concept. | A realist can argue that semantic co-signification leaves metaphysical commonality unexplained. The nominalist owes an adequate positive account, not merely denial of the realist's premise. |
| What follows for the Catholic case? | Nothing about the root follows from these readings alone. The source itself discusses God and immaterial substance, so this nominalist resource is not a synonym for atheism or naturalism. | Further metaphysical, theological and historical premises remain independent. No root edge or closure is added. |

An immanent-form account and this nominalist resource can overlap in retaining
particular forms. A mental sign and a substantial form are different roles;
one need not replace the other. A separate-exemplar defender can likewise
accept that the mental predicate is not a literal part of its objects while
claiming an additional explanatory role for the exemplar. The remaining
disagreement must concern those positive ontological/explanatory commitments,
not the word “universal” alone. These are not three mutually exclusive or
jointly exhaustive theories by definition.

## Premise comparison and next decisive question

The nominalist resource retains real particulars, real specific agreement,
particular forms and meaningful common predication. It replaces an appeal to
a shared substantial constituent with natural mental signification and an
account in terms of the individuals' own forms. Those replacement commitments
remain open in adequacy. The record does not prove the nominalist account or
the realist inference it contests.

The next decisive question is whether an independently specified individual
nature and defensible acquisition/selection law can explain stable, warranted
classification without a further common explanans, or whether a realist
explanation adds indispensable content. Static co-signification alone decides
neither side. An inference needs one explicit semantic profile before any of
the existing Forms certificates can be applied.

Intended and actual root-route open-premise change: none; all 63 reaching-root
leaves and the eight route costs remain unchanged. The successful reading
attempt records evidence, rejected readings and this gap; it establishes none
of its philosophical target statements.
