# One standard for every explanatory stopping point (#746)

The theorems are `stoppingMap` and `foundationNormTest` in
`TruthCore.Results.Explanation.StoppingPoints.Solution`, over
`TruthCore.Explanation.StoppingPoints`, which extends the #755 arena and the #756 stance
vocabulary. Both are zero-axiom. They establish conditional consequence and the
satisfiability of encoded bundles. They establish no norm, exclude no foundation
unconditionally, and discharge no premise on a root route. No source was read for this
work, so nothing below is attributed to anyone.

## The same tests at each stop

Every test takes an arena, a world and a fact. The arena has no predicate for personal
agency, so a necessary personal ground and a necessary impersonal one, and a free choice
and an indeterministic output, are the same `ground` structure: nothing here separates
them. "Passes" is relative to the test; no test is asserted as the right demand.

| Stop (model, fact) | Has a reason | Noncontingent | Work necessitates | Work independent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brute terminus (`bruteTerminus`, `terminus`) | no | no | yes | no |
| Necessary ground with self-reason (`ground true`, `ground`) | yes | yes | no | no |
| Necessary ground, no self-reason (`ground false`, `ground`) | no | yes | no | yes |
| Contingent law (`history true`, `law`) | no | no | yes | yes |
| Unexplained infinite whole (`history false`, `hist`) | no | no | no work | no work |

| Self-reasoned, no contingency (`allNecessary`, `false`) | yes | yes | yes | no |
| Domain with no stop (`cofinite`) | no terminal fact, and no aggregate of its contingent truths | | | |

The necessary ground fails tests in each row it occupies. It gets no exemption: the
second core has no clause for necessary facts, and `ground false` fails its coverage
member. The stop-free `cofinite` domain has factive, necessitating reasons for every true
fact and no aggregate of its contingent truths. An aggregate is a member of the #755
collapse core.

## The fork and the cores

A terminal fact that is a reason for a contingent truth, under factivity and given two
decisions, is one of three things, each witnessed: brute (`bruteTerminus`), contingent
with a self-reason (`selfWork`), or noncontingent with work that does not necessitate
(`ground true`). So a noncontingent stop cannot have necessitating work on a contingent
target. Each core only forces rejecting at least one member. As #553 found, rejecting a
horn does not derive past it.

## The candidate norm

`NoBruteFoundation` is open. It is not contradictory by definition: it holds in the named
survivors. Its implication is nontrivial: with a single stop for an explained contingent
truth, it cannot stand with factive, necessitating, well-founded reasons, although it
excludes only brute foundations and not every brute truth. Its limits:

- `twinRoots` and `isolated` satisfy it with brute truths, so "indispensable" is what it
  excludes, and a second brute root of the same effect removes the status.
- Dropping the contingency restriction gives `NoUnreasonedFoundation`. That convicts
  `ground false`, passes `ground true`, and passes every target copy. So it discriminates
  only with a further condition on self-reasons. Uniform modal independence is such a
  condition and it excludes a necessary fact being a reason for any necessary fact.
  `ReasonIndependent` binds only contingent targets. Whether there is an independent line
  is open. It may have to be hyperintensional, since `ground true` and
  `copy (ground false)` agree on this self-reason and on the ground's truth at each world.

not modelled.

## Interpretation of the new notions

- **Ordinary language.** A terminal fact is where "why?" gets no further answer. Work is
  what it answers for. Necessitating work: given the stop, the target could not have
  failed.
- **Examples and counterexamples.** A decay event is a contingent truth often said to have
  no sufficient reason. A deductive premise necessitates; a free choice as an answer to
  "why this?" is claimed not to.
- **Not the same as.** Terminal is not uncaused, not unknowable and not necessary.
  Necessitating is not productive or causal. Indispensable here is not modal.
- **Structural properties.** Well-foundedness is a named member of the cores, deleted by a
  circle and an infinite domain, not assumed. No transitivity or asymmetry is assumed.
  Supplied chains reasons, but `Supplied` is reachability, not a claim that reasons
  transmit.
- **Generalization.** No move from ordinary explanation to a metaphysical law is made.
- **Competing accounts.** Probabilistic and hyperintensional accounts reject
  necessitation as a test, and they are the exit `ground true` takes. Joint rather than
  alternative reasons are not represented, so a joint-sufficiency reading of `twinRoots`
  is outside this model; #752 owns sufficiency across cycles.
