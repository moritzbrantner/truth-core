suffices if the rows coincide. The registered symmetric statement retains the

## Reuse and descent into causal-essential knowing

The seed's exact `ImmaterialIsIntellectualReading` concludes a primitive
intellectual power from structural immateriality. Its capacity-calibration
context (argument 1ae8b1c3-2835-560f-b054-36c2e83b527d) still needs scope,
positive aptitude and intellectual calibration jointly. It supplies neither
selected-content completeness nor an epistemic extension. Fitch's `Context`
separates modal and epistemic operators, and `CommonKnowerScopes` blocks
exchanging per-content knowers for one common knower. Neither can supply this
missing bridge. `CorrectionCriterion.CriterionCharacterization` is the existing
factorization analogue: classification requires constancy on observation
fibers. It does not establish cognitive adequacy, so is not republished here.

A concrete candidate causal bridge is: for one rigid token a and a fixed effect
x, (i) the necessary bearer's being carries a, (ii) a's performance is sufficient
for x at each accessible world, and (iii) a is the very token alleged to know x
through causing x. In #555's exact vocabulary, (i) is `BeingCarriesAct p a`
and (ii) is `StrictEfficacy p w a x`. The first and seventh components of
`Results.Modal.SimplicityCollapse.core` compose: necessary bearer plus (i)
gives `SameActEverywhere`, which with (ii) excludes `ContingentAt`. The token
identity cannot be exchanged for a world-relative description or another
intentional act. This is the already certified modal argument, not a novel
foreknowledge theorem and not a claim that all causation necessitates.

Serious objection: causal production does not alone give the sufficiency in
(ii), and intrinsic identity does not alone make the relation to this particular
x intrinsic. The exact `inefficaciousAct` omission model keeps a necessary
bearer and necessary token while x varies. `optionalAct` instead keeps efficacy
and varies token occurrence. Neither is adequate cognition merely by its name.
A timeless/extrinsic account can retain one intrinsic act and vary its relation
to x, denying epistemic-row supervenience without temporal acquisition. Its
positive burden is a representational/intentional account that distinguishes
true apprehension from an accidentally matching relation. The row model
provides the extension constraint, not that account.

Descend one step further: introduce a contingent selector S and keep
necessitating efficacy of A-and-S. This explains variation only conditionally;
S requires explanation if complete explanation was promised. #555
`rootExposure`'s final component and `branchCosts` analyze this: a reason-closed,
founded account of a contingent explanandum cannot have every reason
necessitating. The positive rival options include a nonnecessitating reason,
brute contingency, an unfounded chain, or a contingent act. `branchCosts`
contains the relocated-act model: moving contingency into an act does not make
its explanation wholly necessary, and copying the effect as S explains nothing.
These are precise costs relative to those explanatory standards; none is
unreasonable merely because unusual. The `rootExposure` immutable-respect
argument also requires `ModalCapacity`, exclusivity, decree availability and
respect sufficiency. Calling the act immutable does not supply them.

The open-future rival rejects settled earlier bivalent outcomes and instead
knows the branching possibilities and whatever is settled. It must specify
whether determinate future truths exist and are unknown, or do not yet exist;
only the first competes directly with this complete-Boolean-content claim.
No non-bivalent semantics is certified here. A necessitarian rival keeps strict
efficacy and necessary act, rejecting effect contingency. Neither this theorem
nor #555 decides which explanatory standard or account of agency is warranted.

## Provisional overall judgment and reassessment

The relational incompatibility is well supported mathematically within its
selected semantics. It discriminates extension rigidity from a fixed rule and
exposes two independent application burdens: adequate cognition-to-row modeling,
and intrinsic/hard-past supervenience. The causal-essential reading is not
refuted: the strongest surviving alternative denies that an invariant intrinsic
act fixes its contingent intentional/effect relations, while accepting a
nonnecessitating explanatory relation or declining complete necessitating
explanation. Conversely, if both being-carries-act and strict efficacy of this
same effect are independently justified, #555 already rules out its contingency.
No new owner approval or metaphysical winner is required.

The next discriminating test needs a warranted intentional relation for causal
knowing that is neither truth by stipulation nor a covert strict-efficacy axiom.
Unavailable in the inspected seed and supplied summaries is an independently
supported criterion selecting such a relation. More row lemmas cannot supply
it. Coordinator reassessment should choose a concrete causal-intentional
account and its strongest objection, or the settled-future/historical-ownership
question in #945. Independent semantic challenge must assess this complete
proposed judgment before integration as substantive conclusions.
