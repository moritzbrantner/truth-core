## Population aggregation: a proved common calibration burden

Reuse: `FINITE-POPULATION-SCORING-TRADEOFFS`,
`FINITE-POPULATION-AGGREGATION-CONTROLS` and
[the population note](carneades-population-aggregation.md). The new result is
`POPULATION-COMPARABILITY-COMMON-BURDEN`; its Lean boundary is
`TruthCore.Ethics.PopulationComparability.Controls`.

**Objection and common standard.** Suppose welfare measurements supply each
individual's own ordering and cardinal structure, but supply no cross-person
relative unit. Treat independent positive affine changes of each individual's
scale as equally admissible representations. Then require a collective strict
comparison to survive every such representation. This is an explicit proposed
adequacy standard, not an axiom established by the theorem. It is stronger than
mere preservation of a common already calibrated interpersonal scale.

The two coordinates track the **same two people** across two alternatives; list
permutation or changes of population identity are not doing the work. Let the
first person gain d>0 and the second lose e>0. The collective difference after
positive scale weights a,b is a*d-b*e. Choose (a,b)=(2e,d): the difference is
+d*e. Choose (a,b)=(e,2d): it is -d*e. Arbitrary individual origin shifts u,v
cancel because each person's transformation is identical across alternatives.
Every individual strict ordering remains unchanged under both transformations.
This is generic for all opposing strictly nonzero differences, not just a toy
counterexample or an assumed reversal bridge.

For equal count two, average is total divided by two and critical-level score
is total minus 2c. Consequently the **same two transformations** reverse all
three policies simultaneously, for every real c, including every positive
critical level. The proof imports the existing checked scoring Solution and
consumes its list-summary theorem and the earlier equal-count comparison;
it does not introduce the old conclusions as premises.

**Transferred burden.** If the criticism of totalism is solely that these
individual measurement freedoms leave collective tradeoff rankings unstable,
ordinary arithmetic average and critical-level rivals have exactly that burden
on these same alternatives. Their existing ability to avoid the low-replication
conclusion does not supply interpersonal calibration. Conversely, this theorem
does not transfer totalism's variable-count replication consequence to average
or critical-level scoring: their different responses there remain genuine.
Representation sensitivity is not a logical contradiction or proof that welfare
cannot actually be compared. Interpersonal cardinal comparability is a framework
input common to these arithmetic implementations, not a universal requirement
of every theory bearing their informal labels.

**Principled symmetry breakers, also checked.** A justified **common** positive
unit scale a=b preserves all three equal-count comparisons, even with different
fixed person-specific origin shifts. A calibrated interpersonal unit therefore
blocks the representation objection by restricting what counts as the same
measurement. It must be independently defended; naming a numerical value is not
calibration. Also, if both people weakly gain and at least one strictly gains,
every independent positive scaling gives the same strict aggregate direction.
The failure is specifically about interpersonal tradeoffs, not all social
comparisons. A Pareto-only partial ordering can decline to order the tradeoff
while retaining these unanimous comparisons. Lexical priority or defended
person-specific weights can instead select a tradeoff direction, but then add
normative structure beyond the permitted measurement information.

**Guards and open burdens.** Fixed individuals, fixed count, positive scale
weights and the same transformations across alternatives are explicit. The
critical level is held fixed across each comparison. This does not license
arbitrary origin shifts for variable-population comparisons, or changes of
critical level without unit conversion. The theorem makes no assumption that
all welfare numbers are positive; positivity of life relative to a privileged
zero would require a restricted transformation class. No claim that arbitrary
interpersonal rescaling preserves an already meaningful common scale is made.
Actual profile feasibility, welfare interpretation, the correct admissible
transformation class and moral authority remain OPEN. No root premise changes.

## Induction and worldview selection: conditional common demand

Reuse `SCIENCE-INDUCTION-SELECTION`, especially
`TruthCore.Results.Science.InductionSelection.symmetry` and `noUnique`, and
[the induction note](carneades-induction.md). The exact theorem assumes a
supplied invertible evidence/hypothesis recoding, fixed evidence, and an
equivariant selector; a moved selected hypothesis makes unique selection
impossible. Nonempty observations do not turn the coordinate-specific
uniformity test into a basis-neutral test.

A criticism demanding entirely basis-neutral unique worldview selection would
also burden inductive projection **if** the very same fixed-evidence,
hypothesis-moving recoding and neutrality requirements apply. The common cost
is accepting an additional privileged basis/selection norm, admitting multiple
answers, or suspending selection. It does not follow that either actual science
or an actual worldview satisfies this symmetry profile. No religion/science
pair with equally informative evidence is supplied here, so this remains a
conditional comparison rather than a newly certified worldview equivalence.

A serious discriminator is independently supported physical measurement,
projectibility, causal structure or reliable predictive calibration that either
breaks evidence symmetry or justifies restricting the recoding class. The
existing asymmetric-evidence control explicitly shows that equivariant unique
selection is possible then. Simply using the word “natural” or “specific” does
not supply that discriminator. Scientific success also needs its evidential
bridge articulated rather than being equated to a bare pragmatic benefit.

## Wagers and secular pragmatic defense: same invalid truth inference

Reuse `SIGNED-WAGER-PRACTICAL-NONTRUTH`, exact theorem
`TruthCore.Results.Decision.SignedWager.practicalNontruth`, and
[the wager note](carneades-decision-wager.md). It witnesses a practical maximizer
with a false actual religious proposition under non-dogmatic prior and genuinely
negative finite cost. The complementary-policy profile even lets every act
mixture maximize, so maximization alone does not discriminate the proposition.

Under the common standard “a practical payoff justification alone establishes
the proposition's truth”, a religious wager and a secular defense of a useful
belief incur the same missing bridge. Relabeling the proposition's subject
cannot make that inference truth-preserving: practical utility and actual truth
are different fields of the model. This is an inference-level common burden,
not a claim that every secular argument has infinite utilities or is a wager.
A finite-utility secular instance would need its own exact instantiation; none
is counted as a new checked result here.

A legitimate breaker is an empirical reliability or evidential argument linking
the method to truth; instrumental benefit by itself is not such a link. A policy
that claims only practical permission incurs no unfulfilled truth conclusion.
The religious defender may likewise make that narrower claim. Neither the
formal separation nor its shared application refutes the underlying religion,
secular belief or policy, or establishes voluntary control over belief.
