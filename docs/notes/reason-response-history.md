## Two acquisition routes with the same complete present response

An acquisition function takes two earlier Boolean inputs: review u and external
setting m. The declared toy interpretations are:

| Route | Acquired selector |
| --- | --- |
| `selfReview` | u |
| `externalBypass` | m |

The input names do not prove that u is autonomous or m manipulative in reality.
Both acquisition functions are deterministic. The acquired selector populates
the existing `ChoiceContext.History.law` field; reason signal r populates its
preference field. The exact old `action` function supplies the output. Its
`imposed` input remains separately represented. An acquired policy selector is
not asserted to be a complete physical law; the acquisition and response
equations remain fixed throughout each route's interventions.

Actual values are u=m=false, r=true, imposed=false. Both routes then have the
same **entire current History**, the same actual action true and the same entire
Bool-to-Bool response function `r ↦ r`. `observe` includes that History plus the
whole represented reason-response function, rather than only the actual output.
The comparison does not claim to include every real reason or disposition
outside this deliberately one-bit signature.

`SignalSensitive` is computed, not supplied as an opaque agency flag. For every
acquisition function and fixed earlier inputs, the two reason-signal values give
different actions when imposed=false: an acquired selector either follows or
reverses the signal. This property says nothing about rational recognition,
correctly appreciating reasons or freedom/desert.

## What differs, and what information cannot recover it

`ReviewInfluence` asks whether changing only u changes the actual action while
m=false, r=true and imposed=false stay fixed. `ExternalInfluence` changes only m
while the remaining actual values stay fixed.

| Route | Review intervention changes action | External intervention changes action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| self-review copy | yes (u=true) | no |
| external-setting copy | no | yes (m=true) |

These predicates are derived from the displayed two-stage functions and the old
action table. They are neither historical-ownership flags nor declarations of
real causal or normative control. Changing an earlier input does not retain
exactly the same past. The earlier review input may itself be externally
determined: no ultimate sourcehood conclusion is hidden in its name.

The two routes therefore lie in the same `observe` fiber with opposite values
of each influence predicate. The **imported** factorization theorem excludes
any exact classifier of either predicate from these present data alone. Even
an arbitrary infinitary/Prop-valued criterion of this complete small response
map cannot distinguish them; the proof does not invent a new generic
same-input impossibility lemma.

Two bounds keep the consequence precise. First, when earlier interventions
leave the acquired selector equal and the remaining current inputs fixed,
`sameContextSameAction` closes the output under those interventions. If reason
or imposed status also changes, that closure theorem's premises are absent.
Second, imposed=true forces output=true regardless of acquisition: acquisition
sensitivity alone need not produce action sensitivity downstream. Adding the
entire acquisition function to the observed data admits exact review-influence
classification. That repair is information about the stipulated mechanism, not
a justification of ownership or knowledge that a real mechanism has that form.

### Candidate criterion and its rationale

Consider **informed, effective policy revision without bypass**: before the
relevant action, the person has an opportunity to understand a policy's practical
basis, assess objections to it, and retain or revise it through that assessment;
a separate process does not overwrite the resulting policy. This is a proposed
defeasible condition for attributing the policy to the person for guidance, not
a definition of freedom or a sufficient condition for basic desert. It can fail
through blocked access, inability to understand, ineffective assessment, deception
about the policy, or overriding replacement. Retention can qualify: revision need
not occur whenever review occurs. The normative rationale is answerability: asking
someone to answer for a policy is better supported when objections could inform
their effective assessment than when a setter circumvents that process. This
rationale presupposes a practice of answerability; it does not yet justify that
practice or establish blameworthiness.

Unlike a pure origin prohibition, this criterion permits repair after manipulation.
Unlike an ultimate-source requirement, it does not require making the capacities
with which one first learns. These differences are substantive costs and benefits,
not a stipulation that the competitor is unreasonable. A strong objection is that
a manipulator may install both the policy and a disposition to approve it; even
competent review could then express the manipulator's design. Merely reporting
approval therefore cannot meet the criterion. Effective access to objections,
understanding and the ability to revise must be independently assessed. Even
those conditions may leave a source incompatibilist unsatisfied.

### Interface and positive structural bridge

Keep the original acquisition function `a(u,m)` without calling either port an
autonomous person. Add a later revision stage with independently separated ports:
old selector `s`, recommendation `j`, access gate `g`, revision-suppression flag `b`.
Set `R(s,j,g,b) = s` when b is true; otherwise j when g is true; otherwise s.
These equations specify a **structural surrogate**. They do not encode knowledge,
understanding, genuine reasons or permission to intervene in a real person.

Admissible *model* interventions change one supplied port while holding the
stage equation and other ports fixed. Interventions on j test uptake, not an
ability with the same complete past. g represents availability of the modeled
revision channel; b suppresses revision and retains the acquired selector. It
does not provide a replacement value or represent every external influence.
Ordinary teaching can supply content through j; an override suppresses j's
influence. Locating these interfaces in an actual agent remains OPEN: choosing
a neural variable and calling it assessment would not establish the mapping.

There is a positive bridge with content beyond #1083: for **any** original
acquisition function, g=true and b=false make the later selector equal j,
independently of u and m. Also, variation of j changes the later selector at
every old selector exactly when g=true and b=false. This screens the direct old-selector input at fixed recommendation; it does not
establish acquisition-independence of the complete assessment process. It differs
from the old same-present/different-history
fixture. Both conditions have concrete witnesses. Removing g or allowing b
restores dependence on the old selector even with j opposed to it. Thus an earlier
external-copy acquisition does not itself establish lasting bypass; conversely,
an endogenous acquisition supplies no guarantee of later revision access.

The bridge stops at structural uptake. Calling it normative ownership would
silently add understanding, nondeceptive access, practical competence and a
normative justification of attribution. No predicate named Ownership is supplied
to make the result trivial, and no frozen Challenge is weakened.

### Restoring omitted commitments and testing the rivals

1. **Echoed endorsement.** Let j(s,x)=s for either assessment x. An open gate and
   absent override yield unchanged policy under every x. This defeats the
   inference from assent or absence of external override to effective assessment.
   The additional assessment-to-recommendation channel must be tested separately.
2. **Understanding versus sensitivity.** Let j=x or j=not x. Both channels change
   output when x changes. Sensitivity cannot distinguish accurate uptake from
   systematically inverted uptake. Neither x nor its inversion is here a
   truth-bearing practical judgment: assigning a correctness bit would add an
   interpretation, not establish understanding. This is a concrete obstruction
   to deriving rational adequacy from the proposed intervention tests alone.
3. **Ordinary upbringing and rehabilitation.** Earlier external influence is
   compatible with the positive screening equation. The criterion can therefore
   allow learning and rehabilitation without treating all social causation as
   manipulation. Whether access and understanding actually occur is not encoded.
   Endogenous rote learning can fail the echo test just as installed assent can.
4. **Later reflective repair.** A compatibilist can accept initially bypassed
   acquisition and subsequent ownership after effective informed review. A
   nonrepair historical rival can deny that later review cures intentional design.
   Its strongest rationale is continuity of installed evaluative standards, not
   simply dislike of an external cause. Requiring all standards to be independently
   authored threatens regress: evaluating a standard uses further capacities.
   That observation does not refute a rival that requires only nonmanipulated
   acquisition, or one that rejects basic desert universally. A next discriminator
   is whether competent review with access to the manipulation's facts can repair
   attribution while its evaluative standards remain causally continuous.
5. **Same-hard-past incompatibilism.** All these revision functions are
   deterministic. A requirement of different action with exactly the same full
   past and laws can reject them even when the candidate guidance condition holds.
   Its positive account locates desert in a stronger source or alternative
   capacity, or suspends desert if that capacity is unavailable. Its conflict is
   with sufficiency of guidance for desert, not with the screening equation.
   ChoiceContext's complete input is only the table input; it is not a proof
   that nature supplies a complete deterministic past.
6. **Chance.** Adding an independent noise selector after a closed review gate
   can vary output without creating assessment uptake. With noise fixed, assessment
   still has no effect. Opening the review gate can coexist with stochastic
   disturbance, but reliable control then requires specifying its frequency,
   magnitude and intervention background. No stochastic alternative, probability
   distribution, or agent causation is established by a Boolean noise port.

These are selected rivals, not exhaustive or exclusive philosophical senses.
No new corpus sense or vocabulary is being introduced for downstream use; the
candidate's semantic obligations remain in this research draft. If promoted,
structural uptake, informed guidance, nonrepair history and same-past alternatives
need separate typed definitions and explicit bridges, rather than a shared
uninterpreted freedom flag.

### Disclosed manipulation: separate questions rather than another approval flag

Consider two people, one deliberately conditioned and one ordinarily brought up,
who now learn a truthful account of their acquisition histories. Give each the
same practical proposal, relevant factual information, time to inspect it, access
to an advocate and a critic, an opportunity to choose another review procedure,
and an effective means to implement the outcome. This describes a comparison to
investigate, not an established human possibility witnessed by our Boolean table.
It does not hold the whole past fixed across people. Distinguish five burdens:

* Factual understanding includes grasping **that** the standard was installed,
  how the intervention worked, what evidence supports the account, and relevant
  consequences of alternatives. Repeating a disclosure sentence is insufficient.
* Evaluative standards rank considerations: e.g. loyalty, suffering, autonomy or
  consistency. Knowing their origin does not entail rejecting them. Disclosure
  supplies a potential reason for scrutiny, not a deductive instruction to invert
  every acquired judgment.
* Practical competence includes retaining relevant facts, drawing applicable
  implications, recognizing a conflict, and revising means when they fail a
  selected end. These are assessable without agreement with a desired moral
  conclusion. Whether responsiveness to substantive good reasons is additionally
  necessary remains a different normative premise.
* Alternative procedures may include adversarial argument, independent advice,
  delay, comparison with other commitments, and reconsideration of the ranking
  rule itself. A manipulator can install preferences over procedures too. Merely
  increasing their number does not prove their independence or adequacy.
* Effective revision means that a changed assessment can alter policy and action
  under appropriate conditions. It does not mean that someone must change their
  mind, that their practical judgment is true, or that the same complete past
  admits multiple actions. The screening certificate isolates only a surrogate
  for this final condition.

This decomposition repairs the rough draft's temptation to regard factual
transparency plus sensitive uptake as informed ownership. An agent can understand
her origin and consequences while continuing to rank the manipulator's end
highest. Echo is one mechanism for that outcome, but the installed-standards
objection is stronger: active comparative reasoning can itself favor retention.
Inversion is a control on sensitivity, not evidence that every retained installed
standard is mistaken. Neither control decides which practical reasons count.

### Matched outcomes and the positive historical rivals

At the structural level, the universal Screening theorem already entails that
both original acquisitions yield recommendation under open gate/no override,
for the same recommendation; it does not need a duplicate fixture. The entire
later response to both recommendations matches despite different acquisition
functions. Thus differing origins can coexist with identical modeled revision
outcomes. Adding factual and competence descriptions in prose does not extend
that theorem to actual informed agency. Their joint realization is OPEN.

Conditional on such a genuinely matched human case, three accounts diverge:

1. **Repairable guidance attribution.** If the capacities are adequate, access
   nondeceptive, alternatives seriously assessable and assessment effective, the
   person may now answer for retaining or revising a policy despite its origin.
   Endogenous upbringing can qualify or fail by those same conditions. Repaired
   manipulation can qualify; installed standards are not automatically disqualified.
   This account must accept that an intentionally caused, predictably retained
   policy can become attributable. Its positive reason is present answerability
   through the person's capacities, not that causal history vanished.
2. **Nonrepair historical attribution.** Deliberate installation leaves a defect
   even after matched competent review because the authority of the evaluating
   standards remains borrowed from the designer. This account must distinguish
   intentional bypass from ordinary teaching and explain what transfers or
   invalidates authority. Intention, concealment, suppression of criticism and
   control of deliberation are separable candidates. Disclosure removes concealment;
   effective alternative procedures may remove suppression. Intentional design
   and causal continuity remain. If those alone suffice for permanent exclusion,
   the account denies repaired attribution even when no present deficit is found.
   That is its commitment, not a demonstrated contradiction or unreasonable cost.
3. **Source/alternative requirement for desert.** Both cases can support useful
   guidance practices while failing basic desert if neither agent authored the
   relevant capacities or could act otherwise with the same complete past/laws.
   It can therefore accept repair for guidance without accepting desert. The
   determinism of the router supplies neither an argument that all human capacities
   are determined nor an argument that stronger sourcehood exists.

A proposed regress objection needs qualification. Every assessment employs some
capacities, so requiring a prior independent endorsement of **every** capacity
cannot be met by a finite sequence without an unendorsed starting point. But
nonrepair need not demand that: it can require absence of particular intentional
bypass, or deny ultimate desert universally. Consequently regress pressures the
universal self-authorship condition, not all historical restrictions. No theorem
of regress or exhaustive taxonomy is claimed here.

The new independent normative bridge is: adequate, nondeceptive and effective
review is a defeasible ground of **guidance attribution**, including retention.
It is not a routing definition. Its present support is the contrast between
asking someone to respond to reasons through exercisable capacities and demanding
an answer while bypassing them; ordinary learning and rehabilitation make repair
an intelligible aim. This is a conceptual and explanatory rationale, dependent
on the legitimacy of answerability practices. It is not independent empirical
confirmation or an established sufficient condition. The historical rival's
strongest objection is that those very capacities implement the designer's
standards; the criterion cannot simply redescribe their operation as ownership.
A substantive counterexample must preserve adequate understanding and genuinely
available effective reconsideration, rather than merely stipulate reported assent.
