# Teleonomy's exact director-principle rival

The existing director principle says that every noncognitive member tending to
an end has some knowing intelligent director of it to that end. The predicates
must retain the same meanings throughout the comparison. In particular,
broad immanent tending is not conscious intention, historical reproductive
function, local coupling, chance or random mutation.

The old alternative edge used the full, untyped global naturalistic summary.
Its philosophical profile remains unchanged and open. It is not identified
with a finite toy, nor reinterpreted as a proof that mechanisms explain every
natural process. Refuting that broad package would not automatically establish
the director universal.

`UndirectedTendingWitness` explicitly supplies a noncognitive member and end,
broad tending, and absence of a knowing intelligent director to that same end.
The checked specialization derives `NotDirectorPrinciple`, exactly the
negation of the original universal under the same predicates. This exact
negative is now the alternative endpoint. The raw global summary remains a
separate statement. Mapping it to the structural witness requires an **OPEN
interpretation bridge**: vocabulary interpreting actual processes as members
and ends, a populated noncognitive instance, and the same broad tending and
directing senses. No source witness, attribution or historical equivalence is
invented for that bridge. Conditional work with the explicit witness need not
wait for its independent warrant.

The exported controls retain every advertised property. An empty-tending,
no-cognition structure satisfies the director universal vacuously. The existing
finite unguided profile has a concrete undirected noncognitive tending witness
and fails the principle. The old unguided-finality and governed-mechanism
fragments are imported whole as separate facts; no new joint global worldview
model is claimed. Selection and coupling alone imply neither the structural
witness nor the negation of broad tending or intelligence.

## Science and spacetime audit scope

The extension inspected all six vocabulary modules, twenty definition records,
thirty-two linked statement records and thirteen exact namespace-matched bound
result/argument packs. Existing science controls already separate exact
constant-bias identification from true-anchor warrant, variable-bias profiles,
zero-offset accuracy from fallible justification, coordinate transport from
physical naturalness, and neutral set-valued response from unique selection.
No theorem adopts a comprehensive epistemic meaning by fiat.

The boost control already distinguishes frame-time equality, preserved
nonspacelike geometric order, and an additional privileged-present relation.
It supplies a preferred-one-frame survivor, so frame disagreement is not
silently treated as refuting every privileged ontology or as defining actual
causation. Source synchronization descriptions remain separate from the
mathematical model's applicability.

Teleonomy controls already distinguish biological/nonliving scope,
individual/universal director scope, broad tending/cognition, and the
chance-or-intention coverage premise. `FragmentConsistent` is explicitly a
conjunction of separate models, not a joint worldview consistency theorem.
The one consequential latent endpoint gap is repaired here; no extra
competing-definition fork is manufactured for fixed mathematical vocabulary.
Historical source statements without Lean declarations are not silently used
as typed inference premises. Their future formal use needs the corresponding
interpretation/history-constraint/coverage vocabulary, not merely a source
label. Reauthentication of historical claims was not performed by this audit.
