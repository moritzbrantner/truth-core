# Goodness definitions: due actuality and the explicit remainder

The owner’s current direction in [#1116](https://github.com/moritzbrantner/truth-core/issues/1116)
is to formulate “goodness is the actuality due to a thing’s form and end” as a
proposition and investigate rival definitions. It supersedes the earlier idea
of adopting the definition. No floor item or definition is adopted here.

The analyst reconstruction is `STMT-GOODNESS-DUE-TO-FORM-AND-END`:

```
∀ x p, GoodIn x p ↔ (DueFor (form x) (endOf (form x)) p ∧ Has x p)
```

`GoodIn` remains independent, so this can fail. The form/end standard is an
explicit supplied interpretation, not an inference from whatever is actual or
approved. `DueFor` expresses appropriateness of a perfection, not an efficient
causal relation. Whether natural kinds have these forms/ends and why their
standards govern real evaluations remain open applicability questions.

This is a per-perfection, extensional reconstruction. It does not identify
property intensions, define aggregate degrees, determine good simpliciter,
or derive categorical practical authority.

## Two different coverage questions

`GOODNESS-FORM-END-DEFINITION-FORK` proves, under explicit excluded middle:

1. Goodness coextends with due actuality; or
2. some goodness exceeds due actuality (`SurplusGood`); or
3. some due actuality is omitted from goodness (`OmittedDueGood`).

The last two cells can overlap. Excluding both entails the first, but their
exclusion is equivalent to the target in this profile, not independent
philosophical support. This structural partition is not a list of named
worldviews.

The separate named investigation uses three explicit proposals: due actuality,
a feature-based criterion, and a fixed-assessor approval criterion. These are
analyst definition propositions, not full accounts attributed to historical
schools. `DefinitionSpace` records their extensions and the chosen admissible
class. The weakest sufficient coverage premise says every admissible proposal
is **pointwise coextensive** with one of them. A renamed or intensionally
different but coextensive account is already covered. Strong candidate-identity
coverage is preserved separately with a checked `strengthens` registration;
the core elimination does not need it.

`STMT-GOODNESS-ADDITIONAL-SEMANTIC-DEFINITION` explicitly says that an admissible
proposal has an extension different from every named account.
`GOODNESS-SEMANTIC-NAMED-COVERAGE-FORK` proves semantic coverage or that explicit
remainder under excluded middle. It establishes neither philosophical branch.

`GOODNESS-FROM-NAMED-DEFINITION-ELIMINATION` then derives the owner’s proposition
from exactly four open inputs:

- semantic named coverage;
- at least one admissible account correctly describing the profile’s goodness;
- actual correctness failure of the feature proposal;
- actual correctness failure of the approval proposal.

The correct-account input prevents a vacuous “only due actuality remains” when
nothing admitted is correct. Mere disfavor, unexplained authority or an
unanswered objection is not the correctness negation used by this theorem.

Both scope branches have fixed models. In the covered model the due account is
correct. In the remainder model an explicit fourth proposal is correct, the
feature and approval proposals fail, and the due-actuality thesis fails too.
That fourth extension is knife-mass only: it differs from due actuality at knife
mass, and from both other accounts at counterweight mass. It is an actual
formal witness, not an unspecified possible millionth option or an alias.
Finding no further proposal in a source search would not prove coverage.

## Exact connection to the current root leaf

`STMT-GOODNESS-IS-ACTUALITY` means unrestricted `GoodIn x p ↔ Has x p`. Given the
new thesis, that holds **iff every actual perfection is due** (`AllActualitiesDue`).
The finite due-actuality model has actual knife mass outside the cutter/cutting
standard, so the new thesis holds and the old unrestricted coextension fails.
The all-actuality model gives the converse omission control: unrestricted
coextension holds while the new thesis fails.

`GOODNESS-DUE-ACTUALITY-WITH-DUE-BRIDGE-ENTAILS-UNRESTRICTED` registers this
exact two-premise connection to the existing root leaf. Its openly assumed
inputs and the underlying named-account premises become visible on the root
frontier; they are not omitted to keep bare counts unchanged.

Thus the new proposition is not silently substituted for the old route leaf.
The old `AQUINAS-ST-I-Q5-A1-GOODNESS-FROM-DESIRABILITY` proof remains intact:
it composes three open biconditionals. Neither that source-derived reading nor
its premises are replaced by a new analytic definition. A route using the
restricted thesis must discharge the due-status bridge or explicitly restrict
its perfection domain. That is the exact remaining route connection.

## Rival comparisons and surviving accounts

| Account or test | Checked outcome | What remains open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mere actuality as goodness | Surplus relative to the supplied cutter/cutting standard | Whether this is the intended sense of transcendental rather than due/functional goodness |
| Boyd’s recorded sufficient positive-case schema, after explicit aspect transfer | Can occupy coextensive, surplus and omission cells with the **same nonempty fostering/sustaining criteria**, actualities, forms and ends | The positive fragment does not fix the entire extension; adequacy of human goods/cluster and transfer to ontological aspects are not established |
| Railton’s welfare biconditional, after explicit fixed-agent aspect transfer | Has a due-actuality-compatible instance and an all-actuality/surplus instance | Which objective-interest criterion is correct, and whether welfare ascriptions transfer to this ontological question |
| Coarse feature reduction omitting form/end | No exact classifier for the nonconstant artifact goodness relation | The specified projection is insufficient; this does not refute richer naturalist criteria |
| Form-sensitive feature criterion | Exact classifier exists in the same finite profile | Classification alone establishes neither metaphysical property identity, empirical access nor categorical authority |
| Fixed-assessor approval reduction | Conditional elimination requires its genuine correctness negation; it is not independently defeated here | This is a subjectivist identity proposal, not a surrogate for expressivism about moral language |
| Property-identity reading / Moore boundary | Identity entails due coextension; distinct formal referents can coextend | Extensional agreement does not settle simplicity, non-naturalness, indefinability or real property identity |

The Boyd and Railton tests call their existing reading definitions with explicit
schema substitutions. They do not attribute a universal `GoodIn` definition to
Boyd or identify personal welfare, moral rightness and ontological goodness.
Naturalists who include form/end facts in the relevant basis are not defeated
by the coarse-feature control. The exact criterion characterization is reused
from `TruthCore.Results.Logic.CorrectionCriterion.characterization`, not reproved
as a new naturalist objection.

Moore’s open-question concern also cannot be reduced to extension difference.
The existing `UNRECOGNIZED-PROPERTY-IDENTITY-POSSIBLE` result already distinguishes
epistemic openness from property nonidentity. The new calibrated property
presentation addresses the opposite boundary: coextension does not itself
force identity of referents. It does not establish the real existence of distinct
coextensive properties. Intensional predicates for simplicity and definability,
and their applicability, remain the precise unencoded work for a full Moore
comparison.

## Finite witnesses and ultimate perfection

The named `artifact` profile retains exactly a knife and a counterweight, with
existence, edge and mass. Both exist and have mass; neither has an edge. Edge
is due to cutter/cutting, mass to weight/balancing, and existence to both.

| Fixed profile | GoodIn extension | Verified cell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `artifact` | due and actual | coextension |
| `surplusArtifact` | every actuality | surplus, no omission |
| `omissionArtifact` | counterweight due actualities only | omission, no surplus |
| `mixedArtifact` | knife mass only | surplus **and** omission |

Every advertised property is retained in `BoundaryCertificate`, rather than
being incidental to a hidden witness. These are stipulated artifact examples,
not observations or proofs of real metaphysical possibility. The choice of a
form/end standard is exactly where a rival may disagree.

The knife is good in its substantial existence but lacks its due ultimate edge
and is not good simpliciter. The existing
`UltimateDuePerfectionRequiredReading` is reused: lacking ultimate perfection
excludes good simpliciter. `GoodSimply` is independent; no biconditional or
moral authority is added. See the earlier
[natural-goodness/function investigation](natural-goodness-function-and-authority.md)
for the source distinctions and the function-to-practical-authority gap.

## Remaining consequential work

The next step is to constrain the admissible definition space through actual
arguments about due status, actual goodness, interests and approval. A concrete
counterexample may exclude a named extension; an independently warranted scope
argument may establish semantic coverage; a new relevant proposal belongs in
the explicit remainder and must be encoded. Property-intensional alternatives
remain separate even when their extensions match. No complete historical rival
is declared false by these finite controls.

An aggregate or graded account of perfection would need additional vocabulary;
the present per-aspect theorem does not encode it. Real teleological causation
would need a causal relation and an explicit applicability bridge. Practical
obligations require their own normative bridge. These are named encoding gaps,
not prerequisites to exploring the current conditional configurations.

No source, passage or attributed claim is added. The current owner proposal and
all new formulations are analyst propositions with attribution deferred. The
original root statement and generic Challenges stay unchanged; computed
`corpus.py frontier-diff` reports the actual root-premise effect.
