## Precisely the selector used

For an antecedent predicate A and an originating normal context:

- Select **all normal A witnesses**.
- Select the originating repair only if **no normal A witness exists** and the
  repair itself satisfies A.

`SelectedConditional` requires its consequent at every selected state. Success
(selected states satisfy A) is checked. There is at most one selected repair;
there may be none. This profile supplies no closest-world ordering, centering,
seriality or general Lewis/Stalnaker semantics. `NormalConditional` instead
quantifies over normal A witnesses only. Neither definition is material
implication evaluated merely at the originating state.

The general conservativity theorem quantifies over arbitrary A and Q: if A
has a normal witness, selected evaluation is equivalent to normal-only
evaluation, since no repair is selected. Its second clause allows different
antecedent/consequent predicates whose truth values agree at **every normal
state**, and proves identical selected conditional evaluations whenever the
antecedent has a normal witness. Thus off-normal differences cannot affect
this whole ordinary possible-antecedent predicate fragment. No completeness
claim about nested counterfactual languages is made.

For public a off, neither interpretation has a normal witness. Normal-only
evaluation consequently validates both effect-on and effect-off vacuously.
The repair selector instead chooses **exactly** the originating repair in both
modes, independent of the consequent. At origin true, active validates
effect-off and rejects effect-on; idle validates effect-on and rejects
effect-off. Normal and ordinary conditional data therefore do not choose
between these explicit extensions.

conditions for this antecedent. A nonvacuist owes an argument for this particular
selection and equation-retention prescription; an LLM or the mere existence of
the table provides none. Both are preserved as serious semantic alternatives.
The formal comparison alone does not determine actual counterpossible truth.

The antecedent is impossible **relative to the declared normal model**, but is
classically consistent in a repair. No contradictory truth assignment or K3/LP
algebra is introduced. This is not a full nonvacuous treatment of every logically
impossible antecedent: if no normal or repair state satisfies an antecedent,
this successful universal selector remains empty and its conditional vacuous.

Repairs are never admitted to the original modal frame. The separate enlarged
frame control shows that adding them as accessible possibilities destroys
public-atom necessity. That is a change in the modal profile, not merely
counterpossible evaluation while retaining the old profile. Replacing the
public contributor by a genuinely necessary ground or bearer would require a
further semantics/application bridge; this result does not supply one.
