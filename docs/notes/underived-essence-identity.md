## Materialized source and source boundary

including the objections, all three proofs, and replies. The new connection
consumes the existing first-proof readings, rather than adding source claims.

exhaustiveness premise; it is not refuted by renaming it an efficient cause.

That is a different source argument, not an identity between the existing
modal, power-transmission and passive-potency signatures. The reply to the
second objection also distinguishes the act of being from the truth of the
proposition that something exists. The one-way actuality interface therefore
stays explicit and open.
