## General bridge and the exact remaining burden

`SupervenesAt M physical phenomenal a` means that every M-admitted physical
duplicate of `a` has the same phenomenal report. This is an **actual-relative**
supervenience condition, not unrestricted global supervenience, reductive
identity or a causal law. For arbitrary carriers, report functions and frames,
the certificate proves:

1. Actual-relative supervenience holds.
2. The subject knows equality with the actual complete *represented* physical
   value.
3. Every epistemic alternative at actual is M-admitted (`IncludedAt E M a`).
4. Therefore the subject knows equality with the actual phenomenal report.

The proof uses existing box transfer for the conditional connecting the two
report equalities, then applies it to known physical equality. Neither premise
is simply the advertised conclusion. Applied to the concrete learning episode,
this law derives failure of inclusion in the supervenient survivor. The twin
rival shows that keeping inclusion is possible with the identical episode when
supervenience is dropped. A contradictory conjunction rejects at least one input;
it does not choose which input is false in reality.

The strongest physicalist reply here accepts epistemic novelty while denying
that its alternatives must respect every necessary psychophysical identity:
complete physical description need not reveal identity across phenomenal and
physical modes of presentation. The strongest nonphysicalist reply treats
suitably ideal physical-information alternatives as genuinely metaphysically
admissible. Its ideal-reflection/identity-transparency requirement is exactly an
additional bridge to investigate, not a consequence of the word "knowledge".
These are rival analyst configurations, not attributed author claims.

The profile models extensional knowledge of the same report predicate before
and after. It does not formalize concept possession, acquaintance, abilities,
indexicals, hyperintensional modes of presentation or an actual phenomenal
concept strategy. Encoding those further disputes needs corresponding typed
