## Mechanism, quantities and recursive meaning

Select a flywheel driving a resistive load during one interval. The subsystem
contains the wheel and its transmission; the load and thermal environment are
outside. Before/after identify the SAME kinetic-energy stock of that wheel.
Input is energy crossing this boundary; work is energy delivered at the selected
load port; heat is remaining exported dissipation. All five are energies in one
unit. Work and heat are interval integrals, not instantaneous power or capacity.
Internal circulation of existing energy is not a fresh boundary input. Multiple
uses of the same resource must not each count its entire stock as new energy.

The independent mechanical interpretation would connect wheel energy to moment
of inertia and angular speed, and load work to torque integrated over angle.
Those dynamics, resource identification and boundary completeness are **OPEN**:
the Lean object is an aggregate energy ledger, not a derived rotor trajectory.
No empirical numbers or universal causal law are claimed. `Admissible` has a
fully explicit body: nonnegative fields and after+work+heat=before+input.
This is a stipulated balance premise, NOT a definition of production, borrowing,
sourcehood or existence. `Budget` and `TemporalBudget` expose consequences of
that premise. No opaque relation is renamed into a definition of causal power.
No new metaphysical sense or bridge is asserted by these arithmetic records.

Energy delivered to the load is production of a selected effect in this limited
operational reading; it is not creation of energy. Mere maintenance/transport
can preserve stock with zero selected load work. Heat-only dissipation changes
stock without doing the selected work. A production-of-being or production-of-
energy reading would be a different thesis and cannot inherit this certificate.

## Checked tests and proposed-draft corrections

`ProductiveStore.budget`: output plus heat cannot exceed stock plus input.
If stock does not decrease and output plus heat is positive, input is positive.
The nondepletion premise does the discriminating work; no contraction, graph
acyclicity, inductive supply or source requirement is built into the mechanism.
The control tuples in `Controls` show joint satisfiability and isolate closure,
nondepletion and the difference between selected work and dissipation.

`temporalBudget` telescopes the same-stock recurrence. For every finite horizon,
output is bounded by initial stock plus accumulated input. Its exact type only
requires stock nonnegativity; input/output positivity is not needed for the
arithmetic implication. Physical interpretation adds those restrictions.

`decayingDischarge` supplies a stronger omission test: stock at n is
2*(1/2)^n, external input zero, work and heat each (1/2)^n/2. Adjacent stocks
match, and both outputs are positive at EVERY natural interval. Thus a finite
budget does not exclude infinitely many positive outputs. There is no common
positive lower bound. This is a ledger model, not evidence of physically
realizable eternal rotor motion or simultaneous infinitely many contributors.
A finite initial stock can support transient work without ongoing energy input;
its past charging history is outside the interval and remains unspecified.

## Rival walk and bearer bridge

Strongest remaining positive account: globally selected local activity, with
conservative circulation maintaining a state, or decreasing discharge supplying
work from an existing finite stock. These have different explananda. Circulation
alone does not explain positive dissipative load work in a NONDEPLETING CLOSED
ledger; it may still model maintenance. Discharge pays with depletion and output
tending down, not contradiction with an accepted floor. A steady dissipative
account pays with boundary input. Moving the boundary to include the supplier
relocates the budget; it never by itself proves a terminal supplier. Infinite
resource profiles or nonstationary stored energy remain unexcluded unless
bounded-total-stock, matching resource identities and relevant temporal scope
are independently justified. No exhaustive classification is asserted.

Sourcehood of an energy contribution is relative to this resource boundary.
The bearer map assigns the store contribution to the wheel and delivered work
to the load event's bearer. This map is a proposed physical interpretation, NOT
a Lean-certified abstraction into existential dependence. A wheel can supply
stored energy while requiring bearings, a persisting structure and an existential
supporter. Availability of a bearing is not another delivery of the wheel's
kinetic energy. Lean `PowerExistenceBoundary.Profile` separates transmitsPower,
supportsEsse, intrinsicPower and existence, all primitive relations. Its
`UnborrowedPowerAt` includes existence and intrinsicPower and excludes incoming
transmission only; `ExternallySupportedAt` requires a distinct existing supporter.
The existing `powerExistenceBoundary : Boundary` constructs unborrowed power
with external support and contingency. It is a relational omission model, not
a physical wheel model. Its finite-power grounding uses the explicitly inductive
`FinitelyGroundedPower`, whose real applicability remains open.

The existing theorem `originalPowerBlocksEsseSupportIfItTransmits` identifies
an exact sufficient bridge: every esse-support edge transmits this same power.
The wheel/bearing distinction gives no reason to accept that bridge for the
selected kinetic-energy resource. Conversely it does not empirically disprove
metaphysical conservation of esse. Neither input=0 nor input>0 answers whether
the bearer's existence is underived or necessary. `Ontic.ExistsAt` and Causes
are primitive; necessary existence quantifies over the supplied accessible
worlds, not over energy budgets. Quantitative numbers have no world index here.

Existing #734 `Supply` is inductively generated from intrinsic contributions
and entire contributor groups; `LocalPower` is an unselected fixed-point law.
Its `Escape` is a separate open cut principle; nothing in this ledger derives
it. #741's global explanatory-adequacy question remains separate. The existing
source-free all-on and limit controls remain in force; energy accounting does
not turn local consistency into adequate explanation or defeat it globally.

## Uniform-demand continuation (draft for independent challenge)

The new ProductiveSustained batch preserves the original frozen Challenge.
`uniformBudget` specializes the existing temporal bound to zero input and sums
an explicit common lower interval-energy bound: n*epsilon <= stock(0).
`noUniformDischarge` uses real Archimedean order to contradict that bound for
some finite n when epsilon > 0. It does not take an infinite sum, assert a
physical infinity, or construct a trajectory from natural indexing.
`decayingNoUniform` proves that the existing discharge's total output (1/2)^n
has no common positive lower bound. `replenishedUniform` supplies the constant
stock/unit-input/unit-work control, isolating the zero-input hypothesis.

The three readings are therefore: positive exercise in every interval (the
existing decreasing witness); uniformly sustained output (common epsilon > 0);
and nondepleting steady exercise (the original Budget test plus replenished
control). These are explicitly different conditions, not exhaustive senses of
metaphysical power. Uniformly positive output entails positive output pointwise;
it does not entail nondepletion when input is available. Nondepletion alone
permits zero work. No stronger conclusion is hidden in the word persistent.

For equal-duration intervals of duration delta > 0, a justified common average
power lower bound p > 0 would supply epsilon=p*delta. Neither pointwise positive
instantaneous power nor interval positivity supplies p. Even constant positive
power on shrinking intervals need not supply epsilon; time intervals must be
specified before translating a power claim into this energy bound. Equal time
is an application premise, not needed by the discrete arithmetic theorem.

A proposed minimum-work requirement is substantive, not a universal physical
threshold. The concrete required-load profile L(n)=(1/2)^n is met exactly by the
existing zero-input discharge with stock(n)=2*(1/2)^n. It has positive required
load at every interval and finite cumulative demand. Thus replacing a common
minimum by an arbitrary strictly positive load profile restores the reservoir
rival. Conversely any load profile with a finite prefix exceeding initial stock
is excluded by TemporalBudget. This prefix test is more general than uniformity;
no necessity or exhaustiveness theorem about arbitrary loads is claimed here.

Revisiting the parent rough draft: its one-instant contraction and finite-path
results remain checked under #1379's hypotheses; finite conservation implying
strict loss is rejected by its conservative control. This continuation corrects
any all-interval-positivity inference to continuing input. A demand threshold,
time identification and exclusion of stored-resource exercise are still OPEN
application premises. ACT-POWER is contemporaneous caused exercise, not a stated
uniform-in-time load requirement. The source issue supplies no premise that
forces every relevant activity to maintain a common positive energy rate.
A reservoir's commitments are finite depletion and diminishing load, not a
contradiction or an unreasonable price. Its persistent structural bearer may
still have existential support: Profile's transmitsPower, supportsEsse and
