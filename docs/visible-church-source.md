# Stage 5: the visible Church source reading

`STMT-CHRIST-FOUNDED-VISIBLE-CHURCH` says that Jesus founded a visible,
enduring society with authority. *Lumen gentium* §8, first two paragraphs,
directly supplies that commitment: Christ establishes and continually
sustains the visible community, which has hierarchical organs, is entrusted to Peter and the apostles for
pastoral government, and is established in perpetuity. Section 5 identifies the founder as Lord Jesus.
The Latin chapter-I witness is pinned and materialized as
`VATICAN-II-LUMEN-GENTIUM-I`; it reuses the existing document snapshot.

The source also describes the spiritual community and mystical body as the
same complex Church. The linked definitions and disambiguation preserve
that context. The existential stage-5 reading neither reduces the Church to
an external organization nor requires every later office to have been
personally specified at a single initial event. It does not convert the
later *subsistit* clause into the separate stage-6 historical-identity claim.

This is an attributed Catholic conciliar assertion. It supplies primary
source evidence for an existing root premise; it does not independently
establish the historical occurrence, the Church's divine authority, or the
truth of its teachings. The premise remains open. No inference, Lean result,
or change to either root route is advertised by this source connection.
